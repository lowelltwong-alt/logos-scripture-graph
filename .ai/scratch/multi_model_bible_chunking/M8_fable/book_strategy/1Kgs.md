# 1Kgs — Book Strategy (M8_fable, candidate-only, non-authorizing)

## selected_strategy

1Kgs is chunked on tier-1 text signals alone, with the WLC parashah apparatus (51 petuchah + 57 setumah, single-witness, MT numbering — pmarks_1Kgs.json) used strictly as weak corroboration per the m8-mesh-r2 addendum: in the Former Prophets both pe and samekh sit in the weak-corroboration tier and never drive or lift a boundary. The book divides into blocks that demand different chunking discipline: (1) chs 1–2, the succession narrative's conclusion — long choreographed court scenes (Nathan–Bathsheba intervention, the anointing race, David's charge, the four-execution settlement) where dialogue turns must not become seams; (2) chs 3–11, the Solomon cycle, an editorial mosaic of dream theophanies, judgment narrative, administrative lists, treaty correspondence, construction records, the dedication liturgy, annalistic wealth notices, and the adversary/apostasy complex — here the risks run in both directions: lists and building specifications invite shredding at every entry, while paratactic annal chains (9:10–28; 10:14–29) invite lazy agglomeration; (3) chs 12–16, the schism and the alternating regnal framework, where the recurring opening formula ("In the Nth year of X … became king … and did evil/right in Yahweh's eyes") and the source-citation closing formula ("Now the rest of the acts of X … book of the chronicles of the kings …") are the strongest recurring tier-1 boundary devices in the book; (4) chs 17–19, the Elijah cycle, scene-chained prophetic narrative bounded by word-event formulas ("The word of Yahweh came to him, saying"); (5) chs 20–22, the Ahab war/judgment complex (Aram wars, Naboth, Micaiah) closing with two regnal frames (Jehoshaphat; Ahaziah, whose reign runs over the book seam into 2Kgs 1 — flagged, not merged). Boundary drivers throughout: regnal opening and closing formulas, word-event formulas, date/era formulas (6:1; 6:37–38; 7:1), theophany frames (3:4–15; 9:1–9 "as he had appeared at Gibeon"), arrival/embassy formulas (5:1 Hiram embassy; 10:1 Sheba; 12:1 Shechem; 20:1 siege), list/register formulas, fulfillment notices ("according to the word of Yahweh spoken by …"), and explicit scene/participant/location change.

## literature_type_or_mixed_genre

Mixed under a dominant narrative-prose register, with an unusually high proportion of non-scene material for the Former Prophets. Distinct embedded genres, each tagged span-specifically: court succession narrative (1–2), dream theophany (3:4–15; 9:1–9), juridical anecdote (3:16–28), administrative rosters (4:1–6 officials; 4:7–19 district governors), provisioning/prosperity annals (WEB 4:20–28 = MT 4:20–5:8), international treaty correspondence with embedded speeches (WEB 5 = MT 5:15–32), temple and palace construction records (6:1–7:12) with a craft catalogue of bronze and gold works (7:13–51), procession-and-dedication liturgy containing Solomon's long petition prayer with its "hear in heaven" refrain chain (8), annalistic miscellany (9:10–28; 10:14–29), state-visit narrative (10:1–13), apostasy-and-adversaries complex with the Ahijah cloak sign-act (11), assembly/schism narrative (12), prophetic conflict legend (13, the man of God and the old prophet), prophetic judgment oracles against dynastic houses (14:1–18; 16:1–4; 21:17–26), the regnal-formula framework (14:19–16:34; 22:39–53), prophetic cycle narrative (17–19), prophetic war narrative with a parable-verdict scene (20), juridical crime narrative with embedded letters (21), and the heavenly-council vision scene (22:5–28). No poetry-flagged chapters exist in this book's substrate (all 27 risk signals are has_footnote); the nearest approach to elevated register is the dedication prayer and the oracle speeches, which remain rhetorical prose.

## substrate_markers_considered

chapter_profile.json (22 chapter rollups) and span_features.jsonl reviewed. No q1/q2 poetry markers anywhere in the book — consistent with the genre map (narrative, annal, liturgy-prose). Paragraph (¶) density peaks in the dialogue-heavy prophetic chapters (18: 28¶, 20: 33¶, 22: 32¶) and bottoms out in the annal/list chapters (4: 3¶, 5: 4¶) — modern paragraphing, metadata only, noted merely as a coarse mirror of scene density. risk_signals.jsonl carries 27 signals, all has_footnote (translation glosses — hinneh, name spellings, measures); WEB-editorial metadata, never boundary evidence. The parashah distribution is diagnostic of exactly the two hazards the addendum's weak tier guards against: MT ch 4 carries a samekh after nearly every roster entry (4:1, 4:2, 4:4, 4:6, 4:7–13, 4:16–18 — following them would shred the officials and governors registers into name-fragments), and ch 8's samekh set (8:32, 8:34, 8:36) tracks petition ends inside the one continuous dedication prayer. In both cases the list-register rule and the refrain-chain rule keep the units whole, with the marks recorded as weak corroboration of internal articulation only.

## source_metadata_evidence_only_check

web_mt_crosswalk.json (status: verified_chapter_level, non_authorizing): WEB 816 vv, MT 817 vv. Chapters 4, 5, 22 are non-identical and require dual WEB/MT citation; sub-verse claims inside them default to insufficient_evidence unless the mapping is separately established in-row. Orchestrator seam verification against both witness texts (2026-08-03) establishes the standard mapping: WEB 1:1–4:20 = MT identical; WEB 4:21–34 = MT 5:1–14 (WEB 4:21 "Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms" = MT 5:1 ושלמה היה מושל); WEB 5:1–18 = MT 5:15–32 (WEB 5:1 Hiram embassy = MT 5:15 וישלח חירם); chs 6–21 identical; WEB 22:1–42 = MT identical; WEB 22:43 = MT 22:43+22:44 (MT splits the Asa-ways/high-places verse); WEB 22:44–53 = MT 22:45–54. Consequence: every oshb: citation inside WEB 4:21–5:18 and WEB 22:43–53 must dual-cite (e.g. "web:1Kgs.4.21 = oshb:1Kgs.5.1 (MT)"), and every parashah citation anywhere carries "(single-witness)" since only WLC witnesses the marks in this substrate. Canonical tiling target uses WEB numbering (816 verses).

## larger_unit_preservation_check (planned tests)

Known temptation points where a finer split will be considered and, absent independent tier-1 markers on both edges, rejected with documentation: the Nathan–Bathsheba double audience 1:11–31 (one orchestrated intervention with staged entrances, not two petitions); the anointing sequence 1:32–40 vs the feast collapse 1:41–53 (genuine scene change on the noise-report hinge at 1:41); David's charge 2:1–9 + death notice 2:10–12 (the regnal summary closes the charge unit); the four-execution settlement 2:13–46 (four discrete audiences — Adonijah, Abiathar, Joab, Shimei — each opened by its own participant formula: these ARE units, but 2:46b "the kingdom was established" closes the whole complex and must not be orphaned); the judgment anecdote 3:16–28 (one juridical scene; the verdict reversal is a turn, not a seam); the Carmel contest 18:20–40 (challenge, preparation, taunt, fire, execution — one dramatic arc with internal turn-markers); the Micaiah scene 22:5–28 (court prophets, summons, vision report, Zedekiah clash, imprisonment — one audience scene); the old-prophet legend 13:11–32 (journey, deception, oracle at table, lion — one continuous ironic arc bounded by 13:33–34's evaluation).

## list_register_function_check

Register structures identified in advance: 4:1–6 (court officials, "these were the princes"), 4:7–19 (twelve district governors — samekh after nearly every entry; one register), WEB 4:22–28 (provision quotas and stable counts — annal register), 7:13–47 (the bronze-works craft catalogue: pillars 7:15–22, sea 7:23–26, stands 7:27–37, basins-and-summary 7:38–47 — internal seams argued from the ויעש object-introduction formula changes, not from enumeration; the closing inventory 7:40b–47 is the catalogue's own colophon), 7:48–51 (gold furnishings + deposit notice, a distinct register closed by the completion formula), 9:15–23 (corvée building list with its own "this is the account" opening), 10:14–29 (wealth annals: gold income, shields, throne, fleet, horses — paratactic notices treated as one annal register unless a genuine formula shift is shown), plus the adversary notices 11:14–22 and 11:23–25 (parallel "Yahweh raised up an adversary" frames — two units by the repeated raising formula). Each list is one decision unless its own formula visibly changes register mid-stream.

## over_split_risk_check

Highest-risk material: (1) dialogue-dense court scenes where named-speaker formulas repeat (1:11–31; 2:13–25; 3:16–28; 18:7–15; 20:31–34; 22:5–28 — turn-marker principle applies); (2) the construction records (6:2–36 room-by-room specification is one continuous record punctuated only by the 6:11–13 word-event interrupt, which IS a marked unit; 7:13–47 splits only at object-formula changes); (3) the dedication liturgy (8:22–53 prayer is bound by the "hear in heaven" refrain chain — petition edges are articulation, not seams; the liturgy's genuine seams are the procession/glory close at 8:10–11, the blessing frames at 8:12–21 and 8:54–61, and the sacrifice-festival close 8:62–66); (4) the regnal framework — each reign is one unit from opening to closing formula unless a marked prophetic narrative interrupts (15:29–30 and 16:1–4 stay inside their reigns as fulfillment/oracle members; contrast ch 17–19, where the Elijah material has outgrown the frame entirely). Conversely under-split risk: 9:10–28 miscellany must not ride as one lump (Cabul etiology 9:10–14, corvée account 9:15–23, remaining notices 9:24–28 have their own openings); 11:1–43 has four genuine movements (apostasy-and-oracle 11:1–13, two adversary frames, Jeroboam-Ahijah 11:26–40, regnal close); 12 has three (assembly/schism 12:1–19 with 12:20 coda, Shemaiah oracle 12:21–24, calf cult 12:25–33); WEB ch 4–5 annal material has real seams at 4:20/4:21 (prosperity vignette vs dominion summary) and 4:29 (wisdom summary).
