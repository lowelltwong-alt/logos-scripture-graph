# Joshua — Book Strategy (M8_fable, candidate-only, non-authorizing)

## selected_strategy

I read the whole book twice before drafting: once in WEB (`Josh_web_clean.txt`, all 658 verses) to get narrative shape, and once in the Hebrew (`Josh_oshb.txt`, verse-per-line MT) to identify the actual discourse signals — messenger formulas (וַיֹּאמֶר X אֶל Y לֵאמֹר), lot formulas (וַיֵּצֵא / וַיַּעַל הַגּוֹרָל), colophons (נַחֲלַת..., עָרִים...וְחַצְרֵיהֶן), etiological "to this day" tags, resumptive repetition, and rhetorical-particle pivots inside long speeches (רַק, וְאִם, הִנֵּה). The web/MT crosswalk is verified identical (658=658, no offset zones), so every citation uses matching WEB/OSHB verse numbers.

The book has two very different halves that demand different default units. Chapters 1–12 are narrative with a dense, repeated cast of messenger-formula scenes — Yahweh to Joshua, Joshua to officers/priests/people, and the recurring "when [king] heard" coalition type-scene (5:1; 9:1; 10:1; 11:1). Chapters 13–21 are administrative — lot formulas, boundary perambulations, city catalogues, numeric colophons — where the operative signal is almost never a messenger formula but a register/closure pattern. Chapters 22–24 return to narrative/oratorical mode (altar dispute, farewell speech, covenant renewal) and close the book with a small colophon-complex of three parallel death-and-burial notices.

Throughout, chapter numbers, verse numbers, and WEB paragraphing were treated as metadata only. Several units cross chapter lines where the text itself runs on without a break (none turned out to be strictly necessary in Joshua the way they are in Deuteronomy, but I checked every chapter seam for a suppressed continuation and found none — each chapter boundary in Joshua happens to coincide with a genuine formula shift). Parashah marks (52 pe / 42 samekh, two-witness agreement OSHB=UXLC) were cited only where they land exactly at a boundary I had already chosen on textual grounds, as weak corroboration; they never drove a decision and their absence at a boundary was never treated as counter-evidence, per the Prophets-book addendum.

## literature_type_or_mixed_genre

Mixed-genre book; see the form matrix below. Hard-test outcomes:

**1. Chs. 3–4 crossing + memorial stones.** Segmented at every genuine messenger-formula/addressee shift within ch.3 (officers to people, Joshua to priests, Yahweh to Joshua, Joshua to Israel), and at command-vs-fulfillment shifts within ch.4. The key interpretive call is 4:1: it opens with a resumptive repetition of 3:17's closing clause (Wiederaufnahme), which I read as bridging continuity of the crossing *event* while the messenger formula immediately following it opens a genuinely new *command* (the memorial stones) — so it closes 3:14-17 rather than extending it. The two stone-explanation speeches (4:6-7 to the twelve chosen men; 4:21-24 to the whole assembly at Gilgal) are kept as two distinct speech occasions rather than merged as a redundant doublet, since they have different addressees and different rhetorical settings.

**2. Ch. 12 king register, closure test.** Applied directly and asymmetrically to the chapter's own two halves. 12:1-8 is narrative prose with individualized boundary description (kept split into two units by subject: Moses' Transjordan kings, then the superscription for Joshua's western kings). 12:9-24's thirty-one entries are a bare, content-uniform "king of X, one" formula with a single closing total and zero per-entry differentiation — the textbook uniform-content register — so it stays as **one** decision rather than thirty-one, explicitly *unlike* the ch.10 southern-campaign city run, where each city notice carries individualized narrative detail (troop movement, day-count, an extra Gezer-relief episode at Lachish) and its own comparative closing formula, which I split into six separate decisions on the same test.

**3. Chs. 13–21 allotments.** Lot formulas open each tribal unit; נַחֲלַת-colophons close them. Within a tribe, city-list sub-clusters are split wherever the text supplies its own independent numeric tally (Judah's eleven clusters in ch.15; Benjamin's two in ch.18; the four Levitical clan-lists in ch.21), but kept whole where only a single tally closes the whole list (Zebulun, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, Dan — each one decision). The three "could not drive out" notices (15:63; 16:10; 17:12-13) are treated consistently as their own literary type standing alone, never absorbed into the list or border material they follow, since each opens with its own negative-verb formula (לֹא יָכְלוּ / וְלֹא הוֹרִישׁוּ) rather than continuing the catalogue genre.

**4. Josh 21:36-37 variant.** Disclosed in the Merarite-cities decision (21:34-40): present in both OSHB and UXLC as consulted for this campaign, but well known to be absent from a number of medieval Masoretic manuscripts and printed Rabbinic Bibles — a variance that also affects whether the stated Merarite total of twelve cities is reached with or without them. Disclosed, not resolved.

**5. Book-of-Jashar poem, 10:12-14.** Kept as one unit: the narrator's introduction, the poetic citation with its embedded source-formula ("is this not written in the book of Jashar"), and the narrator's closing theological comment are treated as a single citation event, since the closing comment interprets the poem and cannot be severed from it. Confidence capped at medium because the exact prose/poetry boundary is genuinely disputed and I did not want to overstate certainty about where quotation ends.

**6. Ebal ceremony, 8:30-35.** This is the weakest near-edge in the whole draft, and I have not smoothed it over. The unit opens on the adverb אָז ("then"), the same loose connective used elsewhere in the book for scene jumps (10:12; 22:1), with no participant carried over from the Ai narrative except Joshua, and no stated causal link. I capped confidence at medium_low and flagged it explicitly rather than either forcing it into the Ai unit or silently adopting the critical relocation hypothesis (which the owner rules do not permit me to use as a boundary basis). The far edge is clean: 9:1 reopens the recurring "when the kings heard" type-scene.

**7. Chs. 22–24 speech frames and closing colophon complex.** Ch.22 is segmented by speaker/addressee reversal (accusation → defense → resolution); the resumptive repetition at 22:6-8 (Manasseh's two halves bracketed by "blessed them and sent them away... he blessed them") is read as continuity-marking, not a new unit, applying the same Wiederaufnahme principle used in chs.3-4. Ch.23 is segmented at genuine rhetorical pivots (positive exhortation / conditional warning / personal testament) rather than at every WEB paragraph mark — I deliberately did *not* split at 23:6's רַק the way I split ch.1 at similar particles, because 23:6 continues the same charge rather than opening new content. Ch.24's historical prologue (24:2-13) stays as one unit despite several topical movements, since the single quoted divine voice is never interrupted by a new messenger formula. The book's closing colophon complex (24:29-33) is split into three parallel, independent death-and-burial notices (Joshua, Joseph's bones, Eleazar), each with its own subject and closing formula.

## Register/colophon treatments chosen

- **נַחֲלַת-colophon**: treated as the standard closing bracket for every tribal allotment unit, Transjordan and Cisjordan alike.
- **City-total colophon** (עָרִים N וְחַצְרֵיהֶן): treated as the closing bracket for a city sub-cluster; a fresh numeral resets to a new unit even without a restated terrain heading (Judah's second and third lowland clusters, 15:37-41 and 15:42-44).
- **"Could not drive out" notices**: one consistent treatment book-wide — always their own unit, never merged into the preceding list or border material, on the strength of their independent negative-verb opening formula.
- **City-conquest narrative vs. bare king-tally**: the operative distinction throughout is content differentiation. Individualized narrative (ch.10's southern cities) splits; bare uniform formula (ch.12's thirty-one kings) stays whole.
- **Wiederaufnahme / resumptive repetition** (3:17→4:1; 22:6-8): read consistently as marking continuity of one scene around an inserted aside, never as grounds for a new unit by itself.

## Confidence calibration notes

High confidence (122/150) is reserved for boundaries carrying an unambiguous formula on at least one edge and either a matching formula or a clean genre/mode shift (speech→narration, command→fulfillment, list→prose) on the other. Medium confidence (25/150) marks boundaries where the split is defensible and consistent with a book-wide pattern I applied everywhere, but where a genuinely competitive alternative reading exists (most of the register/closure splits in the allotment lists, and the Jashar-poem citation frame). Medium_low (3/150) marks the three places where I judged the evidence itself to be thin: the single-verse sanctification call (3:5), the circumstantial Jericho-shut-up notice (6:1), and the Ebal ceremony's near edge (8:30). I did not use "low" anywhere in this draft — everywhere I considered assigning it, I concluded the boundary was either defensible enough for medium/medium_low or not worth proposing as a boundary at all, and revised the unit accordingly rather than keep a genuinely unsupported line and rate it low.

## Risks for reviewers

- **8:30-35** is the single most contestable boundary in the book. A reviewer favoring the displaced-material hypothesis (common in source-critical treatments) may want this unit relocated or its confidence lowered further; I have deliberately not adopted that hypothesis since it is not grounded in the received text's own signals.
- **Josh 21:36-37** carries real manuscript variance bearing on the Merarite total; any downstream harmonization work should not silently normalize this away.
- **15:58-60** (two small hill-country city clusters folded into one decision rather than split like every other tally-bounded group in the chapter) is an inconsistency I flagged rather than hid — a stricter reviewer might prefer splitting it for mechanical consistency with the rest of ch.15's register treatment.
- **12:9-24 vs. ch.10's city run** is the load-bearing comparison for the whole register/closure methodology in this book; if a reviewer disagrees with treating ch.12 as uniform-content and ch.10 as differentiated, most of the allotment-chapter splitting logic (which leans on the same test) should be re-examined together, not patched piecemeal.
- **Ch.24's historical prologue (24:2-13)** is a single twelve-verse unit with no internal split; a reviewer working from a source-critical framework that detects seams at its topical joints (patriarchs / Exodus / Transjordan / crossing) will find this the most "under-split" narrative unit in the book. I chose not to split it because none of those joints carries a new messenger formula, but I flag it as a real candidate for revision if the evidence bar is set differently downstream.
- **Ch. 21's four Levitical clan-lists** rely on the same register/closure logic as ch.15's Judah lists; I did not attempt to split any individual clan-list's own name-catalogue into sub-clusters, since (unlike Judah) none of the four clan-lists contains an internal numeral reset — but this should be double-checked against the raw list if finer granularity is later wanted.

## literary_form_decision_matrix

Narrative chs 1-12: messenger formulas, coalition type-scenes (וַיְהִי כִשְׁמֹעַ family), etiological
closures, Wiederaufnahme continuity. Administrative chs 13-21: lot formulas open, nahalat colophons
close, city registers split only at independent numeric tallies, boundary itineraries ride
perambulation verb chains. Oratory chs 22-24: speaker/addressee reversals, intra-speech pivots
(fronted pronouns), covenant-dialogue exchange turns, death/burial notice family. Poetry: the Jashar
citation frame (10:12-14) kept whole with its indeterminate poem-edge internal.

## source_metadata_evidence_only_check

Chapter and verse numbers, WEB paragraphing and pilcrows, and section headings were metadata-only
throughout; two WEB-paragraph-leaning challenge lines were stripped to their text-signal cores at the
peer layer, and one -style claim never arose (prose book). No metadata drove any boundary.

## strongs_metadata_considered_evidence_only

No Strong's numbers, lemma tags, or morphology-layer data were used as boundary evidence anywhere in
the book (strong_or_hebrew_tags_used: false on every row); Hebrew citations are verbatim text slices.

## substrate_markers_considered

Parashah record two-witness exact (OSHB=UXLC: 52 pe / 42 samekh). As a Prophets book these are WEAK
corroboration under addendum rule 3: cited only where they land on an independently chosen boundary,
never drivers, never counter-evidence by absence, never confidence-lifters (two attempted lifts were
re-grounded on real signals in the revision round).

## larger_unit_preservation_check

Units cross or coincide with chapter lines only as the text dictates; every chapter seam was tested
for suppressed continuation. Larger complexes are preserved through decision_relations rather than
merged rows: the crossing complex (chs 3-4), the allotment document bracketed by the 14:1/19:51
macro-inclusio, and the 11:16-23 summary inclusio.

## list_register_function_check

Register criterion applied with the closure test both directions: ch 10's six conquest notices split
(entry-specific content, migrating comparison target); the 31-king register of 12:9-24 stays whole
(accent-bound verse pairs, no per-entry closure, single aggregate total); city sub-clusters split
only at their own numeric tallies; the clan-totals insert of 21:4-7 folded inside the 21:1-8
Wiederaufnahme bracket.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable: Joshua contains no epistolary material.

## over_split_risk_check

## post_adjudication_outcome

and 10 created by split: 151-155, 160-164), exact 658/658 coverage, zero chapter-shaped rows.

zero appeals, zero ultra requests, zero human holds. The draft's initial 122-high calibration was
corrected pre-freeze to the both-edges standard (25 high), and the mesh still challenged four of
the five highs in chs. 3-7 — calibration remained the book's recurring battleground.

objects over 55 decisions, with nine blind two-side convergences; 4 peers supported 74 and

two-sided blind revision pair (opus + sonnet over the 52 changed rows, satisfying the two-primary
floor for all ten new siblings in one pass) added 12 more upheld challenges plus a peer-caught
phrase-match error (155), all fixed in micro rounds r2/r3. Total ruled: 89.
