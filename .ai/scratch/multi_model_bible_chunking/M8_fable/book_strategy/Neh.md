## 1. Genre and shape
Memoir-framed rebuilding narrative with embedded registers and liturgical
documents. Unlike Ezra (third-person frame with a memoir switch), Nehemiah
sustains FIRST-PERSON SINGULAR from 1:1 to 13:31 except the register/document
insets and the Ezra-material assembly block (ch 8–9, where Nehemiah recedes).
The person-shift inventory is Neh's own (lesson j): the memoir voice is the
HOST; departures from it (ch 3 register, 7:6-72 golah list, ch 8-9 assembly
narrative, ch 10 covenant document, ch 11-12 lists) are the marked events.

## 6. Inset rule
Prayers, documents, and registers default to LARGER UNITS with their narrative
frame; the frame travels with its inset (Ezra rule, unchanged): 1:5-11 prayer
with 1:4 hinge argued locally; 2:4b flash prayer never its own row; 3:36-37
(WEB 4:4-5) taunt-prayer stays inside the opposition episode; 6:9b and 6:14
prayer-asides stay in their episodes. THE GREAT PRAYER 9:5b-37 is the special
case: 32+ verses of liturgical recital with byte-verifiable epoch shifts
(creation 9:6, Abraham 9:7-8, Egypt/sea 9:9-11, wilderness guidance 9:12,
Sinai 9:13-14, provision 9:15, rebellion 9:16-18, sustaining mercy 9:19-21,
conquest 9:22-25, judges-cycle 9:26-28, prophets ignored 9:29-31, plea
9:32-37). POLICY: writers may subdivide ONLY at these recital moves (wayyiqtol
+ epoch-participant shifts, tier-1), every sub-row carries a parent grouping
relation to the whole prayer (granularity-divergence lesson 11), and the
prayer NEVER merges with 9:1-5a (assembly frame) or with the covenant hinge.
