## 1. Register-dominant device matrix (verified specimens)

Ezra is return-and-rebuilding narrative prose hosting an unusually high density
of EMBEDDED DOCUMENTS and REGISTERS, with two Aramaic blocks (4:8–6:18,
7:12–26) and a first-person memoir section. Boundary-driving (tier-1) devices,
each with a byte-verified specimen:

- **Era/time-frame openers**: 1:1 וּבִשְׁנַ֣ת אַחַ֗ת לְכ֨וֹרֶשׁ֙ מֶ֣לֶךְ פָּרַ֔ס;
  3:1 וַיִּגַּע֙ הַחֹ֣דֶשׁ הַשְּׁבִיעִ֔י + assembly כְּאִ֥ישׁ אֶחָ֖ד; 4:24 (Aramaic)
  עַ֚ד שְׁנַ֣ת תַּרְתֵּ֔ין לְמַלְכ֖וּת דָּרְיָ֥וֶשׁ; 7:1 וְאַחַר֙ הַדְּבָרִ֣ים הָאֵ֔לֶּה
  בְּמַלְכ֖וּת אַרְתַּחְשַׁ֣סְתְּא; 9:1 וּכְכַלּ֣וֹת אֵ֗לֶּה.
- **Document-copy/incipit formulas**: 4:11 דְּנָה֙ פַּרְשֶׁ֣גֶן אִגַּרְתָּ֔א;
  5:6 פַּרְשֶׁ֣גֶן אִ֠גַּרְתָּא דִּֽי שְׁלַ֞ח תַּתְּנַ֣י; 7:11 (Hebrew frame)
  וְזֶ֣ה פַּרְשֶׁ֣גֶן הַֽנִּשְׁתְּוָ֗ן; epistolary address-openers 4:8 (אִגְּרָ֥ה
  חֲדָ֖ה … כְּנֵֽמָא), 7:12 אַ֨רְתַּחְשַׁ֔סְתְּא מֶ֖לֶךְ מַלְכַיָּ֑א … וּכְעֶֽנֶת.
- **List/register incipits + colophons**: 2:1 וְאֵ֣לֶּה בְּנֵ֣י הַמְּדִינָ֗ה;
  8:1 וְאֵ֛לֶּה רָאשֵׁ֥י אֲבֹתֵיהֶ֖ם וְהִתְיַחְשָׂ֑ם; 10:18 וַיִּמָּצֵא֙ מִבְּנֵ֣י
  הַכֹּהֲנִ֔ים; totals/settlement colophons 2:64-67, 2:70 וַיֵּשְׁב֣וּ הַכֹּהֲנִ֣ים.
- **Language-switch seams** (tier-1 ONLY as co-occurring discourse shift, and
  they all do co-occur): 4:8 (switch + letter frame), 6:19 (switch + Hebrew
  wayyiqtol וַיַּעֲשׂ֥וּ + new festival scene), 7:12 (switch + rescript frame),
  7:27 (switch + doxology בָּר֥וּךְ יְהוָ֖ה + 1st-person onset).
- **Person-shift seams (memoir)**: 7:27-28 doxology → אֲנִ֣י framing; 9:1 princes
  approach אֵלַ֤י; 10:1 3rd-person resumption וּכְהִתְפַּלֵּ֤ל עֶזְרָא֙.
- **Speech/prayer frames**: 9:5 posture frame (וָֽאֶכְרְעָה֙ … וָאֶפְרְשָׂ֥ה כַפַּ֖י)
  opening the prayer 9:6-15; proclamation frames 1:1 וַיַּֽעֲבֶר קוֹל֙, 10:7.

Strong corroboration (tier-2, never sole driver): none specific to Ezra beyond
clause-level disjunctive accents. Weak (tier-3): ALL pe/samekh (Writings).
Metadata-only (tier-4): chapters/verses, WEB headings/footnotes, modern ¶,
WEB's |b letter-frame formatting (the 9 has_poetry_or_liturgy risk signals are
this class), red-letter/Strong's.

## 2. Inset rule (documents/prayers/lists)

Default: an embedded document stays IN its host narrative unit — split it out
only where the text gives a tier-1 double frame (introduction formula AND a
resumption after). Applications:
- Cyrus edict 1:2-4 stays inside 1:1-4 (single proclamation frame).
- The five letters each carry real incipit frames (4:11, 4:17, 5:6-7, 6:2b,
  7:11-12) — letter units are legitimate decisions, but the frame verse
  travels WITH its letter; never orphan a כְּנֵֽמָא/וּכְעֶֽנֶת address line.
- Darius: archive memo 6:3-5 and decree 6:6-12 — the מַלְכָּא-search frame
  6:1-2 introduces both; treat 6:1-12 as the larger unit test.
- Ezra's prayer 9:6-15 is ONE unit (single posture frame at 9:5; no interior
  frame); do not split the prayer.
- Registers (2:1-70, 8:1-14, 10:18-44) are single-list units with INTERNAL
  sub-register seams (see block map); never per-name rows.

## 3. Writings marker policy

pmarks_Ezra.json: 33 PE + 102 SAMEKH / 130 verses; tier-3 weak corroboration
ONLY; never driver; absence never counterevidence; every cite carries
"(single-witness)". List chapters 2/8/10 carry entry-level samekhs (incl.
verified multi-mark verses 2.69 S+S, 3.1 S+S, 8.2 S+S+S, 8.3 S+S — disclose
doubles when cited). Because mark density is extreme inside lists, reviewers
must NOT cite list-entry samekhs as seam corroboration for sub-register seams
without disclosing that the same mark follows EVERY entry in that register
(check_marks.py + sweep discipline).

## 4. Aramaic-zone review discipline

Zones 4:8–6:18, 7:12–26 (67 vv, Phase-0 verified). OL review inside zones uses
ARAMAIC grammar (determined state ־א, דִּי relatives/genitives, לֶהֱוֵא
jussive, בֵּאדַ֗יִן/אֱדַ֫יִן discourse openers, participial main verbs); Hebrew
labels there are a validator-flagged defect (check_language_zones.py). The
frame verses 4:7 and 7:11 are HEBREW and may discuss Aramaic. Ketiv/qere pairs
occur in-zone (4:7 כנותו/כְּנָוֺתָ֔יו + ארתחששתא; 5:1 נביאה/נְבִיָּ֗א,
נביאיא/נְבִיַּיָּ֔א; 7:26 לשרשו/לִשְׁרֹשִׁ֔י) — OSHB carries ketiv+qere
adjacent; disclose when a quote spans one, and quote by slicing only.
Sweep-verified discourse-onset counts (word-level, waw-prefix-aware —
CORRECTED 2026-08-06 after part-writer p02 caught the missed וֶאֱדַ֛יִן at
5:5; both counts now byte-reverified): בֵּאדַיִן 3 (4.24, 5.2, 6.1 — each sits at a
narrative juncture [4.24 resumption; 5.2 response-onset bound to 5.1; 6.1
royal response to 5.17], NOT automatically a "fresh move"; argue the local
discourse role) vs אֱדַיִן/וֶאֱדַיִן 8 tokens in 7 verses (4.9, 4.23, 5.4,
5.5 [waw-prefixed], 5.9, 5.16 ×2 [the second inside a prepositional phrase],
6.13 — bare clause-initial openers are only 6 of the 8); a reviewer citing
either form at a seam must give the function-separated count and argue the
local role, never the lexeme alone.

## 5. Cross-book discipline (binding)

2Chr 36:22-23 ≈ Ezra 1:1-3a (decree doublet); Neh 7:6-73 ≈ Ezra 2 (golah list
recension with divergent figures); Haggai/Zech 1-8 backdrop of 5:1; 1 Esdras
parallels. ALL cross-book: typed-relation metadata ONLY, never boundary
evidence in either direction, never harmonize figures across recensions. Ezra
seams are decided from Ezra's text.

## 6. Block map with larger-unit tests

- 1:1-11 decree→response→vessels (3 natural sub-seams: 1:1-4 edict frame;
  1:5-6 rising; 1:7-11 vessel inventory w/ colophon 1:11).
- 2:1-70 golah register: incipit 2:1-2a; laity by family 2:2b-35; priests
  2:36-39; Levites+singers+gatekeepers 2:40-42; nethinim+Solomon's-servants
  2:43-58; undocumented 2:59-63; totals 2:64-67; arrival+settlement 2:68-70.
  LARGER-UNIT TEST: the register is one literary act — sub-rows follow the
  text's own class headers only; NO chapter-shaped single row (cap rule), no
  per-name splitting.
- 3:1-13: altar+festivals 3:1-6; builders commissioned 3:7-9; foundation
  ceremony 3:8?—NO: 3:7 opener וַיִּתְּנוּ־כֶסֶף is the commissioning seam;
  ceremony+mixed response 3:10-13.
- 4:1-24 opposition complex: 4:1-3 rebuffed offer; 4:4-5 harassment summary;
  4:6 Ahasuerus notice; 4:7 Artaxerxes notice (Hebrew); 4:8-16 Rehum letter
  (Aramaic onset); 4:17-22 reply; 4:23 enforcement; 4:24 resumptive return to
  Darius era. LARGER-UNIT TEST: 4:6-23 is one out-of-sequence dossier —
  internal letter seams yes, but the dossier's digression character must be
  stated; 4:24's attachment (closes dossier vs. opens 5:1 unit) is a KNOWN
  LIVE SEAM — argue from בֵּאדַ֗יִן + resumption of 4:5's בטל, expect hold-
  quality dissent.
- 5:1-6:22: prophets spark 5:1-2; Tattenai inspection 5:3-5; Tattenai letter
  5:6-17 (frame 5:6-7a); Darius search+memo 6:1-5; decree 6:6-12; completion
  6:13-15; dedication 6:16-18 (Aramaic close); Passover+Unleavened Bread
  6:19-22 (Hebrew resumption). LARGER-UNIT TESTS: 6:1-12 one royal-response
  arc; 6:19-22 stays one festival unit.
- 7:1-28: Ezra genealogy+arrival 7:1-10 (genealogy 7:1-5 folds into intro —
  its incipit is the era formula, not a list frame); rescript frame+letter
  7:11-26; doxology hinge 7:27-28 — attach FORWARD to the memoir (בָּר֥וּךְ
  opens Ezra's own voice; 8:1 עִמִּ֗י continues it). Expect challenge.
- 8:1-36 memoir: caravan register 8:1-14; Levite recruitment 8:15-20; fast
  8:21-23; treasure custody 8:24-30; journey+arrival+delivery 8:31-36.
- 9:1-15: report+reaction 9:1-4; prayer 9:5-15 (posture frame + prayer ONE
  unit or two at 9:5|9:6 — writer decides from the frame syntax; prayer body
  never split).
- 10:1-44: assembly gathers 10:1-6; proclamation 10:7-8; great assembly
  10:9-15 (note 10:15 dissent-or-deputation notice עָמְד֣וּ עַל זֹ֑את — the opposition reading is contested; argue locally); commission
  10:16-17; offender register 10:18-44 (priests 10:18-22 w/ pledge 10:19;
  Levites/singers/gatekeepers 10:23-24; Israel 10:25-43; closing notice
  10:44).

## 8. Granularity + clusters

Estimate 50-62 decisions (~4.5-5.6 vv/dec, in line with 2Chr's measured 4.8),
finer in narrative, coarser inside registers. Chapter-shaped spans (a full
chapter as one row) cap at medium_low; none expected (even ch 2 subdivides).
Clusters of 8 → expect 7-8 clusters; peer wave at one agent per ~2 clusters →
~4 peer agents (r3 scoping).
