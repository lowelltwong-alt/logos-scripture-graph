# 2Kgs — Book Strategy (M8_fable, candidate-only, non-authorizing)

## selected_strategy

2Kgs is chunked on tier-1 text signals alone, with the WLC parashah apparatus (59 petuchah + 24 samekh over 83 verses, single-witness, MT numbering — pmarks_2Kgs.json) used strictly as weak corroboration per the m8-mesh-r2 addendum: in the Former Prophets both pe and samekh sit in the weak-corroboration tier and never drive or lift a boundary. The book divides into blocks demanding different chunking discipline: (1) chs 1–2, the Elijah conclusion — the Ahaziah/Baal-zebub inquiry with its three captain-of-fifty triads (1:2–17a, one escalating arc, not three units), the regnal close 1:17–18 that finishes the reign OPENED at 1Kgs 22:51–53 (cross-book continuation, REL note carried from 1Kgs), and ch 2, which sits wholly OUTSIDE the regnal framework between Ahaziah's close and Jehoram's opening at 3:1 — the succession block (Jordan itinerary and ascension 2:1–18, bound by the twin refrains "Yahweh has sent me to X" / "As Yahweh lives… I will not leave you"; then two short accreditation vignettes 2:19–22, 2:23–25); (2) chs 3–8, the Elisha cycle: the Moab campaign (3:4–27) inside Jehoram's frame, then the miracle catalogue — a chain of self-contained word-and-deed vignettes each opened by its own arrival/petition formula (4:1, 4:8, 4:38, 4:42, 6:1) around the longer Naaman (5) and Aram narratives (6:8–23; the siege arc 6:24–7:20, which crosses the modern chapter seam and is chunked as its own multi-scene complex; 8:1–6; 8:7–15), closing with two Judahite regnal frames (8:16–24, 8:25–29); (3) chs 9–10, the Jehu revolution — commission (9:1–13), three killing scenes (Joram/Ahaziah 9:14–29; Jezebel 9:30–37; the seventy sons and kinsmen 10:1–17), the Baal-temple ruse (10:18–28), and the evaluation/close (10:29–36), the whole arc threaded by fulfillment notices to Elijah's Naboth oracles; (4) chs 11–16, the Joash-to-Ahaz regnal framework with narrative insets (Athaliah coup-and-counter-coup 11; temple repair 12; Elisha's death complex 13:14–25; Beth-shemesh clash 14:8–14; the seven-reign rapid framework of ch 15; the Damascus altar 16:10–18) — NOTE the versification offset: WEB 11:21–12:21 = MT 12:1–22, every oshb cite there dual-cited; (5) ch 17, the fall of Samaria with the great exile homily 17:7–23 (pe at 17:6 and 17:23 as weak corroboration of both edges) and the resettlement/syncretism complex 17:24–41; (6) chs 18–20, the Hezekiah complex — reform opening, the annalistic tribute notice 18:13–16, the two Assyrian embassies with the Rabshakeh speeches (turn-marker discipline), Hezekiah's prayer, Isaiah's long oracle with the poetic Zion taunt-song 19:21–28 (set as prose in WEB — no q-markers anywhere in this substrate; the elevated register is argued from internal form, not markup), deliverance, illness/shadow sign, and the Babylonian envoys; (7) chs 21–23, Manasseh/Amon and the Josiah complex (book-finding, Huldah, covenant, the location-articulated reform catalogue 23:4–20 with the Bethel fulfillment notice 23:15–18 → 1Kgs 13:2, Passover, wrath notice, Megiddo); (8) chs 23:31–25:30, the fall sequence — four last reigns, two sieges, the destruction and deportation registers, Gedaliah (25:22–26), and the Jehoiachin release ending 25:27–30 (corpus-closing hinge, cross-book note to Jer 52:31–34). Boundary drivers throughout: regnal opening and closing formulas, accession/conspiracy formulas, commission and messenger formulas, arrival/petition formulas opening the miracle vignettes, campaign-muster and date formulas, fulfillment notices, letter frames, explicit scene/participant/location change.
