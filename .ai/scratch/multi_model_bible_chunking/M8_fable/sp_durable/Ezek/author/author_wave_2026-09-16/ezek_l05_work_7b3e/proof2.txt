=== P10-001/boundary_rationale
Onset side: MT 40:1 opens with the dateline בְּאַרְבַּ֤ע עֶשְׂרֵה֙ שָׁנָ֔ה אַחַ֕ר אֲשֶׁ֥ר הֻכְּתָ֖ה הָעִ֑יר (“after the city was struck” (web:Ezek.40.1)) -- the fourteenth and last of the book's fourteen year-bearing datelines -- paired in the same verse with the hand-of-YHWH idiom הָיְתָ֤ה עָלַי֙ יַד יְהוָ֔ה and the transport verb וַיָּבֵ֥א אֹתִ֖י שָֽׁמָּה (oshb:Ezek.40.1). This dateline-hand-transport cluster is the strongest onset class the book offers and is also a HARD seam: no row may cross it. It follows immediately, without intervening material, the utterance formula and pe (oshb:Ezek.39.29, single-witness) that close the preceding unit. The frame continues at 40:2 with the header בְּמַרְא֣וֹת אֱלֹהִ֔ים “In the visions of God” (web:Ezek.40.2; one of only three such headers in the book) plus a further transport pair (הֱבִיאַ֖נִי / וַיְנִיחֵ֗נִי (oshb:Ezek.40.2)), and again at 40:3 (וַיָּבֵ֨יא אוֹתִ֜י שָׁ֗מָּה (oshb:Ezek.40.3)) introducing the measuring man. Close side: 40:4 carries no formula-grade close device; the seam at 40:4/40:5 rests on the man's charge, הַגֵּ֛ד אֶת כָּל אֲשֶׁר אַתָּ֥ה רֹאֶ֖ה לְבֵ֥ית יִשְׂרָאֵֽל (oshb:Ezek.40.4) “Declare all that you see to the house of Israel.” (web:Ezek.40.4), read as ending the entry scene before the measuring reed is put to use in 40:5's וַיָּ֜מָד (oshb:Ezek.40.5), the first of an eighteen-verse run of that verb across 40:5-41:5. Both sides are weighed: the onset is the best-evidenced in the whole span; the close is a content judgment (charge-speech versus measuring-action), not a refrain, and is held accordingly.
