## Selected strategy
Discourse-structure chunking. Deuteronomy is organized as Moses' farewell discourses (historical review, law restatement, covenant renewal, closing poems); substrate: 34 chapters, 959 verses, genre_law, with heavy poetry concentration at ch.31(end)-32 (Song of Moses) and ch.33 (Blessing of Moses), both substrate-confirmed and matching known content. Legal case-law chapters (12-26) are mostly chunked per topic/chapter since that mirrors the text's own organization; the two poetic closing chapters are each kept as a single unit given their compositional unity.

## Literature type / mixed genre
Narrative discourse framing (ch.1-11, 27-34) around a central law_code core (ch.12-26), with two major poetic units (Song of Moses 31:30-32:47; Blessing of Moses 33:1-29).
