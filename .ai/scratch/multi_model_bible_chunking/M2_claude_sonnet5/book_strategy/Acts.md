# Book Strategy — Acts (M2_claude_sonnet5)

- **strategy_id:** literary_marker_aware_v2 | **book:** Acts | **pilot_book:** false

## Selected strategy
Narrative-cycle chunking following Acts' well-established geographic/missionary structure
(Jerusalem ch.1-7, Judea/Samaria/Gentile expansion ch.8-12, Paul's missionary journeys ch.13-21,
Paul's trials and journey to Rome ch.22-28). Multi-chapter episodes (Cornelius's conversion and
its Jerusalem defense, the first missionary journey, the Festus/Agrippa trial) are kept as single
units given their narrative continuity.

## Literature type / mixed genre
narrative throughout, including several embedded sermons/speeches (Peter at Pentecost, Stephen,
Paul at the Areopagus, Paul's farewell to the Ephesian elders).

## Substrate markers considered
WJ/red-letter marker presence noted at Acts.9 (Damascus road, the risen Christ's words),
Acts.18/20/22-23/25-26 (quotations and recollections of Jesus' words, including the otherwise-
unrecorded saying at 20:35); per CD-021/CD-022, cited as evidence only, never as speaker- or
discourse-boundary authority. Poetry markers verified at chapters containing OT/psalm citations
within speeches (2, 4, 7, 8, 13, 15, 28).

## Strong's metadata — evidence only
Not used to resolve any interpretive question.

## Chapter-only fallback
Not used as a default; boundaries follow narrative-episode and missionary-journey structure.

## Expected low-confidence / doctrinally sensitive regions
Pentecost (2, pneumatology/ecclesiology), Ananias and Sapphira (4:32-5:11, divine judgment),
Saul's conversion narratives (9, 22, 25-26), the Cornelius episode (10-11, Gentile-mission
theology), and the Jerusalem Council (15, law/gospel and covenant-theology debates).

## Frontier / atlas candidate expectations
Concentrated at the conversion narratives and the Jerusalem Council.
