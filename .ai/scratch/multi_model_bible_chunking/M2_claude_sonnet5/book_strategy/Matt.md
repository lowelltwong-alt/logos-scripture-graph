## Selected strategy
Narrative-cycle and discourse-unit chunking following Matt's well-established structure.
Major discourses (Sermon on the Mount, Mission/Community/Olivet Discourses in Matthew; the
parables and Olivet discourses in Mark) are kept as single units given their recognized
compositional unity, even where they span multiple chapters (Matt.24-25).

## Literature type / mixed genre
narrative and gospel_discourse.

where applicable. Per CD-021/CD-022, WJ markers are cited as evidence only — they never authorize
Jesus speaker attribution, speaker boundaries, discourse boundaries, or chunk boundaries in this
scratch map. No WJ-driven boundary decision is asserted anywhere in this book.

## Chapter-only fallback
Not used as a default; boundaries follow narrative-scene and discourse-unit structure, frequently
spanning or subdividing chapter lines (e.g., the Sermon on the Mount spans ch.5-7; Matt.24-25 kept
together as one discourse).
