# Book Strategy — Jeremiah (M2_claude_sonnet5)

- **strategy_id:** literary_marker_aware_v2 | **book:** Jer | **pilot_book:** false

## Selected strategy
Section-based chunking following Jeremiah's recognized macro-structure: early judgment oracles
(2-25), narratives about false prophets and Jeremiah's persecution (26-29), the Book of
Consolation (30-33), narratives of Jerusalem's fall and its aftermath (34-45), oracles against
foreign nations (46-51), and the historical appendix (52, paralleling 2Kgs.24-25). Sign-act
narratives (linen loincloth, potter's house, broken flask, yoke, purchased field) are each kept as
one unit since they are self-contained enacted-prophecy episodes.

## Literature type / mixed genre
prophetic_oracle and narrative interleaved throughout, reflecting Jeremiah's own mixed
compositional character (oracles, sign-act narratives, biographical material).

## Substrate markers considered
Non-poetry-flagged (prose) chapters identified directly from the substrate (3-5, 7, 19, 24, 27-29,
34-37, 39-45, 52) and used to inform narrative vs. oracle literature-type distinctions.

## Strong's metadata — evidence only
Not used to resolve any interpretive question.

## Chapter-only fallback
Not used as a default; boundaries follow sign-act, oracle-cycle, and narrative-arc structure and
frequently span or subdivide chapter lines.

## Expected low-confidence / doctrinally sensitive regions
The righteous Branch oracle (23:5-6), the New Covenant oracle (31:31-34, among the most
theologically significant texts in the book across traditions), the Davidic/Levitical covenant
renewal (33), and the fall of Jerusalem (39) as the book's central theodicy turning point.
