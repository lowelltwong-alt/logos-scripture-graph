# Book Strategy — Ezekiel (M2_claude_sonnet5)

- **strategy_id:** literary_marker_aware_v2 | **book:** Ezek | **pilot_book:** false

## Selected strategy
Section-based chunking following Ezekiel's well-established macro-structure: the inaugural vision
and call (1-3), judgment sign-acts and oracles against Jerusalem before its fall (4-24), oracles
against foreign nations (25-32), restoration oracles including the dry-bones vision and the Gog of
Magog oracle (33-39), and the extended Temple vision (40-48). Large visionary/architectural units
(the inaugural vision, the Temple-abominations vision, the Temple-measurement vision, the Egypt
oracle cycle) are kept as single chunks given their recognized compositional unity.

## Literature type / mixed genre
narrative (visions, sign-acts), prophetic_oracle (judgment and restoration oracles), and law_code
(Temple/priestly regulations, 44-46).

## Substrate markers considered
Non-poetry-flagged (prose) chapters identified directly from the substrate (1-5, 7-17, 20, 22, 25,
34-36, 38-48) and used to inform the literature-type distinction; `has_variant_reading` flag noted
at book level but not used as boundary authority.

## Strong's metadata — evidence only
Not used to resolve any interpretive question.

## Chapter-only fallback
Not used as a default; boundaries follow vision, sign-act, oracle-cycle, and architectural-unit
structure, frequently spanning or subdividing chapter lines.

## Expected low-confidence / doctrinally sensitive regions
The inaugural vision (1-3) and Temple-abominations/glory-departure vision (8-11) for their
symbolic density; the two extended marriage-allegory chapters (16, 23) for their graphic content;
the King of Tyre lament (28:11-19) for its reception history; the new-heart promise (36:26-27),
dry-bones vision (37), and Gog of Magog oracle (38-39) for their significant and often disputed
eschatological/restoration-theology weight; the returning-glory (43) and river (47) visions for
their symbolic theological weight.
