# Book Strategy — Job (M2_claude_sonnet5)

- **strategy_id:** literary_marker_aware_v2 | **book:** Job | **pilot_book:** false (not in T423 pilot set, but book_strategy required by the protocol's per-book list)

## Selected strategy
Speech-cycle chunking matching Job's well-attested dialogue architecture: prose frame (1-2, 42:7-17),
three cycles of speeches between Job and his three friends (3-27), the Hymn to Wisdom interlude (28),
Job's closing monologue (29-31), Elihu's four speeches (32-37), the LORD's two speeches with Job's two
brief responses (38-42:6). Substrate confirms the prose/poetry boundary exactly: ch.1-2 carry no
poetry/liturgy marker; ch.3-42 all do (consistent with Job being almost entirely poetic dialogue
after the prologue, including the prose epilogue's chapter (42) because it also contains Job's final
poetic response at 42:1-6).

## Literature type / mixed genre
Narrative (prose frame) and wisdom_dialogue (the entire speech cycle, Elihu's speeches, the divine
speeches).

## Substrate markers considered
Per-chapter poetry/liturgy risk flag checked for all 42 chapters and found to align exactly with the
known prose/poetry boundary.

## Strong's metadata — evidence only
Job carries substantial Hebrew Strong's tags (many hapax legomena and rare vocabulary); not cited as
lexical-truth or doctrinal authority in any chunk boundary decision.

## Chapter-only fallback
Not used; every boundary follows the speech-cycle structure, which frequently spans or subdivides
chapters (e.g., 4-5 as one Eliphaz speech; 12-14 as one Job reply closing a cycle).

## Expected low-confidence / doctrinally sensitive regions
The 'redeemer' text (19:25, long messianic/resurrection-reading history), the disrupted third speech
cycle (25, 26-27 — a genuine compositional-critical question, not resolved here), the Hymn to Wisdom's
speaker-attribution ambiguity (28), the two divine speeches (38-39, 40:6-41:34) and Job's repentance
(42:1-6) which together carry the book's central theodicy weight, per book_specific_hints
PoetryWisdom risks (dialogue_speaker_shift).

## Frontier / atlas candidate expectations
Roughly 6 rows expected — Job concentrates more genuine compositional-critical (not just
theological) uncertainty than any other Wave 3 book so far.
