# Book Strategy — 2 Samuel (M2_claude_sonnet5)

- **strategy_id:** literary_marker_aware_v2 | **book:** 2Sam | **pilot_book:** false

## Selected strategy
Narrative-cycle chunking (David's rise, the Davidic covenant, the Bathsheba/Absalom complex, closing appendices). Substrate: 24 chapters, 695 verses, with poetry at ch.1 (David's Lament), ch.22 (Song of Deliverance, a near-duplicate of Ps.18), and ch.23:1-7 (Last Words), all confirmed. Chapters 2-3 (civil war), 10-12 (Bathsheba complex), and 15-17 (Absalom's rebellion) are each kept as one continuous narrative unit rather than split at chapter lines.

## Literature type / mixed genre
Narrative throughout, with three poetic insets and one genealogical list (the mighty men, 23:8-39).

## Substrate markers considered
All poetry-flagged chapters (1, 22, 23) verified against verse-level data.

## Strong's metadata — evidence only
Not used to resolve any interpretive question.

## Chapter-only fallback
Used for many self-contained episodes; several multi-chapter narrative arcs are deliberately grouped.

## Expected low-confidence / doctrinally sensitive regions
David's Lament (1:17-27), the ark's transport and Uzzah's death (6), Nathan's oracle/Davidic covenant (7, foundational messianic-covenant text), the Bathsheba/Uriah/Nathan complex (10-12), Amnon/Tamar (13), the Gibeonite execution (21), David's Song (22) and Last Words (23:1-7), and the census/plague theodicy (24).
