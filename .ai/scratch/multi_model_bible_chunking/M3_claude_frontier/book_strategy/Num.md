# Numbers — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass, independent judgment)

## Primary literature type
Mixed narrative + law (`genre_narrative`): census/camp lists, wilderness narrative,
priestly law, and substantial embedded **poetry** — the Aaronic blessing (6:24–26),
the Song of the Well / Heshbon ballad (21), and the **Balaam oracles** (23–24).

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- Poetry (`has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` + q1/q2) at 6, 21, 23, 24.
- ch7 p=114 (leaders' offerings — a long repetitive list). Census chapters (1,3,4,26)
  are list-form. `has_strong_h` — Strong's Hebrew **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- **Census/list** blocks (1–4; 26; 33 itinerary) kept as list units.
- **Aaronic blessing** split out (6:22–27) as a poetic benediction and flagged.
- **Spies/rebellion** (13–14) and **Korah** (16–17) as narrative arcs.
- ch21 split into the Song-of-the-Well journey block and the Sihon/Og victory block, both
  carrying poetry and both flagged.
- **Balaam oracles** (23; 24) kept as oracle-cycle units and flagged (incl. 24:17
  "a star out of Jacob", a messianic-reading pressure point — surfaced, not decided).

## Strong's / WJ handling
Strong's Hebrew = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a (OT).

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- All embedded-poetry units (6:22–27; 21; 23; 24) are flagged for stanza/oracle review.
  The Balaam material additionally carries messianic-interpretation pressure, made
  transparent in the frontier/atlas sidecars rather than encoded in the boundary.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow census-list, narrative-arc, law-topic, and oracle-cycle form; poetry is
isolated into its own units; several chapters are split or arced.
