# 3 John — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** a single-chapter
personal letter (`genre_epistles`, 14 verses): commending Gaius, rebuking Diotrephes, praising Demetrius.

## Markers / evidence
Prose (`p`). `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Split into its **epistolary units**: greeting and prayer for Gaius (1:1–4); the body — hospitality,
Diotrephes, and Demetrius (1:5–12); the closing (1:13–14). A deliberate sub-chapter division by letter form.

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
Low overall risk; the Diotrephes/authority conflict is noted as evidence, not encoded in the boundary.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
The single chapter is split into greeting/body/closing by the letter's own form, not treated as one block.
