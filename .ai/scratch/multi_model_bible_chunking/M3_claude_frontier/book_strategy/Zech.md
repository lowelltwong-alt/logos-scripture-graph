# Zechariah — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** apocalyptic
**night visions** (1–6), sermon/promise oracles (7–8), and two poetic **massa oracles** (9–14),
one of the most messianically-cited books in the NT (`genre_prophets`).

## Markers / evidence
Prose-set visions (1–8) with poetry (`has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` + q1/q2) in 9; 10; 11; 13.
`has_footnote` — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's Hebrew **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **vision report / oracle unit**: the eight night visions (each vision its own unit, some
paired as arcs 1:18–2:13; 5:1–11), the crowning of Joshua (6:9–15), the fasting question (7) and
restoration promises (8), then the two massa oracles by movement (the coming king 9; the shepherds
11; Jerusalem's deliverance 12:1–13:6; the smitten shepherd 13:7–9; the day of the LORD 14).

## Strong's / WJ
Strong's = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence / frontier triggers (heavy messianic pressure)
The **Branch** (3:8; 6:12), the **king on a donkey** (9:9, Matt 21:5), the **thirty pieces of
silver** (11:12–13, Matt 27:9), "**they will look on me whom they pierced**" (12:10, John 19:37),
"**strike the shepherd**" (13:7, Matt 26:31), and the **apocalyptic day of the LORD** (14) are all
flagged — surfaced in the sidecars, interpretation not decided by the boundary.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow vision/oracle seams with several sub-chapter and cross-chapter units; the dense
messianic pressure points are isolated and escalated.
