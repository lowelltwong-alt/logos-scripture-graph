# 2 John — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** a single-chapter
personal letter (`genre_epistles`, 13 verses): walk in truth and love; shun deceivers.

## Markers / evidence
Prose (`p`/`f`). `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Rather than one whole-book block, the letter is split into its **epistolary units**: greeting (1:1–3);
the body — walk in truth/love and the warning against deceivers who deny Christ come in the flesh
(1:4–11); the closing (1:12–13). A deliberate sub-chapter division by letter form.

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
Low overall risk; the christological test (v7, "those who don't confess Jesus Christ came in the
flesh") is noted as evidence, not encoded in the boundary.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
The single chapter is split into greeting/body/closing by the letter's own form — the opposite of a
whole-chapter block.
