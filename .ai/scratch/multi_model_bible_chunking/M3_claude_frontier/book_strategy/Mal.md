# Malachi — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** prophetic
**disputation** oracles (`genre_prophets`); prose-set, structured as accusation → objection → reply.

## Markers / evidence
`p`/`f` only. `has_footnote` — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's Hebrew **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **disputation unit**, which cross the chapter divisions: "I have loved you" (1:1–5);
against the priests' blemished offerings (1:6–2:9); faithlessness and divorce (2:10–16); the
messenger of the covenant (2:17–3:5); robbing God in tithes (3:6–12); the righteous and the day
(3:13–4:3); the closing charge and the coming of Elijah (4:4–6).

## Strong's / WJ
Strong's = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence / frontier triggers
2:17–3:5 flagged for "the messenger of the covenant" / "my messenger" (3:1, cited Mark 1:2);
4:4–6 flagged for "Elijah" (Mal 4:5–6, applied to John the Baptist, Matt 11:14; 17:11–13).
Surfaced, not encoded.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the disputation units, which cross chapters — not a chapter map.
