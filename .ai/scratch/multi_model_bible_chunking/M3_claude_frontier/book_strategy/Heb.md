# Hebrews — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** a "word of
exhortation" — a sermon/treatise (`genre_epistles`) alternating **exposition** (Christ's supremacy,
priesthood, and sacrifice) with **warning/exhortation** passages, saturated with OT citation.

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- `has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` + q1/q2 on the catenae (ch1; 8 quoting Jer 31; 10) and heavy `x`
  crossrefs. `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- Chunked by **exposition/exhortation unit**, following the sermon's alternation: the Son superior to
  angels (1); the first warning (2); greater than Moses / the rest (3:1–4:13, arc); the high priest
  and Melchizedek introduced (4:14–5:10); the apostasy warning and God's oath (5:11–6:20); Melchizedek
  (7); the new covenant (8); the sanctuary and once-for-all sacrifice (9); one sacrifice perfects
  (10:1–18); the perseverance warning (10:19–39); the faith chapter (11); the race and Mount Zion (12);
  final exhortations and benediction (13).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek and OT-citation markers are **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide
doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers (dense theological pressure)
- The **christology** (1:3, 8), the **warning/apostasy passages** (6:4–6; 10:26–31 — the
  perseverance/eternal-security crux), the **Melchizedek priesthood** (7), the **new covenant** (8:6–13
  — supersession pressure), and the **atonement** (9:11–28) flagged — surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the sermon's exposition/warning alternation, several as arcs; the warning passages
are isolated as their own units rather than absorbed into chapters.
