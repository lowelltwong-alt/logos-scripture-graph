# Titus — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): a church-order letter for Crete — elders, sound doctrine, and good works.

## Markers / evidence
Prose (`p`/`f` only). `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**. `has_footnote` — evidence only.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **topic unit**: greeting, elders, and false teachers (1); sound doctrine for household
groups and the grace that trains (2); doing good, the washing of regeneration, and closing (3).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The **regeneration/renewal** passage (3:4–7, "the washing of regeneration and renewal by the Holy
Spirit", a baptism/soteriology crux) flagged — surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Each unit's evidence names its church-order topic; the regeneration passage is isolated and escalated
rather than left as a bare chapter.
