# 2 Timothy — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): Paul's farewell letter, exhorting Timothy to guard the gospel and endure.

## Markers / evidence
Prose; `has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` at ch2 (the 2:11–13 faithful saying). `has_strong_g` — Strong's
**Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **topic unit**: guard the deposit (1); the approved worker and the faithful saying (2);
the last days and the God-breathed Scripture (3); the charge to preach and Paul's farewell (4).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The **inspiration-of-Scripture** passage (3:14–17, "all Scripture is God-breathed") flagged for its
bibliological weight — surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the letter's topic units; the faithful saying (2:11–13) is noted within its unit
and the inspiration passage isolated.
