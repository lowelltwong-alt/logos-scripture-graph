# Haggai — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** dated prophetic
oracles calling for the temple's rebuilding (`genre_prophets`); prose-set.

## Markers / evidence
`p`/`f` only. `has_footnote` — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's Hebrew **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **dated oracle unit** (Haggai's own date-headings): the call to rebuild (1); the greater
glory of the latter temple (2:1–9); the ruling on defiled offerings and the turn to blessing
(2:10–19); the word to Zerubbabel as signet ring (2:20–23). ch2 is split into its three dated
oracles rather than kept as one block.

## Strong's / WJ
Strong's = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence / frontier triggers
2:1–9 flagged for "I will shake the heavens" (2:6–7, cited Heb 12:26); 2:20–23 flagged for the
signet-ring promise to Zerubbabel (Davidic/messianic pressure). Surfaced, not encoded.

**Strategy** splits by dated oracle — not silent chapter-only.
