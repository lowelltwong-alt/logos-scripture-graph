# Judges — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass, independent judgment)

## Primary literature type
Narrative (`genre_narrative`) built on the recurring apostasy→oppression→deliverer cycle,
with a major embedded poem (the **Song of Deborah**, ch5) and a double appendix (17–21).

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- Poetry (`has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` + q1=39/q2=58/b=11) at **ch5**; minor q at ch14
  (Samson's riddle). High `p` density on the longer cycles (6, 9, 11, 16, 20).
- `has_footnote` on textual notes — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- **Cycle introductions** (1:1–2:5; 2:6–3:6) as thematic arcs.
- **Deliverer cycles** by judge (Othniel/Ehud/Shamgar together; Gideon 6–8 by scene;
  Abimelech 9; Jephthah 11; Samson 13–16 by scene).
- **Song of Deborah** (5) kept as its own poetic unit and flagged.
- **Appendices** (Micah/Danites 17–18 as an arc; the Levite's concubine 19; the Benjamin
  war 20; the aftermath 21).

## Strong's / WJ handling
Strong's Hebrew = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a (OT).

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- ch5 (Song of Deborah, poetry) flagged for stanza review.
- ch9 flagged for the embedded **Jotham's fable** (9:8–15), a poetic parable.
- ch11 (Jephthah's vow) flagged: an ethical/theological crux (the vow's outcome) — surfaced
  for review, not resolved by the boundary.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Cycle and scene form drive boundaries; the poem is isolated; appendices are arced.
