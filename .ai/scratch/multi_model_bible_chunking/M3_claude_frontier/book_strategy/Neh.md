# Nehemiah — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass, independent judgment)

## Primary literature type
Narrative (`genre_narrative`, largely first-person memoir) with **registers/lists** (3
builders; 7 returnees; 10 signatories; 11–12 residents/clergy) and a long covenant-confession
prayer (9). Substrate `has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` at 7 reflects the `b`/`li1` list-set.

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- `li1`=40 at ch7 (returnee register). High `p` on narrative scenes. `has_footnote` — evidence
  only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- Wall-building narrative by scene (prayer 1; commission 2; opposition 4; oppression 5;
  completion 6). **Lists** (3; 7; 10; 11) as list units; the covenant renewal (8–10) by unit
  (Law read 8; confession 9; sealed covenant 10).
- Final reforms (13) as a distinct unit.

## Strong's / WJ handling
Strong's Hebrew = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a (OT).

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- ch7 (returnee register paralleling Ezra 2) flagged as a list/parallel-text review point.
- ch9 (the great confession, a theology-of-history prayer) flagged for review — surfaced, not
  encoded in the boundary.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
List-form and scene-form drive boundaries; the covenant-renewal block is unit-chunked.
Evidence/strategy cite the substrate list and paragraph markers.
