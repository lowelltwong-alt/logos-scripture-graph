# James — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** wisdom **paraenesis**
(`genre_epistles`): loosely linked exhortations in a diatribe/proverbial style.

## Markers / evidence
Prose; `has_crossref`/`x` (OT and Jesus-tradition echoes). `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **topic/paraenetic unit**: trials and temptation (1:1–18); hearing and doing (1:19–27);
against favoritism (2:1–13); faith and works (2:14–26); the tongue (3:1–12); wisdom from above and
worldliness (3:13–4:12, arc); against the presumptuous rich (4:13–5:6, arc); patience, prayer, and
restoration (5:7–20).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek and Jesus-tradition echoes are **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or
decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
**Faith and works** (2:14–26, "faith without works is dead"; Abraham "justified by works") flagged
for its classic tension with Paul (a Reformation-era crux, and a T385 owner-decision topic in this
repo); surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the paraenetic topics, several crossing chapters; the faith/works unit is isolated
and escalated rather than left as a bare chapter.
