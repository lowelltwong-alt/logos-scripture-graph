# 2 Thessalonians — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): a corrective letter on the day of the Lord and idleness.

## Markers / evidence
Prose; `has_variant_reading` at ch2 — evidence only. `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **rhetorical unit**: greeting, thanksgiving, and judgment at Christ's coming (1:1–12); the
**man of lawlessness** and the day of the Lord (2:1–17); the warning against idleness and closing
(3:1–18).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The **man of lawlessness / the restrainer** (2:1–12, a major eschatological/antichrist crux, carrying
a textual variant) flagged — surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the letter's argument units; the eschatological unit (2:1–17) is isolated and
escalated rather than treated as a bare chapter.
