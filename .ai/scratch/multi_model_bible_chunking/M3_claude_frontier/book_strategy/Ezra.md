# Ezra — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass, independent judgment)

## Primary literature type
Narrative (`genre_narrative`) with **embedded official documents** (Aramaic decrees/letters,
4:8–6:18; 7:12–26) and **returnee lists** (2; 8; 10). The substrate `has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker`
at 4–7 reflects the block-set (`b`) document material, not verse poetry.

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- `b` (block) markers at 4–7 (set-off documents); `li1` list-item markers at 8, 10 (registers).
- `has_footnote` textual notes — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- **Returnee lists** (2; 8:1–14 within the journey; 10:18–44) as list units.
- **Document-bearing** episodes (opposition/letters 4; rebuilding+Darius decree 5:1–6:22 arc;
  Ezra's commission+Artaxerxes letter 7) chunked as scene units and flagged, since they embed
  quoted decrees and an Aramaic language shift.
- Ezra's prayer (9) and the intermarriage resolution (10) as their own units.

## Strong's / WJ handling
Strong's Hebrew/Aramaic = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a (OT).

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- ch4, 5:1–6:22, and 7 flagged: embedded quoted documents and the Hebrew↔Aramaic language
  shift are review points (where a document begins/ends, and its evidential status). The
  documents are treated as evidence, not as source-tradition authority.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow list/document/scene form; the rebuilding decree is arced. Evidence/strategy
cite the substrate block and list markers per unit.
