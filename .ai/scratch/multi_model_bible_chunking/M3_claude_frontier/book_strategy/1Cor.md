# 1 Corinthians — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): responses to reports and to the Corinthians' questions ("now concerning…"),
topic by topic.

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- `has_wj` at ch11 (the Lord's-Supper words, 11:23–25) — **evidence only**. `has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker`
  on OT citations (ch1; 2; 15). `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- Chunked by **topical/rhetorical unit**: greeting+thanksgiving (1:1–9); divisions and the wisdom of
  the cross (1:10–4:21 by movement); immorality and lawsuits (5–6 by topic); marriage (7); food and
  idols and freedom (8:1–11:1 by sub-topic); worship order (11:2–14:40, with the Lord's Supper, love,
  and gifts as units); the resurrection (15); the collection and closing (16).
- The **love chapter** (13) is isolated as its own rhetorical unit within the gifts section.

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
WJ (11:23–25) and Strong's Greek are **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- The **Lord's Supper** (11:17–34, eucharistic/discernment pressure) and the **resurrection** (15,
  the fullest NT treatment, incl. 15:29 baptism-for-the-dead) flagged — surfaced, not decided. The
  head-covering (11:2–16) and women-in-worship (14:34–35, a textual/interpretive crux) noted.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the topical units (several crossing chapters); the love chapter and the Supper are
isolated as their own rhetorical units, not left as bare chapters.
