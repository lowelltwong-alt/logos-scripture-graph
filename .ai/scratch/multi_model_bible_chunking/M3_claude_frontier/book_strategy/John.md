# John — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** Gospel
(`genre_gospels`): a Logos prologue, a "book of signs" (1–12) with long discourses, a Farewell
Discourse and High-Priestly Prayer (13–17), a passion, and an epilogue (21). High christology and
several textual/theological cruxes.

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- `has_wj` very dense in the discourses (ch8 wj=92; ch6 wj=82) — **evidence only**. `has_crossref`/`x`;
  ch7 carries an `nb` (no-break) marker around the disputed pericope. `has_strong_g` — Strong's
  **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- Chunked by **sign / discourse / dialogue unit**. The prologue (1:1–18) is isolated; the bread-of-life
  (6), Good-Shepherd (10), and Farewell (14; 15:1–16:4; 16:5–33) discourses are units; the High-
  Priestly Prayer (17) is its own unit.
- **The pericope adulterae (7:53–8:11) is isolated as its own unit** and ch8 is split at 8:11/8:12,
  because that passage is textually uncertain (absent from the earliest manuscripts).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
WJ/red-letter and Strong's Greek are **evidence only** — the very heavy red-lettering in John is not
allowed to set boundaries or decide christology; discourse seams follow the narrative frame. The
**John 3 Jesus/narrator speaker boundary** (where Jesus' words end and the evangelist's comment
begins) is treated as an open review question, not resolved by the boundary.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- **7:53–8:11 flagged** as a major **textual variant**; the **Logos prologue** (1:1–18), the "I AM"
  sayings (8:58), "I and the Father are one" (10:30), the eucharistic bread discourse (6:53–58),
  "I am the way" (14:6), and "it is finished" (19:30) flagged for christological pressure; the John 3
  speaker boundary flagged. Surfaced, not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Sign/discourse seams drive boundaries, several discourses cross chapters, and the pericope adulterae
is isolated rather than absorbed into the chapter.
