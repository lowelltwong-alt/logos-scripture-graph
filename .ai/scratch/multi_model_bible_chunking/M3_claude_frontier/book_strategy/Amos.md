# Amos — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** prophetic poetry
+ vision reports (`genre_prophets`).

## Markers / evidence
Dense q1/q2 poetry (prose in ch7 vision/Amaziah narrative). `has_footnote` — evidence only.
`has_strong_h` — Strong's Hebrew **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **oracle / vision unit**: the "for three transgressions" oracle cycle against the
nations (1:1–2:16, arc); the "hear this word" oracles (3; 4; 5; 6); the vision cycle with the
Amaziah confrontation (7:1–8:3, arc); the famine of the word (8:4–14); the altar vision and
restoration (9). The "booth of David" (9:11–12, cited Acts 15) is inside the closing unit.

## Strong's / WJ
Strong's = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence / frontier triggers
9:1–15 flagged for the restoration/"booth of David" passage (Acts 15:16–17) — surfaced, not encoded.
Single-chapter poetic oracles marked `medium_low` for strophe review.

**Strategy** is oracle/vision-cycle based, not silent chapter-only.
