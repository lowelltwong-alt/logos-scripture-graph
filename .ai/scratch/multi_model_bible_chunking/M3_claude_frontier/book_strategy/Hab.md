# Habakkuk — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** prophetic
**dialogue** (complaint/answer) + a closing psalm (`genre_prophets`).

## Markers / evidence
Prose-set dialogue in 1–2; ch3 is poetry (`has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` + q1/q2, with `qs` Selah
and the "Shigionoth" heading). `has_footnote` — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's evidence only.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **dialogue turn and poem**: the prophet's complaints and the LORD's first reply
(1:1–2:1, split at the watchtower 2:1); the LORD's answer with the five woes and "the righteous
shall live by faith" (2:2–20); the prophet's psalm/theophany (3).

## Strong's / WJ
Strong's = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence / frontier triggers
2:2–20 flagged for **2:4** ("the righteous shall live by his faith", cited Rom 1:17; Gal 3:11;
Heb 10:38) — a central NT proof-text, surfaced not encoded. ch3 flagged as a poetic theophany-psalm
(Selah markers) for strophe review.

**Strategy** follows dialogue turns and isolates the psalm — not silent chapter-only.
