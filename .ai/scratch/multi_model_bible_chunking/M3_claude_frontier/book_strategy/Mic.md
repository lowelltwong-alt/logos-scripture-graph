# Micah — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** prophetic poetry
(`genre_prophets`): alternating judgment and hope oracles.

## Markers / evidence
Dense q1/q2 poetry throughout. `has_footnote` — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's Hebrew
**evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **oracle unit** (mostly one clear oracle per chapter here): judgment on Samaria/Judah
(1); oppressors and regathering (2); rulers and false prophets (3); the mountain of the LORD (4);
the ruler from Bethlehem (5); the LORD's controversy / "what does the LORD require" (6); misery and
trust (7).

## Strong's / WJ
Strong's = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence / frontier triggers
4:1–3 (Zion's future, paralleling Isa 2:2–4) and **5:2** (the ruler from Bethlehem, cited Matt 2:6)
flagged for messianic/intertextual pressure — surfaced, not encoded. Single-chapter poetic oracles
marked `medium_low` for strophe review.

**Strategy** is oracle-based (with the chapter alignment explained per unit), not silent chapter-only.
