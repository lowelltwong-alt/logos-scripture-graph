# Ezekiel — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass, independent judgment)

## Primary literature type
Prophetic **vision + sign-act + allegory + oracle** (`genre_prophets`), dated and largely prose
with poetic laments/oracles. Structure: inaugural chariot-throne vision and call (1–3); judgment
on Judah/Jerusalem (4–24); oracles against the nations (25–32); restoration (33–39); the
visionary temple (40–48). Substrate adds `has_variant_reading` (ch22) — evidence only.

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- `has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` on the laments/oracles (18; 19; 21; 26–32). Dense `p` in the
  temple-vision measurements (40–48). `has_footnote` — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's
  evidence only.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- Chunked by **vision report, sign-act, allegory, and oracle unit**, with arcs (2:1–3:27 call;
  4:1–5:17 sign-acts; 8:1–11:25 temple-abominations vision; 26:1–28:19 Tyre; 29:1–32:32 Egypt;
  38:1–39:29 Gog; 40:1–42:20 temple measurements).

## Strong's / WJ handling
Strong's Hebrew = evidence only. Textual-variant flags = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- The **chariot-throne (merkabah)** vision (1) and the temple-abominations vision (8–11) flagged;
  the **king of Tyre** passage (28:11–19, read variously of the king or a fallen cherub) flagged;
  **individual responsibility** (18) and the **new heart** (36:26–27) flagged for theological
  weight; the **dry bones** (37) and **Gog/Magog** (38–39) flagged as restoration/apocalyptic
  material. All surfaced in sidecars, interpretation not decided by the boundary.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow vision/sign-act/allegory/oracle seams with many multi-chapter arcs — the
opposite of a flat chapter map. Evidence/strategy cite the substrate poetry and paragraph markers.
