# Philippians — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): a warm letter of joy and partnership, with the embedded **Christ hymn** (2:6–11).

## Markers / evidence
Prose (no `q` markers, though 2:6–11 is a hymn). `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **rhetorical unit**: greeting/thanksgiving (1:1–11); imprisonment and "to live is Christ"
(1:12–30); the call to humility with the **Christ hymn** (2:1–11); work-out-salvation and the envoys
(2:12–30); righteousness through faith and pressing on (3:1–21); rejoice/peace/contentment and
closing (4:1–23).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The **Christ hymn** (2:6–11, kenosis/christology — "being in the form of God… emptied himself") and
the righteousness-through-faith passage (3:7–11) flagged — surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the rhetorical units; the Christ hymn is isolated within its exhortation rather
than left as a bare chapter.
