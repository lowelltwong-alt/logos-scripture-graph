# Jude — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** a single-chapter
polemical letter (`genre_epistles`, 25 verses): contend for the faith against ungodly intruders,
citing extra-biblical traditions.

## Markers / evidence
Prose (`p`/`f`). `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Split into its **rhetorical units**: greeting (1:1–2, matching the pilot-gate reference span); the body
— the call to contend and the denunciation/typology of the false teachers (1:3–16); the closing
exhortation and doxology (1:17–25). A deliberate sub-chapter division.

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The body (1:3–16) is flagged for its **noncanonical citations** — the dispute over Moses' body (v9, the
Assumption of Moses) and the quotation of **1 Enoch** (vv14–15) — a canon/source-tradition crux;
surfaced as evidence, no canon decision made. The 2 Peter 2 parallel is a source-relationship review point.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
The single chapter is split into greeting/body/doxology, and the noncanonical-citation unit is isolated
and escalated rather than treated as one block.
