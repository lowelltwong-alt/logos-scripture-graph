# Ruth — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass, independent judgment)

## Primary literature type
Narrative novella (`genre_narrative`) in four acts, closing with a genealogy linking to David.
No poetry markers in the substrate.

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- `p` density marks dialogue turns (ch2 p=17). `has_footnote` on textual/name notes —
  evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's Hebrew **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- The four acts (return; gleaning; threshing floor; redemption) are genuine literary scenes
  that happen to align with the chapters — documented as scene units, not chapter fallback.
- ch4 is **split** into the gate-redemption scene (4:1–12) and the birth/genealogy coda
  (4:13–22), to isolate the Davidic genealogy as its own list unit rather than treat the
  chapter as one block.

## Strong's / WJ handling
Strong's Hebrew = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a (OT).

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- None of high risk; the genealogy coda (4:13–22) is the main review point (kinsman-redeemer
  and Davidic-line significance) but the boundary is clear.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries are act/scene-based and ch4 is split — a deliberate literary decision, with the
strategy documenting why each act is its own unit.
