# 1 John — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle/homily
(`genre_epistles`): a spiral of interwoven tests of life (belief, love, obedience), hard to divide.

## Markers / evidence
Prose. `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**. `has_footnote` — evidence only.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **thematic movement** (acknowledging the letter's spiral structure): prologue (1:1–4);
God is light / the advocate (1:5–2:2, arc); knowing God / love not the world (2:3–17); antichrists and
abiding (2:18–29); children of God / love one another (3); test the spirits (4:1–6); God is love /
faith overcomes (4:7–5:5, arc); the testimony and assurance (5:6–21).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The final unit (5:6–21) flagged for the **Comma Johanneum** (5:7–8, the trinitarian gloss absent from
the Greek manuscripts) and the assurance-of-life passage; surfaced as a textual variant, no decision made.
The spiral structure means most boundaries carry some uncertainty and are marked accordingly.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the thematic movements (several crossing chapters), not the chapters; the Comma
Johanneum unit is isolated and flagged as a textual crux.
