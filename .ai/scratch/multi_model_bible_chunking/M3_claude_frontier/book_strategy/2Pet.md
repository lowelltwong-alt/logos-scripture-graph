# 2 Peter — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle/testament
(`genre_epistles`): a warning against false teachers and scoffers, sharing material with Jude.

## Markers / evidence
Prose; `has_crossref`/`x` — evidence only. `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **argument unit**: confirm your calling and the certainty of prophecy (1); the denunciation
of false teachers (2, closely parallel to Jude); the day of the Lord and the new heavens and earth (3).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- ch1 flagged for "partakers of the divine nature" (1:4, theosis) and the inspiration statement
  (1:20–21, "no prophecy of Scripture… men spoke from God").
- ch2 flagged for its **literary relationship with Jude** (source-tradition question) — evidence only.
- ch3 flagged for Paul's letters being called "Scripture" (3:15–16) and the eschatology (3:8–13).
Surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Each chapter is a distinct argument unit here, and each is flagged with its specific theological or
source-critical review point rather than treated as a silent block.
