# 1 Peter — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): encouragement to suffering exiles, with a household code and OT-saturated exhortation.

## Markers / evidence
`has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` on OT citations (ch1–3). `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.
`has_crossref`/`x` — evidence only.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **rhetorical unit**: greeting and living hope (1:1–12); be holy / the living word (1:13–2:3,
arc); the living stone and chosen people (2:4–10); the household code — government, slaves, wives/husbands
(2:11–3:7, arc); suffering for righteousness and Christ preached to the spirits (3:8–22); living for God
amid suffering (4); elders, humility, and closing (5).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek and OT-citation markers are **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide
doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The **spirits in prison / Christ's proclamation** and "baptism now saves you" (3:18–22) — a notorious
exegetical and sacramental crux — flagged; surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the rhetorical units, several crossing chapters (the household code and the
holiness exhortation are arcs); the 3:18–22 crux is isolated.
