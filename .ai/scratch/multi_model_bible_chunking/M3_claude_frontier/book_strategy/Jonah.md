# Jonah — M3 frontier chunking strategy (pilot-fragile book)

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** prophetic
**narrative** (`genre_narrative`) with an embedded psalm (ch2). Jonah is a pilot-fragile book, so
each unit is flagged and surfaced to the sidecars.

## Markers / evidence
Prose narrative (1; 3; 4) with poetry (`has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` + q1/q2) only at ch2 (the
psalm from the fish). `has_footnote` — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's Hebrew **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **scene**: the flight and storm (1); the psalm of thanksgiving (2, isolated as poetry);
Nineveh's repentance (3); Jonah's anger and the plant (4). The four scenes are discrete and here
coincide with the chapters — documented as scene units, not chapter fallback.

## Strong's / WJ
Strong's = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence / frontier triggers
All four units flagged `medium_low` (pilot-fragile book): the psalm (2) for its prose/poetry seam,
and the sign-of-Jonah / repentance / divine-compassion themes (cf. Matt 12:39–41) surfaced for
review — never encoded in the boundary.

**Strategy** is scene-based with the psalm isolated; the chapter alignment is explained, not silent.
