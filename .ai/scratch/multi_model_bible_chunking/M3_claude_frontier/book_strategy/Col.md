# Colossians — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): a christological letter against a syncretistic "philosophy," with the embedded
**Christ hymn** (1:15–20).

## Markers / evidence
Prose; `has_variant_reading` at ch1 — evidence only. `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **rhetorical unit**: greeting/thanksgiving/prayer (1:1–14); the **supremacy-of-Christ
hymn** (1:15–23); Paul's ministry (1:24–2:5); freedom from human regulations / fullness in Christ
(2:6–23); the new life and household code (3:1–4:1, arc); closing exhortations and greetings (4:2–18).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The **Christ hymn** (1:15–20, "image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation" — a
historic Arian pressure point) and "in him the fullness of deity dwells bodily" (2:9) flagged —
surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the rhetorical units; the Christ hymn is isolated within the thanksgiving, and
several units cross chapters — not a chapter map.
