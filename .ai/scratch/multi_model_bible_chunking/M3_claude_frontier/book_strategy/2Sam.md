# 2 Samuel — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass, independent judgment)

## Primary literature type
Narrative (`genre_narrative`): David's reign, framed by poetry — the **Lament of the Bow**
(1:17–27), and the closing appendix's **Song of Deliverance** (22 = Ps 18) and **David's
last words** (23:1–7).

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- Poetry (`has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker`) at ch1 (q1=11/q2=18), ch22 (q1=50/q2=65/b=7 —
  the densest), ch23 (q1=8/q2=15). High `p` density on the court narrative (13, 14, 18, 19).
- `has_footnote` textual notes — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- ch1 **split**: the Amalekite's report (1:1–16) and the Lament of the Bow (1:17–27, poetry).
- The **Court/Succession Narrative** (9–20) chunked by scene (Bathsheba 11; Nathan 12;
  Amnon/Tamar 13; Absalom's revolt 15–19).
- The **appendix** (21–24) chunked into famine/giants (21), the Song (22), last words
  (23:1–7), mighty-men list (23:8–39), and the census (24).
- The **Davidic covenant** (7) kept whole and flagged for its messianic weight.

## Strong's / WJ handling
Strong's Hebrew = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a (OT).

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- The three poems (1:17–27; 22; 23:1–7) flagged for stanza review. The Davidic covenant (7)
  flagged for messianic/theological review — surfaced, not encoded in the boundary.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Scene and appendix-form drive boundaries; poems are isolated (ch1 and ch23 split). Evidence/
strategy cite the substrate poetry and paragraph markers.
