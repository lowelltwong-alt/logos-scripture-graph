# 1 Thessalonians — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): thanksgiving-heavy pastoral letter, ending with teaching on the Lord's coming.

## Markers / evidence
Prose. `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**. `has_footnote` — evidence only.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **rhetorical unit**: greeting/thanksgiving (1:1–10); Paul's ministry among them (2:1–16);
Timothy's mission and report (2:17–3:13, arc); living to please God (4:1–12); the coming of the Lord
and the dead in Christ (4:13–5:11, arc); final exhortations and closing (5:12–28).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek is **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine. `wj` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The **parousia** passage (4:13–5:11, "the dead in Christ will rise first… caught up together" — a
rapture/eschatology crux) flagged — surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the pastoral/rhetorical units; the Timothy-report and parousia units are arcs that
cross chapters rather than a chapter map.
