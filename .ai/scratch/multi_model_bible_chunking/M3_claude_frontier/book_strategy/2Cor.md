# 2 Corinthians — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): Paul's most personal letter — a defense of his apostolic ministry, the collection
appeal, and the "fool's speech."

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- `has_wj` at ch12 (12:9, "my grace is sufficient") — **evidence only**. `has_variant_reading` at ch4
  — evidence only. `has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` on OT citations (6; 9). `has_strong_g` — Strong's
  **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- Chunked by **rhetorical unit / argument arc**: greeting and comfort (1:1–11); the change of plans
  and forgiveness (1:12–2:13); the new-covenant ministry (2:14–4:6); hope amid affliction (4:7–5:10);
  reconciliation (5:11–6:2); marks of ministry (6:3–7:4); joy at repentance (7:5–16); the collection
  (8:1–9:15); the defense of authority (10:1–11:15); the fool's speech and the thorn (11:16–12:13);
  final warnings (12:14–13:10); the closing benediction (13:11–14).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
WJ (12:9) and Strong's Greek are **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide doctrine.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- The new-covenant/letter-and-Spirit passage (3:6), the ministry of reconciliation and "he made him
  to be sin" (5:21, imputation), the thorn/third-heaven (12:1–10), and the **Trinitarian benediction**
  (13:14) flagged — surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the letter's argument arcs, most crossing chapters; the benediction and the
theological cruxes are isolated.
