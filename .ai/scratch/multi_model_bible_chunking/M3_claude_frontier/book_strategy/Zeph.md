# Zephaniah — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** prophetic oracle
of the day of the LORD (`genre_prophets`); prose-set (no `q` markers).

## Markers / evidence
`p`/`f` only. `has_footnote` — evidence only. `has_strong_h` — Strong's Hebrew **evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **oracle unit**: the great day of the LORD / judgment on Judah (1); the call to
repentance and oracles against the nations (2); woe to Jerusalem followed by the promise of joy
and restoration (3).

## Strong's / WJ
Strong's = evidence only. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence / frontier triggers
Low overall risk; the day-of-the-LORD theme (1:14–18, echoed in later liturgy) noted as evidence.

**Strategy** is oracle-based; each unit's evidence names its oracle rather than the bare chapter.
