# 1 Timothy — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): a church-order letter with instructions on worship, offices, and conduct.

## Markers / evidence
Prose; `has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` at ch3 (the 3:16 christological hymn); `has_variant_reading` at
ch1. `has_wj` at ch5 (5:18 dominical saying) — evidence only. `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
Chunked by **topic unit**: charge and false teachers (1); prayer and worship, incl. women (2);
overseers/deacons and the godliness hymn (3); warnings and the good minister (4); widows, elders,
and instructions (5); slaves, contentment, and the final charge (6).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek and the 5:18 dominical citation are **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or
decide doctrine.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
The women-in-worship instructions (2:8–15, a heavily contested passage) and the **3:16 christological
hymn** ("He who was revealed in the flesh…", with the "God/who" textual variant) flagged — surfaced,
interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the church-order topics; the godliness hymn (3:16) is noted within its unit and the
contested passages are isolated and escalated.
