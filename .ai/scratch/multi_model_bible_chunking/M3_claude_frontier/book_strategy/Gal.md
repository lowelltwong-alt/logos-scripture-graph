# Galatians — M3 frontier chunking strategy

**Strategy id:** `literary_marker_aware_v2` (frontier pass). **Literature type:** epistle
(`genre_epistles`): a polemical defense of justification by faith apart from works of the law,
structured autobiography → argument → paraenesis.

## Local marker signals (Rust substrate)
- `has_poetry_or_liturgy_marker` on OT citations (ch4; ch3 with `x`=14 crossrefs) — evidence only.
  `has_strong_g` — Strong's **Greek evidence only**.

## Boundary handling (independent rationale)
- Chunked by **rhetorical unit**: greeting and "no other gospel" (1:1–10); the autobiographical
  defense and justification thesis (1:11–2:21, arc, incl. 2:16, 2:20); the scriptural argument (3,
  Abraham/curse/law); sons-and-heirs with the Hagar/Sarah allegory (4); freedom and the fruit of the
  Spirit (5); the closing paraenesis and "new creation" (6).

## Strong's / WJ handling (evidence only)
Strong's Greek and OT-citation markers are **evidence only**; never used to set a boundary or decide
doctrine. `wj_or_red_letter` n/a.

## Low-confidence & frontier escalation triggers
- **Justified by faith not works** (2:16), "crucified with Christ" (2:20), "Christ redeemed us from
  the curse" (3:13), "no Jew nor Greek… all one in Christ" (3:28), and "new creation" (6:15) flagged
  for justification/law-gospel pressure — surfaced, interpretation not decided.

## Why this is not silent chapter-only
Boundaries follow the argument's autobiography/argument/paraenesis units, several crossing chapters;
the justification cruxes are isolated.
