# Job — M6_fable5 book strategy (literary_marker_aware_v2)

- **Selected strategy:** speech-unit chunking. Prose frame (1-2; 42:7-17) as narrative units; the
  poetic dialogue chunked strictly by speaker turn (Job's lament; three Eliphaz-Bildad-Zophar
  cycles with Job's replies; the ch 28 wisdom hymn; Job's final defense 29-31; four Elihu speeches
  32-37; two Yahweh speeches with Job's two answers; retraction 42:1-6).
- **Literature type / mixed genre:** wisdom dialogue poetry inside a prose frame; every dialogue
  chapter is q1/q2-saturated with `b` stanza breaks; speaker-introduction formulae ("Then X
  answered...") are the primary seams.
- **Substrate markers considered:** `q1/q2` density and `b` breaks confirm poetry; prose `p`
  markers isolate the frame (1-2; 32:1-5; 42:7-17); speaker formulae carry the turn boundaries.
- **Strong's/Hebrew metadata:** evidence-only; Job's rare-word lexicon (many hapax) noted only as
  future original-language review pressure; `strong_or_hebrew_tags_used: false`.
- **Expected low-confidence regions:** third-cycle breakdown (25 is unusually short; 26-27 mix
  voices; 27:13-23 sounds like Zophar — classic attribution debate); ch 28 wisdom hymn (speaker
  and placement debated: Job's voice vs interlude); 40:3-5 and 42:1-6 short answers; speech units
  that coincide with whole chapters are marker-rich and take the protocol medium_low cap even
  though speaker-turn evidence is strong (rigidity noted for the gap register).
- **Chapter-only fallback:** not used — units are speaker turns; many simply span whole chapters.
- **Frontier/atlas expectations:** escalation rows for the third-cycle mix, the ch 28 hymn, and
  every capped chapter-coincident speech.
- **WJ/speaker concerns:** divine speeches (38-41) are speaker-marked units; no wj markers in OT.
- **Independent boundary rationale:** boundaries follow speaker-introduction formulae confirmed
  against substrate poetry/paragraph evidence; no template span or other model folder used.
