# Esth — M6_fable5 book strategy (literary_marker_aware_v2)

- **Selected strategy:** court-novella chunking by scene following the feast-structured plot
  (Vashti's feast through Purim's feasts), with the Purim institution (9:20-32) separated from the
  victory narrative.
- **Literature type / mixed genre:** diaspora court narrative; embedded decrees (3:12-15; 8:9-14);
  festival etiology (9:20-32); brief royal-chronicle epilogue (10, three verses).
- **Substrate markers considered:** paragraph `p` at scene seams; footnotes evidence-only; no
  poetry markers. **Canon note:** WEB canonical-66 Esther is the Hebrew text; Greek Additions are
  boundary material and are not present or imported (canon scope untouched).
- **Strong's/Hebrew metadata:** evidence-only; `strong_or_hebrew_tags_used: false`. The book's
  famous non-mention of God means no divine-name capitalization pressure.
- **Expected low-confidence regions:** 9:1-19 vs 9:20-32 seam (victory account vs institution);
  ch 10 three-verse epilogue (could attach to ch 9; kept separate as chronicle coda).
- **Chapter-only fallback:** not used; scenes coincide with chapters by design of the novella.
- **Frontier/atlas expectations:** escalation row for the ch 9/10 seam decisions.
- **Independent boundary rationale:** boundaries follow banquet/decree scene shifts anchored to
  substrate paragraphs; no template span or other model folder used.
