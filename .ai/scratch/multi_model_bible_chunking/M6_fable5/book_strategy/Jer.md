# Jer — M6_fable5 book strategy (literary_marker_aware_v2)

- **Selected strategy:** oracle/episode chunking in MT order: call (1), early poetic oracles
  (2-6), temple-sermon complex (7-10), covenant-lament cycle with the five confessions isolated
  or paired with their triggers (11-20), king/prophet oracles (21-24), cup of wrath (25),
  conflict narratives (26-29), Book of Consolation (30-33), siege and aftermath narratives
  (34-45), nations oracles (46-51) per nation, historical appendix (52).
- **Literature type / mixed genre:** prophetic poetry and prose sermons interleaved; sign-act
  narratives (13; 19; 27); confessions (personal laments); letter (29); land-purchase deed (32);
  new-covenant oracle (31:31-34 — high theology-pressure passage, evidence-only).
- **Substrate markers considered:** `q1/q2` poetry vs prose `p` shifts mark the poetry/sermon
  seams; `b` breaks inside oracle collections; `m` markers; footnotes evidence-only.
- **Strong's/Hebrew metadata:** evidence-only; `strong_or_hebrew_tags_used: false`.
- **Textual-variant pressure:** Jer MT vs LXX differ in length (~1/8 shorter) and in the
  placement of the nations oracles (after 25:13 in LXX vs 46-51 in MT) — the dossier queue lists
  this; ch 25 and the nations block are flagged medium_low with variant concerns.
- **Expected low-confidence regions:** poetry/prose seam placement in 2-10 (units bridge
  chapters); confession boundaries (11:18; 15:10; 17:14; 18:18; 20:7); ch 25 (LXX hinge); ch 49
  multi-nation oracle; 30-33 consolation internal seams.
- **Chapter-only fallback:** not used; oracle formulas ("the word that came to Jeremiah") carry
  the seams.
- **Frontier/atlas expectations:** rows for the variant-pressure blocks, confessions, and the
  new-covenant unit.
- **Independent boundary rationale:** boundaries follow word-event formulae, sign-act frames, and
  poetry/prose genre shifts anchored to substrate evidence; no template span or other model
  folder used.
