  the seven letters one chunk each (2-3), throne and Lamb (4; 5), seal cycle with its interlude
  (6; 7; 8:1-5), trumpet cycle with interludes (8:6-13; 9; 10; 11:1-14; 11:15-19), the cosmic

  embedded hymns and doxologies throughout (4:8,11; 5:9-13; 7:10-12 q1-marked; 11:17-18; 15:3-4

  beatitude chain (seven "blessed" sayings); dense OT allusion without citation formulae (Daniel,
  Ezekiel, Zechariah, Isaiah — the apocalyptic intertext dossier governs, evidence-only).

  true`**; escalation rows cover each vision cycle; Revelation remains research/prep-only under
  `REV-T344-E` for governed implementation, and this scratch map claims no implementation
  authority. Pilot-fragile: chapter-coincident chunks take the protocol medium_low cap.

  asserted; wj-bearing chunks set `wj_or_red_letter_considered: true`.
- **Substrate markers considered:** `p` seams; `q1/q2` on some hymns (5; 7; 15); `x` crossrefs;
  footnotes (textual notes at 1:8; 13:1,10; 22:14 tree/robes variant — evidence-only).
