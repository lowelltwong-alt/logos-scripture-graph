# Song — M6_fable5 book strategy (literary_marker_aware_v2)

- **Selected strategy:** six-poem lyric chunking bounded by the adjuration refrain ("do not stir
  up love": 2:7; 3:5; 8:4) and scene shifts: 1:1-2:7; 2:8-3:5; 3:6-5:1; 5:2-6:3; 6:4-8:4; 8:5-14.
- **Literature type / mixed genre:** lyric love poetry with dialogue. WEB carries explicit `sp`
  speaker markers (Beloved/Lover/Friends) — the only OT book here with machine-readable speaker
  boundaries; used as primary evidence alongside the refrains.
- **Substrate markers considered:** `sp` speaker markers; `q1/q2` density; `b` breaks; footnotes
  evidence-only.
- **Strong's/Hebrew metadata:** evidence-only (gender-marked Hebrew forms underlie the sp
  attributions but are not boundary authority); `strong_or_hebrew_tags_used: false`.
- **WJ/speaker concerns:** speaker identity in Song is editorially assigned (WEB sp headings are
  translation formatting, not ancient canonical boundaries per repo policy) — all chunks flagged
  medium_low with speaker_attribution concern per the extra-care book list.
- **Expected low-confidence regions:** every poem boundary — Song subdivision is the least stable
  in the canon (proposals range from 5 to 30+ poems); the 3:6-5:1 wedding sequence and the 8:5-14
  coda mosaic are most contested.
- **Chapter-only fallback:** not used; poem boundaries deliberately cross chapters (2:7 mid-ch,
  5:1 close).
- **Frontier/atlas expectations:** all six poems fed to the sidecars with speaker/genre concerns.
- **Independent boundary rationale:** refrain-closure plus sp-marker clustering define the six
  units; no template span or other model folder used.
