# Isa — M6_fable5 book strategy (literary_marker_aware_v2)

- **Selected strategy:** oracle-unit chunking across the canonical five movements: Zion oracles
  (1-12), nations oracles (13-23) per massa' superscription, Isaiah apocalypse (24-27), woe cycle
  (28-33), Edom/blossom diptych (34-35), Hezekiah narrative (36-39), comfort oracles (40-55) with
  the four servant songs isolated (42:1-9; 49:1-13; 50:4-11; 52:13-53:12), and restoration
  oracles (56-66).
- **Literature type / mixed genre:** prophetic poetry dominant (q1/q2 with b oracle breaks);
  narrative islands (6-8 memoir; 20 sign-act; 36-39 parallel to 2Kgs 18-20 — parallel-text
  pressure); vineyard song (5:1-7); hymns (12; 25; 26); communal lament (63:7-64:12).
- **Substrate markers considered:** `q1/q2` poetry, `b` breaks as oracle seams, `p` in narrative
  chapters, massa'/hoy openings as formula seams; footnotes evidence-only.
- **Strong's/Hebrew metadata:** evidence-only. 7:14 almah and other pressure lexemes are known
  non-orthodox-pressure passages (per the pressure dossier queue) — noted as evidence, no
  theology authority claimed.
- **Expected low-confidence regions:** 9:8-10:4 refrain unit crossing the chapter break; the 2:6
  and 3:1-4:1 seams; nations-oracle internal joins (14:24-32; 21 triple oracle); servant-song
  frame edges (whether 42:5-9 belongs to the song); 56:9-57:21 unit; chapter-coincident poetic
  oracles in 24-35 and 40-66 take the protocol medium_low cap.
- **Chapter-only fallback:** not used; where oracle units coincide with chapters (esp. 40-66) the
  boundary follows the oracle opening formula, logged via the cap.
- **Frontier/atlas expectations:** rows for servant songs (messianic/theology pressure), 7:14
  Immanuel unit, the refrain unit, and all capped oracle chapters.
- **Independent boundary rationale:** boundaries follow oracle superscriptions (massa', "in that
  day", hoy-woes, "comfort" imperatives) and refrain closures anchored to substrate poetry/break
  evidence; no template span or other model folder used.
