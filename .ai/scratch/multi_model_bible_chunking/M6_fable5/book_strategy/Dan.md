# Dan — M6_fable5 book strategy (literary_marker_aware_v2)

- **Selected strategy:** two-panel chunking honoring the book's double structure: court tales
  (1-6) one per tale; apocalyptic visions (7-12) one per dated vision, with the final vision
  (10-12) split at its three movements (angelic preparation, kings of north and south, sealed
  end) and flagged as one continuous unit.
- **Literature type / mixed genre:** court narrative + apocalyptic vision reports; embedded
  doxology poems (2:20-23; 4:3,34-35; 6:26-27; 7:9-10,13-14 q1/q2 and pi1 markers); prayer of
  confession (9:3-19); **language structure:** Hebrew 1:1-2:4a, Aramaic 2:4b-7:28, Hebrew 8-12 —
  the Aramaic chiasm (2||7, 3||6, 4||5) crosses the tales/visions genre seam; the substrate does
  not expose the language shift (gap-register item).
- **Frontier handling:** Dan is a frontier book — every chunk sets `frontier_flag_considered:
  true`; multiple frontier-queue rows are written; apocalyptic symbols (four kingdoms, son of
  man, seventy weeks) are maximal theology/eschatology-pressure content kept evidence-only per
  the apocalyptic intertext dossier queue.
- **Substrate markers considered:** `p` seams; `q1/q2/pi1` isolate doxologies and vision poetry;
  `m` at 5:26-27 (MENE TEKEL); footnotes evidence-only.
- **Strong's/Hebrew/Aramaic metadata:** evidence-only; Aramaic lexemes carry Hebrew-style Strong's
  tags in the substrate; never boundary authority; `strong_or_hebrew_tags_used: false`. **Canon note:** WEB canonical-66 Daniel is
  the Hebrew-Aramaic book; Greek additions are boundary material, not imported.
- **Expected low-confidence regions:** all chapter-coincident units in a pilot-fragile book (protocol
  cap); 9:24-27 seventy weeks (heaviest interpretive-pressure span); 10-12 partition of the single
  final vision; 2:4 language seam inside the ch 2 tale.
- **Chapter-only fallback:** not used; tale/vision units genuinely coincide with chapters.
- **Independent boundary rationale:** boundaries follow tale openings and vision date formulae
  (7:1; 8:1; 9:1; 10:1) anchored to substrate evidence; no template span or other model folder
  used.
