  and plague-cycle unit; Sinai law (19-24) by legal-code subsection; tabernacle instruction (25-31)
  and construction (35-40) by artifact/report unit; golden-calf crisis (32-34) by scene.
- **Literature type / mixed genre:** narrative with embedded law codes (Decalogue 20:1-17,

  Sea 15:1-18 + Miriam 15:21, q1x20/q2x28 in substrate), and a genealogy insertion (6:14-27).

  `q1/q2` isolate the ch 15 poem; footnotes `f` (translation/measure notes) treated as evidence
  only. No `wj`, no `s` headings in WEB.
- **Strong's/Hebrew metadata:** evidence-only; no Strong's number used as boundary authority;
  `strong_or_hebrew_tags_used: false` throughout.
- **Expected low-confidence regions:** 6:14-27 genealogy interrupting the call narrative (resumption
  seam at 6:28 debated); Covenant Code internal subdivision (20:22-23:33 has no single traditional
  partition; my six-cut division follows topic seams at substrate paragraphs); 12:43-13:16 mixed

- **Chapter-only fallback:** not used; chapters that appear whole (16; 18; 19; 24; 26-29; 33; 34;
  40) are deliberate literary units (manna cycle, Jethro visit, theophany, ratification, artifact
  panels) whose seams genuinely coincide with chapter edges; none are marker-rich per protocol keys.
- **Frontier/atlas expectations:** escalation rows for the genealogy insertion, Covenant Code
  seams, and the 12:43-13:16 bridge unit; no frontier-book flags.
- **Independent boundary rationale:** boundaries follow plague-cycle formulae ("Yahweh said to
  Moses, Go in to Pharaoh"), law-code topic shifts, and tabernacle artifact lists, each anchored to
  substrate paragraph evidence; no other model folder consulted; template examples not used.
