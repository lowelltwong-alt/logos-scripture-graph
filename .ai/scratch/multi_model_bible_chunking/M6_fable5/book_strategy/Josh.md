# Josh — M6_fable5 book strategy (literary_marker_aware_v2)

- **Selected strategy:** two-panel composition: conquest narratives (1-12) by campaign episode;
  land allotment lists (13-21) per tribal grant; covenant epilogue (22-24) by scene.
- **Literature type / mixed genre:** conquest narrative with embedded ceremony scripts (5:1-12;
  8:30-35), a defeated-kings list (12, m-markers), boundary/town lists (15-19 with m-marker town
  rosters), refuge/Levite-city lists (20-21), and covenant speeches (23-24).
- **Substrate markers considered:** paragraph `p` for episode seams; `m` markers isolate list
  bodies (12:8-24; 15:32-60; 19 rosters); footnotes evidence-only. Land-allotment and list
  material is a protocol escalation trigger, so those chunks carry sidecar rows.
- **Strong's/Hebrew metadata:** evidence-only; `strong_or_hebrew_tags_used: false`.
- **Expected low-confidence regions:** 3-4 crossing/memorial seam (4:1 cut is conventional but the
  two chapters interleave); allotment list internals (15; 19; 21) where district seams could split;
  10:28-43 southern-sweep formulaic conquest list.
- **Chapter-only fallback:** not used; whole-chapter units are genuine episode/grant units.
- **Frontier/atlas expectations:** escalation rows for allotment lists and the crossing seam.
- **Independent boundary rationale:** boundaries follow campaign itinerary notices, tribal-grant
  headings ("the lot came to..."), and ceremony frames anchored to substrate paragraph/list
  evidence; no template span or other model folder used.
