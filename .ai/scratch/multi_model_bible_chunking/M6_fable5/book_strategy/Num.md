  oracle cycle; Moab-plains preparation (26-36) by census/law/allotment unit.

  evidence-only. Census/list/genealogy material is a protocol escalation trigger, so list-heavy
  chunks carry sidecar rows.
- **Strong's/Hebrew metadata:** evidence-only; never boundary authority;
  `strong_or_hebrew_tags_used: false`.
- **Expected low-confidence regions:** ch 15 mixed offering-law + sabbath-gatherer narrative +

  oracle chapters where whole-chapter poetic units are chapter-coincident and marker-rich

- **Chapter-only fallback:** not used; chapter-coincident units are deliberate episode/list units;

- **Frontier/atlas expectations:** escalation rows for oracle poetry, list/census units, and the
