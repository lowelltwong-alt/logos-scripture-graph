# M7 Sol independent whole-Bible chunking campaign

Status: foreground, resumable, non-authorizing candidate production. The machine-readable controller specification is `campaign.json`; it validates as `long_run_campaign.v2` and does not authorize unattended execution.

## Objective

Produce Sol's independent literary chunk candidate for all 66 books without reading M1–M6 maps or comparison outputs. Read each book directly, write its strategy before its chunks, preserve larger coherent forms, and complete a challenge mesh for every frozen decision.

## Per-book gate

1. Read the whole canonical book plus allowed evidence surfaces.
2. Write `book_strategy/<Book>.md`.
3. Freeze contiguous chunks with exact canonical verse coverage and positive contiguous integer indices.
4. Run two artifact-blind, role-separated primary reviews: literary/form and original-language/translation/canonical context.
5. Run a distinct peer crosscheck over the frozen reviews.
6. Sol answers every challenge; a Sol/high boss freezes a provisional ruling before reading peer/premortem output, then records any evidence-driven change.
7. Return the ruling to the primary reviewers. Any reviewer may append a reasoned appeal.
8. Run a role-separated post-resolution check with explicit shared-substrate disclosure.
9. Validate exact coverage, official whole-Bible map schema, 1:1 review and final-status parity, typed relations, sidecars, current hashes, and the repository literary-quality gate.
10. Write the completion receipt last, then rerun receipt and manifest consistency.

The intra-model mesh is one correlated model voice. Later cross-model convergence or human review supplies actual external independence.

## Dissent rule

Consensus is never forced. An appeal records the passage context, disputed claim, evidence, uncertainty, response to the boss ruling, and requested next reviewer. Unresolved dissent results in the larger coherent candidate at `low` or `medium_low` confidence and `deferred_human_or_external_ai`; it is not silently declared resolved.

## Language and context rule

Hebrew, Aramaic, and Koine Greek are evaluated from phrase to clause, syntax, discourse, book, and canonical context. Roots, lemmas, Strong's identifiers, English paragraphing, headings, and cross-references are evidence only. Second Temple and later rabbinic evidence are dated and kept in their historical layers; neither supplies boundary authority.

## Stop conditions

Stop the affected book on prohibited cross-model exposure, source or policy pin drift, non-contiguous/gapped coverage, stale review digests, overlapping writes, missing review parity, a second material revision cycle, or any proposed theological/preferred-reading/source-tradition decision. Preserve the packet and route the stop rather than forcing completion.

## Current pilot

Genesis is the first book. Its 80-decision frozen draft covers all 1,533 canonical verses in exact order and is in blind primary review.
