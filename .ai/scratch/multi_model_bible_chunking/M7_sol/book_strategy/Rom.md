# Romans candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace all sixteen chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB Romans coordinate set. Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, audience identity, doctrinal system, law/gospel scheme, supersessionism, election system, ethics, gender/office ruling, chronology, preferred witness, or preferred reading.

## faithful_route

Trace sender/recipient greeting and thesis/thanksgiving; accusation and accountability movements; diatribe objections and scriptural catena; Abraham example and promise argument; comparison and reign sequences; rhetorical questions, baptism analogy and slavery analogy; law analogy, command/sin argument and divided-voice passage; life/Spirit/suffering/hope/intercession/assurance movement; Israel argument through sorrow, examples, objections, potter/citations, pursuit, proclamation and remnant; mercy/branches/doxology close; body/renewed-mind exhortation, gifts and love list; governing/neighbour-love and time exhortations; weak/strong dispute, conscience, stumbling, mutual reception and Christ/citation close; ministry rationale, travel plan and collection; commendation/greetings, warning and doxology received coordinates. Prefer the larger coherent argument, objection-response, analogy, catena, exhortation cluster, paraenetic movement, travel-plan unit, greeting list, warning, or doxology.

Text-critical awareness includes major papyri/uncials, Byzantine/TR/critical-text differences and versional/patristic evidence without selecting a witness. Hot zones include Rom.1.7, 1.16, 3.22/25/28, 4.1, 5.1, 5.12, 7.6, 7.14-25, 8.1-2, 8.28, 9.5, 9.28, 10.5, 11.6, 11.31, 12.11, 13.1, 14.4, 14.10/19, 14.23-15.1, 15.7-13, 15.24/29/31, 16.1-2, 16.7, 16.20, 16.24, 16.25-27 and doxology placement.
