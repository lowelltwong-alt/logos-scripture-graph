## Selected strategy

Read Joshua as a narrated succession-and-land sequence combining divine and human commissions, espionage and battle episodes, ritual crossings and memorials, etiological notices, campaign summaries, geographical boundary registers, city and personnel lists, legal-administrative procedures, a tribal dispute, farewell addresses, covenant renewal, and burial notices. Chapter numbering is never boundary evidence. Prefer complete scenes and larger functional registers; split only where speaker, scene, governing list function, procedure, embedded speech/poetry, or summary/closure demonstrably changes.

## Macro reading frames — not automatic chunks

1. Josh.1.1-5.15: succession commission, spies/Rahab, Jordan crossing and memorial, covenant preparation, Passover/produce, and commander encounter.
2. Josh.6.1-12.24: Jericho/Ai, Ebal ceremony, Gibeonite treaty, southern and northern campaigns, and conquest summaries/registers.
3. Josh.13.1-21.45: remaining-land commission, Transjordan recap, Judah/Joseph/remaining-tribe allotments, refuge cities, Levitical cities, and summary.
4. Josh.22.1-24.33: eastern-tribe release and altar dispute, Joshua's farewell, Shechem covenant renewal, and burial epilogue.
