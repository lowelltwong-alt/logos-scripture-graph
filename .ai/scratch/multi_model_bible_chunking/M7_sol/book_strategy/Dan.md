# Daniel candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. This strategy replaces the twelve chapter-sized scaffolds with structural movements derived from the received 66-book coordinate scope. It decides no theology, canon, authorship, date, court/empire identification, historical reconstruction, ethics, miracle claim, prophecy fulfillment, chronology, resurrection doctrine, eschatology, preferred witness, or preferred translation.

## faithful_route

Trace court-scene changes, royal decrees, accusations, tests and reversals; dream/vision report plus interpretation; hymnic/prayer responses; messenger arrival; language shifts; first-person/third-person transitions; dated vision superscriptions; symbolic sequences; angelic dialogue; question-answer closures; and the continuous final revelation. Prefer complete tales, dream/interpretation pairs, prayer-response movements, and vision-dialogue parents while recognizing genuine internal scenes.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

- The received Hebrew-to-Aramaic transition at 2:4b and return to Hebrew after chapter 7 are major form evidence, not automatic authorship/source/date boundaries.
- Hebrew and Imperial Aramaic morphology, court titles, names, loanwords, repeated decree/accusation formulas, bar-enash language, maskilim, tamid, shiqqus/shomem expressions, sevens/weeks, time formulas, qere/ketiv, rare syntax, and wordplay are evidence only.
- MT, Old Greek, Theodotion, Qumran Daniel fragments, Syriac, Vulgate, and other versions show wording, order, length, numbering, and addition/omission pressure. No witness or reading is preferred.
- Greek additions associated with Daniel are reception/textual evidence but are outside this campaign's Protestant 66-book coordinates; this scope statement makes no canon judgment and imports no extra verses.
- Roots are not meanings. Strong's identifiers are lexical locators only and never boundary authority.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

Retain macro parents for the chapter 1 court-entry trial; chapter 2 dream crisis/report/interpretation/outcome; chapter 3 image-furnace-decree tale; chapter 4 royal proclamation/dream/interpretation/fulfillment-restoration; chapter 5 feast-writing-interpretation-fall; chapter 6 conspiracy-prayer-den-deliverance-decree; chapter 7 beasts/court/human-like figure vision and interpretation; chapter 8 ram-goat vision and interpretation; chapter 9 prayer and messenger response; and chapters 10-12 as one final revelation with encounter, conflict disclosure, culmination, and closing dialogue. Disputed seams collapse to the full parent and remain LOW.

## list_register_function_check

Court official lists, instrument lists, empire/symbol sequences, accusations, gifts, animal horns, kings, wars, time periods, allotments, and angelic disclosures are cumulative narrative, liturgical, symbolic, or visionary registers. Do not split every instrument, official, kingdom, horn, ruler, campaign, number, or prediction into an independent chunk.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable: Daniel consists of court tales, prayers, dreams, visions, and dialogues, not an epistle. No salutation, thanksgiving, paraenesis, travel plan, doxology, or benediction template may be imposed.

WEB coordinates, OSHB/UXLC morphology, translation metadata, headings, chapter/verse numbering, manuscript labels, Greek-addition metadata, Qumran provenance, rabbinic/Jewish reception, patristic reception, and internal Bible relations are navigation/corroboration only. ## over_split_risk_check

Highest risks are chapter fallback; detaching dream/vision from interpretation; atomizing court-instrument or kingdom lists; proof-texting 2:44, 3:17-18, 6:10, 7:13-14, 9:24-27, 11:36, or 12:2-3; forcing identities for the human-like figure, horns, kings, princes, Michael, or messengers; calculating seventy weeks or time-times-half; selecting Old Greek/Theodotion/MT order or numbering; importing Greek additions; and splitting the continuous 10-12 revelation by chapter alone. Prefer larger units, exact alternatives, LOW confidence, and deferred review.
