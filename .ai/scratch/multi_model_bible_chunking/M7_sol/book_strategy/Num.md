## Selected strategy

Read Numbers as a mixed-form whole whose narrative travel movement contains censuses,
camp and Levitical registers, legal and ritual procedures, speeches, complaint and
rebellion scenes, itinerary formulae, embedded songs, poetic oracles, battle reports,
inheritance cases, and book-closing land administration. Prefer the largest coherent
biblical unit; later children are permitted only where the text changes tribe, clan,
day, offering, location, speaker, scene, procedure, law function, poem/oracle, or
argument movement. This is a strategy and risk forecast, not a proposed boundary map.

## Literature type or mixed genre

Primary prior: narrative pericope. Required secondary forms: legal covenant, ritual
procedure, census/register, wilderness itinerary, attributed speech, poetic blessing,
embedded song/quotation, prophetic oracle, battle report, negotiation, land boundary,
and colophon. The substrate's `genre_narrative` flag is only a prior and cannot flatten
these locally evidenced forms.

## Macro reading frames — not chunk proposals

1. `Num.1.1-Num.10.10`: Sinai camp constituted through census, camp order,
   Levitical service, camp laws, dedication, Passover, cloud, and trumpets.
2. `Num.10.11-Num.22.1`: Sinai-to-Moab journey with travel, complaint, rebellion,
   supplemental law, priestly confirmation, deaths, battles, and embedded poems.
3. `Num.22.2-Num.25.18`: Balak/Balaam narrative-oracle cycle and Peor crisis.
   An alternate defensible frame keeps this inside the larger journey-to-Moab unit.
4. `Num.26.1-Num.36.13`: new-generation ordering in Moab through census,
   inheritance/succession, calendar, vows, war, settlement, itinerary, land, refuge,
   and the Zelophehad resolution.

The two censuses (`Num.1`, `Num.26`) frame the wilderness generations. The distant
Zelophehad passages (`Num.26.33`, `Num.27.1-11`, `Num.36.1-12`) require relation/context
records, not a noncontiguous chunk. `Num.13-14` is explicitly recalled in
`Num.32.6-15`. These observations do not authorize boundaries or graph edges.
