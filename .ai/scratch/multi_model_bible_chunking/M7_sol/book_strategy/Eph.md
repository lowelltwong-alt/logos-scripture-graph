# Ephesians candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace all six chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB Ephesians coordinate set (155 verses). Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, audience, circular-letter/addressee reconstruction, apostolic authority, election/predestination doctrine, ecclesiology, household-role authority, spiritual-warfare doctrine, ethics, history, preferred witness, reading, punctuation, or speaker.

## faithful_route

Trace prescript; extended blessing/eulogy; thanksgiving and illumination prayer; former condition and grace/new-life contrast; Gentile alienation and reconciliation/household-temple movement; stewardship/mystery report and intercessory prayer; unity exhortation and one-body confession; gifts/body growth movement; old/new walk contrast; truth, anger, labor, speech, forgiveness and imitation paraenesis; darkness/light and wisdom-filled-life exhortation; household address; strength/armor exhortation; Tychicus notice, peace/grace benediction. Prefer complete eulogy, prayer, contrast/inference, reconciliation-temple movement, stewardship/prayer, unity/body argument, old/new paraenesis, light/wisdom movement, household address, armor exhortation or epistolary close.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

Koine prescript/eulogy/prayer formulas, long periodic syntax, en Christo/anaphora, participial chains, hina clauses, gar/oun/de/alla, men-de, quotation formulas, inferential dio, vocatives, imperatives, vice/virtue contrasts, household-address forms, armor metaphor and benediction formulas are evidence only. Hot zones include Eph.1.1, 1.3-14, 1.15, 1.18, 1.22-23, 2.1, 2.5, 2.8, 2.15, 3.1, 3.9, 3.14, 4.8-10, 4.9, 4.26, 5.2, 5.14, 5.21-22, 5.32, 6.5, 6.9, 6.21 and 6.24. No witness, reading, punctuation, addressee, quotation source/speaker, household policy, cosmology, or composition is selected. Roots, etymology, Strong-style tags, morphology and headings are not authority.

Prefer larger units, alternatives, LOW/deferred.
