# Hebrews independent literary-chunking strategy — M7_sol

## Scope and authority

Replace thirteen chapter-frame fallbacks with a candidate-only literary map independent of M1–M6, `comparison/`, and T417. Segment exordium, citation catena, exposition, warning/exhortation, comparison, scriptural quotation, cultic/tabernacle description, promise/oath argument, example catalogue, paraenesis, benediction, and epistolary close. Do not decide authorship, date, audience history, chronology, priesthood/covenant/sacrifice doctrine, supersession, canon, textual witness, fulfillment, or theology. Original-language and internal-Bible evidence locate discourse only. All difficult routes LOW and deferred.

## Internal-Bible and ancient-context control

Direct source relations to Genesis, Exodus–Deuteronomy, Psalms, Jeremiah, Habakkuk, Haggai and other cited/alluded passages must be recorded as evidence-only decision relations. Greek/Hebrew textual differences, Second Temple interpretive patterns, ancient cult/letter context, and later Jewish/patristic reception may explain form or semantic pressure but cannot select a witness, harmonize, decide fulfillment, priesthood/covenant/sacrifice doctrine, supersession, canon, authorship, or theology.
