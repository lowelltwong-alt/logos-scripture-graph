# Nahum corrective literary strategy (T556)

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. This strategy governs the corrective rereview of Nahum under Lowell Wong's literary-only completion ruling. It may identify literary units and preserve retrieval context; it may not decide theology, canon, authorship, date, historical reconstruction, divine-violence ethics, national identity, fulfillment, textual-witness preference, or promotion. Agreement inside the M7 mesh is evidence, never authority.

## Inventory and coordinate guard

- WEB inventory: 47 verses, Nah.1.1 through Nah.3.19, each exactly once and in order.
- WEB chapter counts: 15, 13, 19. WLC-family chapter counts: 14, 14, 19.
- Required crosswalk: WEB Nah.1.1-1.14 = WLC Nah.1.1-1.14; WEB Nah.1.15 = WLC Nah.2.1; WEB Nah.2.1-2.13 = WLC Nah.2.2-2.14; WEB Nah.3.1-3.19 = WLC Nah.3.1-3.19.
- OSHB and UXLC are correlated WLC-family views, not independent witnesses. Coordinates, paragraph marks, accents, morphology, lemmas, and roots are evidence only and never boundary authority.

## Literary-form routing

Read the book as a compact prophetic sequence whose local forms must stay recoverable inside their larger rhetorical movements:

1. superscription and vision heading;
2. divine-name/attribute and storm-theophany hymn;
3. refuge-versus-overwhelming-judgment antithesis;
4. plotted-counsel challenge and total-end declaration;
5. alternating deliverance and hostile-name decrees;
6. mountain good-news and feast summons;
7. siege-preparation imperatives with restoration rationale;
8. kinetic attack, breached-city collapse, flight, plunder, and desolation tableau;
9. lion-den taunt answered by a divine-warrior verdict;
10. bloody-city woe, battle-sound catalogue, and stated cause;
11. exposure-and-abandonment oracle;
12. No-Amon comparison and matching captivity inference;
13. fortress, gate, fire, and futile-defense taunts;
14. merchant/official locust swarm taunt;
15. closing address to the Assyrian king: sleeping shepherds, scattered people, incurable wound, and the hearers' clap.

These are form hypotheses for blind review, not a pre-authorized route. Prefer the larger coherent unit when a possible child cannot stand without its speech, comparison, cause, verdict, or mandatory parent.

## Deciding markers and hard seams

- Nah.1.1 is a recoverable heading; the repeated divine name and avenging attributes begin at 1:2.
- Nah.1.2-6 accumulates attributes, cosmic effects, and two endurance questions. Test 1:3b and 1:6 as internal stanza pressures without atomizing the theophany.
- Nah.1.7-8 is a compact goodness/refuge versus overflowing-end antithesis. Test a 1:6|1:7 seam and a 1:8|1:9 seam; do not isolate 1:7 as a detached maxim.
- Nah.1.9-11 moves from rhetorical challenge and total-end assurance to the wicked counselor emerging from the addressed city. WLC samekh after 1:11 is real closure evidence.
- Nah.1.12-13 and 1:14 contain addressee and decree changes. Test separate deliverance and hostile-name units, but preserve 1:9-15 as a mandatory address/decree parent if speaker or pronoun scope remains unstable.
- Nah.1.15 is WEB's good-news/feast verse and WLC Nah.2.1. Preserve it as a full proclamation and never treat the chapter-number difference as a literary decision.
- Nah.2.1-2 couples siege imperatives with the restoration rationale. Test but do not presume a child seam at 2:1|2 because the causal `for` may bind them.
- Nah.2.3-10 is a single accelerating siege-collapse tableau. Test dependent scene children at 2:3-7 and 2:8-10 only if attack, breach, flight, plunder, and desolation remain obligatorily hydrated together.
- Nah.2.11-13 carries the lion-den rhetorical taunt through a first-person divine verdict. Test 2:12|13 as an internal answer seam, not an automatic surface split. WLC samekh after WEB 2:13/WLC 2:14 closes the movement.
- Nah.3.1-4 binds woe, battle noises, bodies, and the stated `because` accusation. Do not detach the cause in 3:4.
- Nah.3.5-7 is a complete exposure, spectacle, flight, and no-comforter oracle marked by `Behold, I am against you`.
- Nah.3.8-11 compares No-Amon's defenses, allies, and captivity before applying `you also`; keep comparison and inference together.
- Nah.3.12-15 turns from ripe-fig fortresses and open gates to futile preparation and matching fire/sword consequence. Test 3:13|14 and 3:15|16 only with the larger defense-futility parent intact.
- Nah.3.16-17 is a merchant/guard/official locust taunt whose disappearance image should not be atomized.
- Nah.3.18-19 is the closing royal lament and universal-response coda; retain both verses together.

## Original-language and translation hot zones

- Possible alphabetic/acrostic pressures in 1:2-10 are evidence for stanza testing only. Do not reconstruct missing letters, emend the text, or let a proposed acrostic override the extant rhetoric.
- Rare forms, soundplay, rapid participles, qinah/taunt cadence, feminine city address, imperatives, vocatives, rhetorical questions, and paragraph marks may support seams but do not decide them alone.
- WLC samekh after 1:11, pe after 1:14, and samekh after WLC 2:14/WEB 2:13 are strong closure evidence, not authority over speaker scope or surface retrieval.
- Pronoun/addressee ambiguity in 1:9-14, the WEB 1:15/WLC 2:1 seam, the difficult wording around WEB 2:7, No-Amon in 3:8, and the locust/official wording in 3:15-17 require explicit counterevidence and confidence calibration.
- Roots are not meanings. Strong-number, lemma, morphology, accent, or English gloss metadata must never be promoted into lexical or theological authority.
- No local primary LXX, DSS, Targum, rabbinic, ANE, or Second-Temple claim may be made unless a qualified, passage-pinned source packet is actually available. Otherwise record the evidence gap.

## Whole-Bible relation guard

Internal relations to Exodus/Sinai storm language, Isaiah's good-news wording, Jonah's Nineveh narrative, Zephaniah's Nineveh oracle, or other biblical reuse may be recorded only as later/parallel canonical context. They may not decide authorship, direction of dependence, historical identity, fulfillment, theology, or a boundary by themselves. Let the immediate form lead; use the whole Bible to protect context rather than flatten local rhetoric.

## Parent hydration and proof-text premortem

Expected mandatory parents to test and record are 1:1-15, 1:9-15, 2:1-13, 2:3-10, 2:11-13, 3:1-7, 3:8-15, and 3:16-19. In particular:

- never surface 1:7 without the 1:2-8 theophany/refuge/end movement;
- never surface 1:15 without the 1:9-15 address/decree/good-news parent and the WEB-WLC crosswalk;
- never surface 2:2 without the 2:1-2 siege/restoration movement;
- never surface battle imagery in 3:2-3 without the woe and causal accusation in 3:1-4;
- never surface No-Amon's fall without the 3:8-11 comparison-and-inference unit;
- never surface 3:19 as a detached celebration apart from the 3:18-19 royal lament and the book's violence/ethics caution.

## Review and hold discipline

For every proposed decision, three blind roles—Hebrew/textual/versification, prophetic literary form, and canonical retrieval premortem—must supply decision-specific support, counterevidence, and source references. Each scoped challenge receives an author response; a fresh boss rules without forcing consensus; every specialist gets a post-ruling appeal opportunity; all losing alternatives remain append-only.

Accept a boundary when form and seam evidence support it. Hold only a genuinely contested retrieval choice with a specific, answerable human-or-external-AI question. Speaker ambiguity, versification, acrostic reconstruction, rare wording, or later reuse lowers confidence only when it materially changes the surface boundary. Do not defer the whole book by default.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

The accepted route deliberately retains the larger coherent units at 1:2-8, 1:12-14, 2:3-10, and 3:12-17 after blind-role disagreement. Mandatory parents are 1:1-15, 1:2-8, 1:9-15, 1:15-2:2, 2:1-13, 3:1-7, and 3:8-19. Internal child evidence remains append-only and cannot be surfaced without its parent.

## list_register_function_check

Nahum's divine attributes, cosmic effects, weapons, chariots, palace-collapse actions, lion imagery, battle sounds, allies, fortresses, siege preparations, merchants, guards, officials, and locust terms function as cumulative hymn, tableau, taunt, comparison, or lament registers. Preserve each register's rhetorical result; never turn list members or lexical roots into independent chunks or total meanings.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable. Nahum is prophetic hymn, oracle, siege tableau, taunt, comparison, and lament rather than an epistle. Argument-movement rules for letters do not override its poetic and prophetic forms.

## over_split_risk_check

For tied evidence, keep the larger surface, lower confidence when needed, and preserve the child seam as dissent rather than a default hold.
