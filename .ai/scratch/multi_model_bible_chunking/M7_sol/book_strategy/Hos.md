# Hosea candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. This strategy replaces the fourteen chapter-sized scaffolds with literary movements derived from the received 66-book coordinate scope. It decides no theology, canon, authorship, date, compositional layer, historical reconstruction, marital or sexual ethics, prophetic enactment, divine emotion, covenant doctrine, monarchy judgment, restoration chronology, fulfillment, preferred witness, or preferred translation.

## faithful_route

Trace superscription; enacted-sign narrative and naming speeches; accusation, summons, lawsuit, lament, warning, recollection, appeal, judgment, restoration, promise, and wisdom-colophon forms; speaker/addressee shifts; imperatives and vocatives; messenger formulas; question chains; agricultural, familial, marital, animal, warfare, and healing imagery; refrain-like returns; inclusio; and clear discourse resets. Prefer complete accusation-to-consequence, recollection-to-appeal, and judgment-to-restoration movements while retaining genuine internal stanzas.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

- Hebrew prophetic formulae, vocatives, imperatives, rhetorical questions, pronoun/addressee changes, finite-verb chains, clause particles, repeated roots, sound patterns, parallelism, and lexical fields are evidence only.
- The Hosea 1-2 / 2-3 versification offset in many English traditions is a coordinate hazard; this campaign follows its local canonical verse inventory and records alternate numbering without importing or deleting material.
- Difficult Hebrew in passages such as 4:4-5, 5:1-2, 6:5, 6:7, 7:4-7, 8:10, 9:6, 10:10, 11:3-4, 11:12-12:1, 12:7-8, 13:1, and 13:14 creates translation and seam pressure. Ambiguity lowers confidence rather than authorizing emendation.
- MT, Septuagint, Dead Sea evidence where extant, Syriac, Vulgate, Targum, and other versions may preserve wording, segmentation, order, or number differences. No witness or reading is preferred.
- Roots are not meanings. Strong's identifiers and morphology are lexical/navigation aids only, never boundary authority.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

Retain macro parents for 1:1-2:1 enacted signs and naming reversals; 2:2-23 marital/lawsuit-restoration discourse; chapter 3 enacted restoration sign; 4:1-5:7 lawsuit and leadership accusation; 5:8-7:16 alarm, failed return, and political/ethical indictment; chapters 8-10 sowing/reaping and cult-monarchy judgment movements; 11:1-11 parental recollection and compassion; 11:12-12:14 ancestor/prophet recollection and indictment; 13:1-14:1 death/judgment lament; and 14:2-9 return, healing, flourishing, and wisdom close. Disputed seams collapse to the larger parent and remain LOW.

## list_register_function_check

Names, places, rulers, priests, tribes, agricultural products, animals, weapons, foreign powers, cult objects, ancestors, and accumulated accusations function inside narrative signs, lawsuits, poems, laments, recollections, or restoration promises. Do not split every name, metaphor, charge, place, or consequence into an independent chunk.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable: Hosea is a prophetic collection with superscription, enacted signs, lawsuits, laments, appeals, warnings, promises, and a wisdom-like close, not an epistle. No salutation, thanksgiving, paraenesis, travel-plan, doxology, or benediction template may be imposed.

## over_split_risk_check

Highest risks are preserving chapter fallback; detaching accusation from consequence or judgment from restoration; proof-texting famous verses; treating every image change as a boundary; forcing the identity of speakers, spouse/children, Adam at 6:7, king/prince, Assyria/Egypt referents, Jacob traditions, prophet/Moses, death/Sheol, or the final wise reader; selecting a textual emendation; and missing the English/Hebrew versification offset. Prefer larger coherent units, exact alternatives, LOW confidence, and deferred review.

Relations to Torah, Samuel-Kings/Chronicles, Psalms, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, the Twelve, and later reuse are evidence only and cannot force harmonization, identity, chronology, fulfillment, or theology.
