# Esther literary strategy — candidate evidence only

Candidate-only, non-authorizing, independent of M1–M6/comparison/T417. Cover only the canonical
WEB/MT Esther text in the 66-book scope; do not import Greek additions. Read Esther.1.1–10.3 as
court narrative, paired banquets, royal deliberation, decree/dispatch, selection/recognition,
concealed identity, assassination report, adversarial promotion, annihilation edict, lament and
intercession, counter-banquets, exposure, reversal, counter-edict, conflict report, festival
institution and closing royal notice. Chapter numbers and English headings are never boundary
authority. Prefer the larger coherent unit where a banquet needs its court consequence, an edict
needs its dispatch and response, a request needs its answer, or a reversal needs its narrated
outcome.

## Literary form decision matrix — literary_form_decision_matrix

Use dated/geographic court frames; banquet cycles; command-refusal-counsel-decree; search and
selection narrative; identity concealment and adoption notices; assassination-discovery-record;
promotion, lot and accusation; royal edict and courier dispatch; public mourning; messenger
dialogue; fast/request approach; invitation and deferred petition; insomnia-record-recognition;
honor reversal; accusation/exposure/execution; property and office transfer; counter-edict drafting
and dispatch; communal response; conflict-and-rest reports; feast institution and explanatory
letter; tribute/source/office closure. Track repeated words, paired scenes, delay, irony, recognition
and reversal as literary evidence without turning them into providential or theological rulings.

## Larger-unit preservation check — larger_unit_preservation_check

Preserve Ahasuerus’s display banquets through Vashti’s banquet/refusal and the counselors’ decree;
the young-women search through Esther’s selection and coronation; Mordecai’s assassination report
through its archival recording; Haman’s promotion through lot, accusation, royal authorization,
edict and city response; Mordecai’s mourning through Esther’s messenger dialogue and fast/request;
Esther’s approach through the first banquet and delayed petition; Haman’s household counsel through
the insomnia record-reading, Mordecai honor reversal and warning; Esther’s second banquet through
exposure, execution and property transfer; the counter-edict drafting through dispatch and communal
reversal; the two-day conflict/rest sequence; Purim’s practice, explanation and letters; and the
closing tribute/Mordecai notice.

## List/register function check — list_register_function_check

Officials, provinces, feast durations, attendants, royal advisers, candidates, gifts, couriers,
dates, peoples, conflict totals, Haman’s sons, festival obligations and closing royal notices stay
grouped by governing narrative or decree function. Split only when speaker, audience, legal
document, banquet stage, request-response phase, date, conflict phase, or explanatory purpose
changes. Do not atomize names or numerals.

## Epistle unit check if applicable — epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Esther is not an epistle, but it embeds decrees and festival letters. Preserve sender/authority,
audience/provinces, operative command, date, seal, couriers and narrated public response as a
complete document-action unit; retain the larger narrative reversal where detaching the decree
would orphan its cause or outcome.

## Language, translation, textual, and versification gates

The canonical route uses Hebrew Esther. Flag without deciding: Persian royal names/titles,
administrative vocabulary, feast/court objects, eunuch terminology, dates/calendars, `pur`/lot,
ethnonyms and identity language, law/custom/decree formulas, annihilation and self-defense wording,
property/confiscation, gallows/tree height, “another place” deliverance language, fasting, royal
approach, request/delay, record-book, honor, mourning/joy, fear language, conflict totals, spoil
denial, rest/feast terminology, Purim naming and obligation, and source/tribute closure. Preserve
MT/LXX differences and Greek additions as textual-history review holds only; do not import additions
into verse coverage, select a tradition, fill perceived theological silence, infer providence,
determine historicity/chronology, define ethnicity, authorize violence, or settle moral/theological
questions.

## Source metadata evidence-only check — source_metadata_evidence_only_check

WEB layout/headings/chapters/footnotes and OSHB/UXLC lemmas, morphology, accents, paragraph
markers, qere/ketiv and Strong tags are evidence only. They cannot decide roots-as-meaning,
speaker identity, textual preference, chronology, authorship, historicity, ethnicity, providence,
ethics, theology, or seams without local form/discourse evidence. OSHB/UXLC are correlated
WLC-family witnesses.

## Over-split risk check — over_split_risk_check

Highest risks: separating a banquet from its refusal/request/consequence; detaching edict content
from dispatch and city response; splitting the Esther–Mordecai messenger dialogue; fragmenting the
approach/first-banquet/delayed-petition arc; detaching insomnia and record-reading from Mordecai’s
honor reversal; splitting exposure from execution/property reversal; atomizing the counter-edict or
conflict reports; and separating Purim practice from its explanatory letters. Also avoid one giant
“reversal” chunk that erases real document, speaker, scene and day transitions.

## Canonical relation candidates

Record evidence-only relations to Joseph/Daniel court narratives, Exodus/Deuteronomy festival and
memorial language, Saul–Agag/Amalek passages, exile/restoration texts, Proverbs’ court and reversal
sayings, and later canonical reuse. Relations cannot harmonize chronology, establish genealogy,
define ethnicity, import divine causation, force boundary symmetry, authorize violence, or decide
theological/moral claims.

## Ancient Jewish and rabbinic context gap

No qualified pinned ancient Jewish/rabbinic corpus is available. Do not simulate it. Route Persian
court administration, Jewish identity/diaspora, Purim reception, Amalek/Haman reception, fasting,
violence/self-defense ethics, MT/LXX/additions textual history and later interpretation to qualified
external specialists with chronology labels.
