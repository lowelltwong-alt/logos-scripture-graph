# Habakkuk corrective literary strategy (T557)

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. This strategy governs the corrective rereview of Habakkuk under Lowell Wong's literary-only completion ruling. It may identify literary units and preserve retrieval context; it may not decide theology, canon, authorship, date, historical reconstruction, the identity or morality of nations, fulfillment, textual-witness preference, or promotion. Agreement inside the M7 mesh is evidence, never authority.

## Inventory and coordinate guard

- WEB inventory: 56 verses, Hab.1.1 through Hab.3.19, each exactly once and in order; chapter counts are 17, 20, and 19.
- The local OSHB and UXLC WLC-family views use the same Habakkuk chapter and verse coordinates as WEB. Correlated agreement between them is not an independent textual witness.
- WEB wording, WLC paragraph marks, accents, morphology, lemmas, roots, Strong-number hints, and translation footnotes are evidence only and never boundary authority.

## Literary-form routing

Read the book as a prophetic dialogue that opens into a five-woe taunt and closes as a titled prayer or psalm:

1. vision/revelation superscription;
2. first how-long complaint, moving from unanswered cries to paralyzed instruction and perverted justice;
3. divine answer announcing and characterizing the Chaldean force;
4. renewed address and complaint over a supposedly appointed agent who devours without mercy;
5. watchpost transition awaiting an answer;
6. written-vision answer, appointed-time assurance, proud-versus-faithful contrast, and insatiable-oppressor bridge;
7. taunting-proverb introduction and first woe over plunder and reversal;
8. second woe over unjust household gain, self-destruction, and the wall's witness;
9. third woe over blood-built city labor answered by divine purpose and earth-filling knowledge;
10. fourth woe over forced drink, exposure, the returning cup, and violence to land and creatures;
11. image-making critique, fifth idol woe, and the living-temple silence reversal;
12. prayer superscription and plea for renewed work with mercy;
13. theophanic advent from Teman and Paran through cosmic shaking;
14. warrior-theophany questions and march whose stated purpose is deliverance;
15. embodied trembling, patient waiting, crop-failure concession, rejoicing confession, strength image, and musical subscription.

These are hypotheses for blind review, not a pre-authorized route. Prefer the larger coherent unit when a possible child cannot stand without its complaint, answer, woe consequence, divine reversal, theophanic purpose, or prayer parent.

## Deciding markers and hard seams

- Hab.1.1 is a recoverable superscription; direct lament address and the repeated how-long/why questions begin at 1:2.
- Hab.1.2-4 is a complete complaint: cry, witnessed disorder, and the therefore-result in paralyzed law and distorted justice. Do not detach 1:4 as an abstract maxim.
- Hab.1.5 begins an imperative divine answer and 1:6 supplies its named instrument. The description continues through the guilt verdict at 1:11.
- Hab.1.12 reopens direct address and 1:13 renews the why-question. Fish, hook, net, sacrifice, prosperity, and the final mercy question form one escalating complaint through 1:17; OSHB samekh after 1:17 confirms closure pressure.
- Hab.2.1 is a first-person watchpost transition. Test whether it surfaces separately, closes the second complaint, or opens the answer; do not decide from chapter numbering.
- Hab.2.2 begins the explicit divine answer. Verses 2-3 concern writing, appointed time, waiting, and assured arrival; 2:4 states the proud/righteous contrast; 2:5 describes the unsatisfied gatherer and prepares the nations' taunt at 2:6. Test 2:2-5 as one answer-prologue while preserving its internal 2:3|4 and 2:4|5 pressures.
- Hab.2.6 explicitly introduces a parable/taunting proverb. The repeated woe formulas and matching judgments organize a cycle: 2:6-8, 2:9-11, 2:12-14, 2:15-17, and the image/idol movement at 2:18-20. OSHB paragraph markers after 2:8, 2:11, 2:14, 2:17, 2:18, and 2:20 are strong seam evidence but do not mechanically dictate surface chunks.
- Hab.2.18 poses the image-making critique before the explicit fifth woe in 2:19 and the living-Yahweh/holy-temple reversal in 2:20. Test 2:18-20 as a complete idol-to-silence unit versus dependent 2:18 and 2:19-20 children; never isolate 2:20 as a contextless command.
- Hab.3.1 is a prayer and performance superscription; 3:2 is direct petition. Test them together and separately while requiring full prayer hydration.
- Hab.3.3 begins divine advent; the scenery and nations tremble through 3:7, followed by OSHB samekh.
- Hab.3.8 begins rhetorical questions about rivers and sea. Bow, cleaved earth, cosmic bodies, wrathful march, deliverance purpose, and trampling continue through 3:15. OSHB pe after 3:13 and Selah signals within the poem are stanza evidence; they must not detach the purpose clause from the warrior movement.
- Hab.3.16 returns to the first-person hearer and bodily response. Verses 17-18 form an even-though/yet concession and response; 3:19 completes the confession, movement image, and musical subscription. Test 3:16 as a transition only if 3:16-19 remains the mandatory response parent.

## Original-language and translation hot zones

- The wording and textual history of 1:12, the difficult syntax and referents in 2:4-5, the exact reach of the taunt introduction at 2:6, and the relation of 2:18 to the fifth woe at 2:19 require explicit counterevidence and confidence calibration.
- `Woe`, `therefore`, `for/because`, direct-address changes, rhetorical questions, speech formulas, Selah, Shigionoth, musical subscription language, paragraph marks, and poetic parallelism may support seams but never decide them alone.
- The expressions usually rendered faith/faithfulness in 2:4, wine or wealth in 2:5, anointed in 3:13, and the rare performance or musical terms in chapter 3 are translation hot zones. Do not resolve their total lexical meaning or theology in order to choose a boundary.
- Roots are not meanings. Morphology, lemma numbers, accents, English glosses, and wordplay proposals are evidence only.
- No local primary LXX, DSS, Targum, rabbinic, ANE, or Second-Temple claim may be made unless a qualified, passage-pinned source packet is actually available. Otherwise record the evidence gap.

## Whole-Bible relation guard

Later use of Hab.1.5 in Acts 13 and Hab.2.4 in Romans, Galatians, and Hebrews may be recorded as canonical reception and retrieval-risk evidence only. Exodus/Deuteronomy, Psalms, Judges, and other prophetic theophany parallels may protect the full form of Habakkuk 3 but may not decide dependence, chronology, fulfillment, theology, wording preference, or local seams. Let the immediate complaint, answer, taunt, and prayer forms lead.

## Parent hydration and proof-text premortem

Expected mandatory parents to test and record are 1:1-2:5, 1:12-2:5, 2:2-20, 2:6-20, 3:1-19, 3:3-15, and 3:16-19. In particular:

- never surface 1:4 without the 1:2-4 complaint;
- never surface 1:5 or 2:4 as a detached slogan without its immediate answer movement and book dialogue context;
- never surface an individual woe's judgment without its accusation and consequence;
- never surface 2:20 without the 2:18-20 image/idol contrast and the 2:6-20 taunt parent;
- never surface cosmic-warrior imagery from 3:3-15 without the prayer heading, deliverance purpose, and non-authorizing violence/ethics caution;
- never surface crop failure or rejoicing in 3:17-18 without the 3:16-19 embodied-response confession.

## Review and hold discipline

For every proposed decision, three blind roles - Hebrew/textual/versification, prophetic and poetic literary form, and canonical retrieval premortem - must supply decision-specific support, counterevidence, and source references. Each scoped challenge receives an author response; a fresh boss rules without forcing consensus; every specialist gets a post-ruling appeal opportunity; all losing alternatives remain append-only.

Accept a boundary when form and seam evidence support it. Hold only a genuinely contested retrieval choice with a specific, answerable human-or-external-AI question. Textual difficulty, versification, later use, rare performance vocabulary, or dense theology lowers confidence only when it materially changes the surface boundary. Do not defer the whole book by default.

## Completion checks

Habakkuk is complete only when the active candidate has bespoke per-chunk rationales, real literary-form labels, rejected alternatives, honest confidence, decision-local reviews, preserved dissents and appeals, integer indices 1..N, exact 56/56 ordered coverage, passing whole-map, corrective-depth, exact-coverage, and literary-quality validators, and a fresh hash-bound postcheck. Global sidecar installation is not a completion gate under the owner ruling.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

The expected outer architecture is superscription plus complaint-answer dialogue (1:1-2:5), five-woe taunt cycle (2:6-20), and titled prayer/theophany/response (3:1-19). Candidate surface units must remain linked to those parents. When a stanza or paragraph marker conflicts with sentence, speech, causal, or purpose continuity, preserve the larger coherent surface and record the child seam as dissent.

## list_register_function_check

Habakkuk's disorder terms, Chaldean traits, fish and net imagery, woe accusations, building materials, land and creature violence, idol materials, cosmic features, weapons, crop failures, and animal losses function as cumulative complaint, answer, taunt, theophany, or confession registers. Preserve each register's rhetorical result; never turn list members, glosses, or lexical roots into independent chunks or total meanings.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable. Habakkuk is prophetic dialogue, taunting proverb, prayer, and theophanic psalm rather than an epistle. Later epistolary reuse of 2:4 is reception evidence only and cannot impose letter-argument boundaries on Habakkuk.

## source_metadata_evidence_only_check

WEB coordinates define candidate coverage. OSHB and UXLC are correlated WLC-family views. Paragraph marks after 1:17; 2:8, 11, 14, 17, 18, 20; 3:7; and 3:13, along with accents, morphology, lemmas, roots, Strong-number hints, and translation footnotes, are evidence only. They cannot authorize a boundary, reading, translation, history, theology, or witness preference.

## over_split_risk_check

Highest risks are detaching 1:4, treating 2:1 as independent without dialogue hydration, isolating 2:4, splitting a woe before its consequence, separating 2:18 from 2:19-20 without the full idol unit, atomizing theophany around Selah, or isolating 3:17-18 from trembling and strength. For tied evidence, keep the larger surface, lower confidence when needed, and preserve the child seam as dissent rather than a default hold.

## sidecar_specificity_plan

If a genuinely low-confidence or held boundary appears, its record must name the exact decision, span, marker, rejected alternative, parent hydration, source limitation, answerable external or human question, and appeal ID. Global pass-one sidecars are not rewritten under the owner ruling.
