# Ezra literary strategy — candidate evidence only

Candidate-only, non-authorizing, independent of M1–M6/comparison/T417. Read Ezra.1.1–10.44 as
imperial proclamation, return and cultic-restoration narrative, genealogical and inventory
registers, opposition dossier, Aramaic correspondence, prophetic restart, temple completion,
commission, memoir, journey, prayer-confession, assembly, and implementation register. Chapter
numbers and English headings are never boundary authority. Prefer the larger coherent unit where
an edict requires its narrated response, a letter requires its answer, a register requires its
governing purpose, or a prayer requires the assembly response.

## Literary form decision matrix — literary_form_decision_matrix

Use decree/proclamation formulas; inventory and returnee-register heads/totals; place/time/actor
resets; altar/festival/foundation stages; lament and praise responses; adversary request and refusal;
accusation-letter-answer dossiers; prophetic restart and governor inquiry; archival memorandum and
royal decree; completion/dedication/Passover closure; genealogy-to-commission transition;
first-person memoir and travel notices; recruitment and camp register; fasting/prayer/entrustment/
arrival sequence; officials’ report; confession prayer; public assembly proposal/oath; and final
case register. Embedded documents remain distinguishable without being detached from the narrative
question they answer.

## Larger-unit preservation check — larger_unit_preservation_check

Preserve Cyrus’s proclamation with mobilization and vessel restoration; the returnee register with
its stated return function and totals; altar restoration through festival and foundation
preparation; foundation ceremony with mixed communal response; opposition through accusation and
cessation; the Aramaic Rehum letter with Artaxerxes’s answer; prophetic restart, Tattenai inquiry,
Darius search/decree, and completion as a dossier-driven narrative while retaining document seams;
dedication through Passover closure; Ezra’s genealogy with commission introduction; Artaxerxes’s
Aramaic commission as a full embedded document; Ezra’s response and administrative implementation;
the Ahava recruitment, Levite supplement, fast, entrusted treasure, journey, arrival and delivery;
the officials’ report, Ezra’s embodied response and confession; and the assembly proposal, public
meeting, investigation, and concluding register.

## List/register function check — list_register_function_check

Vessels, returnees, clans, priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, servants, disputed genealogies,
livestock, gifts, temple inventory, recruited families, entrusted treasure, delivered offerings,
officials, and final marriage-case names remain grouped by governing function. Split only when the
register’s stated purpose, constituency, legal/cultic status, narrative scene, speaker, language,
document, or closing total changes. Do not atomize names.

## Epistle unit check if applicable — epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Ezra is not an epistle, but its embedded Persian-period correspondence requires the analogous
document-unit check. Preserve each sender, addressee, salutation, accusation or request, decree or
answer, and narrated implementation as distinguishable forms; keep the larger inquiry-response
dossier intact when detaching a letter would orphan the question it answers.

## Language, translation, textual, and versification gates

Require explicit Hebrew/Aramaic routing. Ezra 4:8–6:18 and 7:12–26 are Imperial Aramaic; the
surrounding book is Hebrew. Language switch is evidence and a document/form signal, not automatic
authority. Flag without deciding: Cyrus/Darius/Artaxerxes/Ahasuerus naming and chronology; Persian
titles and administrative vocabulary; temple vessels, weights, monetary units, dates, clans and
totals; `nethinim`/temple-servant terminology; genealogical exclusion and purity vocabulary;
`ever-hannahara`/Beyond the River; letter salutations and legal formulas; archival search wording;
prophetic attribution; sacrifice/festival terminology; Ezra’s priestly-scribal titles; “law of the
God of heaven” and royal-law formulas; first-person/third-person transitions; fasting, protection,
treasure and offering language; covenant, guilt, holy-seed, foreign-women/peoples terminology; rain,
assembly, oath, investigation, dismissal, and final-list syntax. No preferred reading, empire
chronology, ethnic/racial theory, marriage doctrine, covenant/theology ruling, historical
reconstruction, or authorization.

## Source metadata evidence-only check — source_metadata_evidence_only_check

WEB layout/headings/chapters/footnotes and OSHB/UXLC lemmas, morphology, accents, paragraph
markers, qere/ketiv, and Strong tags are provenance-bearing evidence only. They cannot decide
roots-as-meaning, textual preference, imperial chronology, authorship/source theory, ethnic
identity, legal or cultic authority, theology, or seams without local form/discourse evidence.
OSHB/UXLC are correlated WLC-family witnesses.

## Over-split risk check — over_split_risk_check

Highest risks: detaching Cyrus’s edict from its response; atomizing the chapter-2 or chapter-10
registers; splitting altar, festival, foundation, and response only at modern chapters; treating
each Aramaic letter as unrelated to its narrative inquiry; separating Tattenai’s question from
Darius’s archive search and decree; detaching dedication from Passover closure; fragmenting Ezra’s
commission document; dividing the Ahava fast/treasure/journey/arrival sequence; isolating confession
phrases into theological proof-texts; and separating the assembly proposal from investigation and
case register.

## Canonical relation candidates

Record evidence-only relations to Exodus vessel/tabernacle material, Numbers priest/Levite and
assembly registers, Deuteronomy covenant/festival/foreign-marriage texts, Kings–Chronicles temple
destruction/restoration and Cyrus closure, Haggai and Zechariah’s temple-restart setting,
Nehemiah’s lists/assemblies, Daniel’s imperial court vocabulary, Esther’s Persian setting, and
prophetic restoration language. Relations cannot harmonize lists or chronology, define ethnicity,
select witnesses, force boundary symmetry, or authorize legal/cultic/theological conclusions.

## Ancient Jewish and rabbinic context gap

No qualified pinned ancient Jewish/rabbinic corpus is available. Do not simulate it. Route Persian
administration, Imperial Aramaic epistolography, Second Temple priestly/Levitical offices,
genealogical status, festival practice, intermarriage terminology, textual history, and later
reception to qualified external specialists with chronology labels.
