# Ezekiel candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. This strategy replaces the 48-row chapter scaffold with structural movements derived from the received text. It decides no theology, canon, authorship, date, historical reconstruction, ethics, divine agency, identity, fulfillment, eschatology, temple blueprint, preferred witness, or preferred translation.

## faithful_route

Trace dated word-events, throne/temple/valley visions, prophetic commission, sign command-performance-interpretation sequences, messenger reports, oracle addressees, disputations, allegories/riddles, laments, historical rehearsals, nation-oracle headings, watchman renewals, restoration sequences, visionary tours, measurement itineraries, cultic/legal instruction, land allotments, and closing city/gate formulae. Prefer the complete larger vision, oracle, sign-act, allegory, lament, disputation, tour, or allocation movement.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

- Hebrew/Aramaic morphology, repeated word-event and date formulae, ben-adam address, ruach polysemy, glory/throne/creature/wheel vocabulary, directional verbs, temple terms, measurement units, qere/ketiv, rare lexemes, and syntax are evidence only.
- MT, LXX/Old Greek, Papyrus 967, DSS fragments, Syriac, Vulgate, and other versions may show wording, order, omission/addition, and versification pressure; none selects a preferred witness or forces a boundary.
- Roots are not meanings. Strong's identifiers are lexical locators only and never boundary authority. Wordplay and lexical repetition require contextual, morphological, syntactic, discourse, and translation checks.
- Merkavah, rabbinic, Jewish liturgical, patristic, and later interpretive traditions are reception evidence only. They cannot identify figures, authorize access restrictions, settle a temple blueprint, or determine seams.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

Retain explicit macro parents for 1-3 call/commission; 4-7 siege signs and end oracles; 8-11 temple vision and glory departure; 12-24 signs, allegories, disputations, Jerusalem histories, laments and the siege-day/wife-sign complex; 25-32 nation oracles; 33-39 watchman renewal, shepherds, mountains, bones/sticks and Gog sequence; and 40-48 temple/land/city vision. Disputed internal seams collapse to the complete parent, stay LOW, and enter append-only appeal.

## list_register_function_check

Object inventories, abomination tours, accusations, historical rebellions, ship cargo/crew lists, royal/nation catalogues, military hosts, bones, temple measurements, chambers, sacrifices, festivals, tribal allotments, boundary coordinates, gates, and city names perform cumulative visionary, lament, legal, spatial, or rhetorical functions. Never split every item, measurement, tribe, direction, or refrain into an independent chunk.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable: Ezekiel is prophetic vision/oracle/narrative, not an epistle. No epistolary salutation, thanksgiving, argument, paraenesis, travel plan, doxology, or benediction template may be imposed.

## source_metadata_evidence_only_check

WEB coordinates, OSHB/UXLC morphology, translation metadata, headings, source-file order, modern pericope titles, manuscript metadata, rabbinic restrictions, reception history, and internal Bible relations are navigation/corroboration only. They do not decide Ezekiel's authorship/date, event sequence, redaction, historicity, identity of the human figure/prince/shepherd/David/Gog, divine-agency ethics, resurrection, fulfillment, eschatology, temple practice, canon, or theology.

## over_split_risk_check

Highest risks are chapter fallback; throne-vision image atomization; separating sign command from performance or interpretation; splitting allegory from explanation; extracting 18:20, 34:23, 36:26, or 37:1-14 as proof texts; treating every nation/date as isolated; deciding Gog or prince identity; harmonizing the fall report with Kings/Jeremiah; using P967/Greek order to select a reading; and turning chapters 40-48 into either one opaque block or hundreds of measurements. Prefer coherent tours and instruction movements, retain exact larger alternatives, and defer.

## sidecar_specificity_plan

Every LOW decision projects to all three sidecars with exact span/decision ID, deciding marker, complete macro parent, rejected alternative, Hebrew/textual/translation pressure, speaker/addressee/identity risk, violence/sexualized imagery/divine-agency risk, premortem failure mode, and requested specialist. Cross-references to Torah, Samuel-Kings/Chronicles, Jeremiah, Isaiah, the Twelve, Psalms, Daniel, Zechariah, and later reuse are evidence only and cannot force boundary symmetry, harmonization, identity, fulfillment, or theology.

## independent_review_plan

Use blind Hebrew/textual/translation, literary-form/vision/sign/allegory, and canonical-relations/premortem primaries. Peer review challenges every final unit and audits every overlapping proposal and global hot-zone record. Sol answers every challenge; the boss may retain larger LOW units but cannot force consensus. Unresolved disputes become append-only appeals. A fresh role-separated postchecker must recompute exact 1,273-verse coverage, contiguous positive integer indices, review parity, sidecars, T467 quality, hash bindings, and non-authority guards before receipt closure.
