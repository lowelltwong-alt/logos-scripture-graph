# Galatians candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace all six chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB Galatians coordinate set (149 verses). Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, audience/opponent reconstruction, chronology, apostolic authority, law/covenant system, justification doctrine, circumcision policy, gender/status doctrine, ethics, history, preferred witness, reading, punctuation, or quotation speaker.

## faithful_route

Trace prescript; astonished rebuke and rival-message anathema; servant/commission claim; autobiographical defense from former conduct through Arabia/Jerusalem/Syria-Cilicia; later Jerusalem consultation and mission recognition; Antioch confrontation; works/hearing and Abraham-faith proof sequence; curse/redemption/covenant-promise argument; law-purpose, pedagogue, baptismal-unity and heir/guardian sequence; former enslavement, adoption/Spirit cry, return warning and personal appeal; Hagar-Sarah allegorical argument; freedom/circumcision warning, love summary and leaven/persuasion; flesh-Spirit conflict and vice/fruit catalogues; restoration, burden-sharing, sowing-reaping and doing-good paraenesis; autograph, boasting/circumcision contrast, marks and benediction. Prefer complete rebuke-defense, autobiographical narrative, Scripture proof sequence, heir/adoption analogy, personal appeal, allegory, freedom warning, vice/fruit contrast, paraenetic or epistolary-close unit.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

Koine prescript and rebuke formulas; gar/oun/de/alla chains; temporal epeita markers; rhetorical questions; diatribal address; quotation and catena signals; pisteos/nomos/epangelia lexical recurrence; men-de contrasts; vocatives; allegory markers; imperatives; vice/virtue list framing; autograph and grace formulas are evidence only. Textual/translation/speaker hot zones include Gal.1.6-9, 1.15, 1.18, 2.1, 2.5, 2.16, 2.20, 3.1, 3.10, 3.16, 3.20, 4.13, 4.25, 5.1, 5.12-13, 5.16-17, 5.24, 6.2, 6.11 and 6.17. No witness, reading, punctuation, quotation-speaker, addressee, opponent, event sequence, or chronology is selected. Roots, etymology, Strong-style tags, morphology, headings, rhetoric and mirror-reading are not authority.
