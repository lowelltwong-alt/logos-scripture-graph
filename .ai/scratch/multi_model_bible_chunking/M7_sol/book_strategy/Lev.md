# Leviticus book strategy — M7 Sol independent candidate

## selected_strategy

Use `literary_marker_aware_v2` with the T467 larger-unit overlay. Read Leviticus as a sustained final-form Hebrew legal-ritual book: offering instructions; priestly installation, service, and crisis; clean/unclean distinctions and restoration; annual sanctuary access and atonement; communal and priestly holiness; sacred time and land; covenant consequences; and a valuation appendix with a second book-level close. Preserve complete procedures, case hierarchies, affordability branches, instruction/execution relations, sanction frames, and corpus summaries. Paragraphs, chapter numbers, repeated speech formulas, Hebrew lemmas, morphology, Strong-style identifiers, and later reuse remain evidence only.

## literature_type_or_mixed_genre

Leviticus combines worshiper-facing and priest-facing ritual procedures, conditional/casuistic cases, classified lists, diagnostic algorithms, narrative command execution, divine-fire climax, fatal breach and adjudication, clean/unclean status rules, annual ritual sequence, prohibition/exhortation/sanction collections, priestly status and food rules, festival calendar, a narrative case with generalized ruling and execution, land/redemption law, conditional blessing and escalating sanctions, and valuation/dedication schedules.

## substrate_markers_considered

The pinned WEB witness contains 859 verses, 272 paragraph markers, 30 footnote groups, and one short lineated quotation at 10:3. OSHB and UXLC local Hebrew views cover the book. These features locate review pressure but do not authorize boundaries. The most important non-literary risk is versification: Hebrew/MT Leviticus 5:20–26 corresponds to WEB/English 6:1–7, and Hebrew/MT 6:1 corresponds to WEB 6:8. Every observation at that seam must identify its versification; raw OSIS IDs may not be joined as though identical.

## strongs_metadata_considered_evidence_only

Speech roots `דבר`/H1696, `אמר`/H559, and opening `קרא`/H7121; offering terms `קרבן`, `עלה`, `מנחה`, `שלמים`, `חטאת`, and `אשם`; status/procedure terms `טמא`, `טהור`, `צרעת`, and `כפר`; legal/refrain terms `קדש`, `חק`, `תורה`, `נפש`, and `כרת`; and calendar/redemption terms `מועד`, `שבת`, `יובל`, `גאל`, and `ערך` are phrase-, clause-, and form-level evidence only. Roots and glosses neither define English meaning automatically nor generate a boundary.

## source_metadata_evidence_only_check

WEB paragraphs, footnotes, headings, typography, English offering labels, and quotation layout are non-authorizing. OSHB morphology and lemma attributes are lookup hints rather than lexical or textual-critical authority; UXLC is a second text view, not a preferred reading. Later references may illuminate source form—Exodus 29 with Leviticus 8–9, Leviticus 10 with 16, Luke 2 with 12, Gospel cleansing commands with 14, Hebrews 9 with 16, Matthew 22 with 19:18, Numbers 28–29 with 23—but cannot atomize the source or impose theology.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

Preserve the 1–7 offering corpus and its worshiper/priest perspectives; the 8–10 instruction–execution–crisis movement; the 11–15 distinction/restoration corpus; the long-distance 10:1–2 to 16:1 access relation; the 18 prohibition, 19 exhortation, and 20 sanction relations; the 23 calendar and Booths recap/supplement; the 24 case–ruling–execution frame; the 25 land/Jubilee system; and the double close at 26:46 and 27:34. Smaller retrieval children require explicit parent relations.

## list_register_function_check

Animal classes, offering variants, diagnostic symptoms, priestly defects, forbidden kinship relations, festivals, property cases, sanction escalations, and valuation bands are functional lists, not expendable data and not automatic one-item chunks. Split only when governing addressee, procedure, actor, case function, or closure materially changes.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable. The analogous guard preserves direct-speech corpora, complete procedures and case branches, narrative command/result chains, classified lists, motive clauses, sanctions, and colophons.

## over_split_risk_check

Highest risks are one unit per animal, symptom, ritual action, command, refrain, festival date, property case, sanction escalation, or valuation band. Repeated `Yahweh spoke`, `this is the law`, `I am Yahweh`, `cut off`, paragraph markers, and chapter seams remain review signals only. Day of Atonement stages, Leviticus 18–20 controversy texts, and Jubilee cases must retain their larger frames.

## sidecar_specificity_plan

Each low or medium-low candidate receives exact-span rows naming competing seams, Hebrew/English translation pressure, procedural dependency, downstream retrieval risk, and the requested specialist. Material disagreements receive two blind primary reviews, peer crosscheck, Sol boss ruling, and an appeal opportunity. Two material rewrite cycles are the stop limit; unresolved dissent remains low-confidence and deferred for human or external-AI review.

## chapter_only_fallback_reason_if_used

No chapter boundary is accepted merely because it is modern. Whole-chapter candidates are permitted only when direct reading finds a complete procedure, collection, narrative, or discourse and the sidecars record internal seams. The WEB/MT mismatch around 5:14–6:8 is a specific prohibition against chapter-number reasoning.

## frontier_or_atlas_candidate_expectations

Mandatory candidates include WEB 4:1–6:7, especially the MT/English versification seam; 8:1–10:20; 13:1–14:57; 16:1–34; 18:1–20:27; 23:1–24:23; 25:1–55; 26:1–27:34; and translation-sensitive `צרעת`, `עזאזל`, `קרי`, and `חרם` contexts. No reviewed ANE, Second Temple, ancient Jewish, rabbinic, Mishnah, Talmud, Midrash, Sifra, Philo, or Josephus corpus exists locally in scope. None is simulated; reception/history questions require separately sourced review.

## Independent review and appeal route

Every frozen decision receives blind Hebrew/translation/canonical-context and literary/form primaries, followed by a distinct peer. A Sol/high boss adjudicates material disputes and returns the ruling to each challenger. A reasoned appeal preserves exact passage context, claim, evidence, counterevidence, uncertainty, remedy, and requested next reviewer. Consensus is not required, reviewer count is not authority, and unresolved appeals remain non-authorizing human/external-AI holds.
