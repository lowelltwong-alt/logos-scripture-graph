# Jeremiah literary strategy — candidate evidence only

## Selected strategy - selected_strategy

Use a marker-led prophetic-anthology route. Start with exact received WEB coverage, test Hebrew discourse and form evidence at every seam, preserve cross-chapter sermons and scenes, and keep the larger coherent parent whenever verse-only resolution would force a false textual, speaker, order, or chronology judgment.

## Literature type or mixed genre - literature_type_or_mixed_genre

Mixed prophetic anthology: superscription and call vision; poetic lawsuit, lament, confession, woe, judgment, and restoration oracles; prose sermons, trials, royal encounters, prison and siege scenes; sign acts; letters; scroll narratives; nation burdens; registers; and an historical appendix. Each active chunk must name its actual local form rather than the generic anthology.

## Substrate markers considered - substrate_markers_considered

Consider received paragraphing, word-event and messenger formulas, date and reign formulas, vision question-answer, vocatives, speaker and addressee resets, imperative chains, lament and confession envelopes, refrain closures, sign command-performance-interpretation sequences, prose actor and location changes, letter and scroll frames, nation headings, lists under a governing speech, and appendix transitions. These observations are boundary evidence, never authority over canon, theology, identity, chronology, historicity, or textual tradition.

## Strongs metadata considered evidence only - strongs_metadata_considered_evidence_only

OSHB and UXLC lemma, morphology, accent, qere/ketiv, and Strong-tag metadata may locate repeated wording or grammatical transitions. Roots are not meanings, tags are not translation rulings, and the two WLC-family sources are correlated rather than independent witnesses. Any missing LXX, DSS, Targumic, Second-Temple, or rabbinic primary corpus is recorded as a gap and never simulated.

Candidate-only, non-authorizing, independent of M1–M6/comparison/T417. Read Jeremiah 1:1–52:34 as a prophetic anthology of superscription/call, poetic oracles, prose sermons and conflict narratives, sign acts, symbolic visions, laments/confessions, royal and temple speeches, consolation/restoration poems, Baruch/scroll narratives, nation oracles and a historical appendix. Chapter numbers and English headings are never boundary authority. Prefer the larger complete oracle, sermon, sign-act sequence, poem, prose scene, letter, scroll episode or nation-oracle unit.

## Literary-form decision matrix — literary_form_decision_matrix

Use date/king/word-event formulas; “word of YHWH” and messenger introductions; call/vision question-answer; addressee and genre shifts; lawsuit, lament, confession and woe forms; temple, gate, potter, king and prophet settings; sign-act command/performance/interpretation; prose scene, actor and time resets; letter and scroll formulas; “days are coming” restoration sequences; nation-oracle headings; narrative resolution and appendix framing. Formula labels remain evidence.

## Larger-unit preservation check — larger_unit_preservation_check

Preserve macro parents while testing complete inner units: 1 call/visions; 2–6 early lawsuit and invasion poetry; 7–10 temple sermon/covenant/idol and lament sequence; 11–20 covenant conflict, sign acts and confessions; 21–25 royal/prophet oracles and seventy-year/cup sequence; 26–29 temple trial, yoke conflict and exile letter; 30–33 consolation/restoration and field-sign sequence; 34–36 covenant/slavery, Rechabites and scroll narratives; 37–39 siege, imprisonment and fall; 40–45 Gedaliah/remnant/Egypt and Baruch material; 46–51 nation oracles; 52 historical appendix. Preserve cross-chapter speeches, prose scenes and sign-act interpretation.

## List/register-function check — list_register_function_check

Keep sins, invaders, drought images, kings/prophets, temple offenses, covenant clauses, sign-act objects, exiles, restoration promises, siege actors, remnant delegations and nation judgments grouped by their governing oracle, sermon, narrative or symbolic action. Do not atomize every poetic line, lament image, “word” formula or judgment item.

## Epistle-unit check if applicable — epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Jeremiah 29 is a letter-form unit, not a New Testament epistle: preserve sender/recipient, exhortation, warning and closing frame. Otherwise preserve prophetic and narrative units.

## Hebrew, translation, textual, order and versification gates

Flag without deciding: Hebrew poetry/prose transitions; qere/ketiv; rare words; symbolic names; speaker/addressee shifts; 1:11–16; 4:10; 7:22; 10:1–16; 11–20 confessions; 20:7–18; 23:5–8; 25:1–14 and cup oracle; 27–29 chronology/names; 30–33 restoration/new-covenant language; 31:15–22 and 31:31–37; 33:14–26; 36 scroll; 39:1–10; 40–45 chronology; 46–51 nation-oracle order; 51:59–64; 52 and Kings parallels. The shorter/differently ordered Greek Jeremiah and MT/LXX/DSS variants are evidence only. No preferred witness/order, emendation, speaker allocation, authorship/redaction strata, chronology repair, historicity, covenant/servant/messianic identity, fulfillment, ethics, divine-agency ruling, canon or theology.

## Source-metadata evidence-only check — source_metadata_evidence_only_check

WEB headings/layout and OSHB/UXLC lemmas, morphology, accents, qere/ketiv and Strong tags are evidence only. They cannot decide roots-as-meaning, prose/poetry seam, speaker, textual order, preferred reading, dating, authorship/redaction, fulfillment or theology. OSHB/UXLC are correlated.

## Over-split risk check — over_split_risk_check

Highest risks: chapter fallback; one chunk per “word” formula; fragmenting poems/confessions; splitting sign command from performance/meaning; breaking temple/royal speeches or prose trials at chapters; atomizing consolation promises; importing LXX order into WEB coverage; separating nation headings from their oracle; harmonizing the appendix with Kings; and later-reuse proof-text seams. Opposite risk is swallowing distinct date/scene, letter, scroll, sign, nation and appendix units. When contested, retain the larger unit, lower confidence and preserve the appeal.

## Sidecar-specificity plan — sidecar_specificity_plan

Every LOW row names its exact span, competing units from each blind primary, Hebrew/textual/order/speaker/form pressure, context-loss risk and requested specialist. Generic “Jeremiah is complex” is invalid. Appeals remain append-only and may disagree with the boss.

## Canonical relation candidates

Record evidence-only relations to Torah covenant/exodus, Samuel–Kings/Chronicles prose, Psalms and wisdom, other prophets and later canonical reuse. Relations cannot harmonize Kings, choose MT/LXX order, settle chronology/authorship, identify covenant figures, claim fulfillment, force seams or authorize theology.

## Ancient Jewish and rabbinic context gap

No qualified pinned DSS/Second Temple/rabbinic corpus is available. Do not simulate it. Route MT/LXX/DSS order and variants, ancient history, prophetic form, temple/royal conflict, covenant reception, Targumic/rabbinic interpretation and later Jewish/Christian readings to qualified external specialists with chronology labels.

## Completion boundary

Complete only with genuine integer-indexed exact coverage, blind Hebrew/textual-order, literary/prophetic-narrative and canonical-premortem primaries, peer crosscheck, author response to every challenge, boss ruling, append-only appeals, fresh postcheck, relations, three sidecars and a hash-bound receipt listing the official chunk-map validator. Agreement is evidence, never authority.
