# Matthew candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace twenty-eight chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB Matthew coordinate set. Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, source priority, historicity, chronology, harmonization, Torah or church doctrine, ethics, identity, messianic fulfillment, eschatological timetable, resurrection adjudication, preferred witness, or preferred reading.

## faithful_route

Trace title/genealogy list function; birth and infancy scenes with dream/quotation/movement closures; John, baptism, testing, Galilee opening and disciple call; the mountain teaching discourse as complete saying clusters and argument movements rather than verse atoms; descent and miracle/discipleship cycles; authority/controversy and compassion transition; Twelve commissioning and mission discourse; response-to-Jesus cycles and rest saying; Sabbath/controversy, sign, return, and kinship movement; parable discourse with frame, public parables, purpose/explanation, private parables, and closure; rejection, execution report, feeding, sea crossing, purity dispute, Gentile encounter, second feeding; signs, confession, passion prediction and discipleship; transfiguration, healing and community teaching; community discourse; journey debates, children, wealth, laborers, passion, service, healing; entry, temple, fig tree, authority, vineyard/banquet controversies; question cycles and David-son close; woes/lament; temple departure and eschatological discourse through watchfulness, stewardship, virgins, talents, and judgment scene; passion preparation, meal, Gethsemane, arrest/trial, denial; Roman hearing, death, burial/guard; tomb and commissioning close. Prefer the larger complete scene, controversy, parable cluster, discourse movement, trial sequence, or passion episode.
