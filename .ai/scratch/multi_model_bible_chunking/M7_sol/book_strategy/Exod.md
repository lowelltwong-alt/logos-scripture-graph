# Exodus book strategy — M7 Sol independent candidate

## selected_strategy

Use `literary_marker_aware_v2` with the T467 larger-unit overlay. Read Exodus as a final-form movement from oppression and commission through deliverance, wilderness formation, Sinai speech and covenant ratification, sanctuary instruction, breach/renewal, construction, installation, and book-level closure. Preserve setup/payoff, command/response, instruction/execution, law/ratification, and narrative/song relationships across modern chapters. WEB layout, source paragraphs, English footnotes, Strong-style tags, Hebrew lemmas/morphology, and editorial cross-references remain evidence only.

## literature_type_or_mixed_genre

Exodus is sustained Hebrew narrative containing a genealogical threshold and embedded register, commission and objection dialogue, repeated confrontation/plague cycles, ritual instruction interwoven with enactment and memorial explanation, itinerary and wilderness episodes, archaic victory poetry and refrain, theophany, apodictic and casuistic law, covenant ceremony, architectural and priestly procedures, intercession/presence dialogue, execution reports, inventories, and a closing installation/travel notice.

| Crisis/intercession/renewal | Preserve causal dependence across speech and chapter seams | 32:1–34:35 |

## substrate_markers_considered

The pinned WEB source supplies 349 paragraph markers across 1,213 Exodus verses. Dense `q1/q2` layout in 15:1–21 identifies the Song of the Sea as poetry; the remaining markers mainly expose speech and paragraph starts. No Exodus WEB section headings were observed. These markers support local form recognition but do not authorize boundaries. Candidate OSHB and UXLC source views are available for every Exodus verse and are used for Hebrew phrase, clause, syntax, and discourse questions only.

## strongs_metadata_considered_evidence_only

Strong-style identifiers, OSHB lemmas, morphology, and roots are review signals, not definitions or boundary generators. Important review signals include speech frames `אמר`/H559 and `דבר`/H1696, legal `כי`/`אם` clauses, song language `שיר`/H7891, and instruction/execution verbs `צוה`/H6680 and `עשה`/H6213. The review order is phrase, clause, syntax, discourse, literary form, book context, then canonical context.

## source_metadata_evidence_only_check

WEB paragraphs, poetry lineation, footnotes, quotation punctuation, headings, canonical editorial cross-references, and Hebrew-source attributes remain non-authorizing witnesses. Later reuse in Acts 7, the Gospels, Romans, Hebrews, Revelation, and elsewhere can show that source context must be preserved, but citation granularity never atomizes Exodus. Modern geography, historical reconstruction, textual criticism, ethics, and doctrine remain outside this chunking decision.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

The plan preserves the call/commission complex, genealogy plus recommission bridge, each complete plague scene within the cumulative cycle, Passover instruction/event/memorial relationships, sea narrative/song response, complete wilderness episodes, Sinai preparation and response frames, the covenant-code function, ratification/ascent, sanctuary instruction clusters, the breach/intercession/presence/renewal sequence, construction clusters, completion inspection, and the whole installation/glory/travel close. A larger unit is preferred where a smaller unit would detach an answer from its objection, an ordinance from its enactment, a law corpus from its ratification, or execution from its commissioning and completion.

## list_register_function_check

The 1:1–7 threshold, 6:14–27 genealogy, technical furnishing and garment lists, 38:21–31 material inventory, and repeated construction reports are literary architecture rather than expendable data. The initial candidate may separate object or procedure clusters when the function changes, but it will not split every measurement, name, formula, or item. Repetition between 25–31 and 35–40 is recorded as instruction/execution correspondence without selecting a compositional theory.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable: Exodus is not an epistle. The analogous guard is to preserve complete direct-speech frames, legal collections, ritual procedures, poems, intercessions, registers, and command/fulfillment relationships.

## over_split_risk_check

Highest over-split risks are 2:23–4:31, 6:2–7:13, individual substeps in the plagues, 10:21–11:10, 12:1–13:16, 14:1–15:21, the 15:22–18:27 wilderness sequence, 19:1–24:18, individual laws in 20:22–23:33, every object in 25–31 or 36–39, 32–34 chapter seams, and execution formulae. A paragraph, chapter, repeated speech formula, wayyiqtol, lemma, or later citation alone never establishes a split.

## sidecar_specificity_plan

Every `medium_low` or `low` candidate receives exact-span rows in the low-confidence register, frontier queue, and atlas feed, naming competing seams, the Hebrew/translation issue, larger-unit dependency, downstream risk, and requested specialist. Material disagreement is preserved in the per-decision review packet. After two material revision cycles, automatic rewriting stops; unresolved dissent retains the larger unit at reduced confidence as `deferred_human_or_external_ai`.

## chapter_only_fallback_reason_if_used

No chapter-only fallback is used because a modern chapter exists. Whole-chapter candidates are allowed only when direct final-form reading identifies a complete episode, register, procedure, or book-closing movement and the sidecars record any fragility required by T423/T467.

The repository has no reviewed ANE, Second Temple, ancient Jewish, or rabbinic corpus in scope. ## Independent review and appeal route

Every frozen decision receives two blind primaries: one Hebrew/translation/canonical-context review and one literary/form review. A distinct peer checker compares their claims, and a Sol/high boss adjudicates material disputes only. Each original reviewer then sees the ruling and may accept or append a reasoned appeal. Appeals preserve the passage context, exact disputed claim, evidence, rationale, uncertainty, proposed remedy, response to counterevidence, and requested human/AI reviewer. Consensus is not required: unresolved dissent is explicit, lower-confidence, non-authorizing, and queued for human or external-AI review.
