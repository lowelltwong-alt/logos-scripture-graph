## faithful_route

Trace the word-event superscription; elder/hearer summons; unprecedented-disaster questions; tell-to-generations sequence; locust catalogue and agricultural lament; wake/mourn/fast/consecrate/assemble/cries; day-of-YHWH alarm; advancing-host similes and cosmic imagery; return oracle; priestly intercession; answer and reversal; rain/harvest restoration; spirit-pouring and signs; salvation/escape declaration; nations summons, lawsuit, battle taunts, harvest/winepress imagery, enthroned judgment, refuge, and closing land/city restoration. Prefer complete summons-to-response, alarm-to-return, judgment-to-restoration, and nations-lawsuit-to-closing movements while recognizing genuine stanza and speaker seams.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

- Hebrew messenger formulae, imperatives, vocatives, rhetorical questions, parallelism, repeated roots, sound patterns, deictics, temporal formulas, speaker/addressee shifts, and chains of jussives/yiqtol/weqatal are evidence only.
- English Joel has three chapters, while the Masoretic/Hebrew tradition commonly numbers four; English 2:28-32 corresponds to Hebrew chapter 3 and English chapter 3 to Hebrew chapter 4. The campaign follows its local 73-verse coordinate inventory and records offsets without importing, deleting, or privileging verses.
- Difficult terms and images—successive locust names, shaddai, the northern one, teacher/rain language at 2:23, afterward, all flesh, pillars of smoke, survivors, Jehoshaphat/decision valleys, Sabeans, harvest/winepress, and Egypt/Edom—create translation and referent pressure. Ambiguity lowers confidence rather than authorizing emendation or identification.
- MT, Septuagint, Dead Sea evidence where extant, Syriac, Vulgate, Targum, and other versions may preserve wording, segmentation, order, or numbering differences. No witness or reading is preferred.
- Roots are not meanings. Strong's identifiers and morphology are lexical/navigation aids only, never boundary authority.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

Test fourteen locally complete forms before accepting them: 1:1; 1:2-4; 1:5-12; 1:13-14; 1:15-20; 2:1-11; 2:12-14; 2:15-17; 2:18-27; 2:28-32; 3:1-8; 3:9-11; 3:12-17; and 3:18-21. Preserve their macro parents for hydration: 1:1-20; 2:1-17; 2:18-27; 2:28-3:21; and 3:9-21. The formal superscription, priestly-fast summons, renewed trumpet, response pivot, temporal formula, named-address change, judicial-sitting change, and closing `in that day` tableau are tested as real form seams rather than accepted because of chapter numbering.

Do not erase the canonical premortem's larger-unit objections. It argues for 1:1-12, 1:13-20, 2:12-17, 3:1-3 plus 3:4-8, and 3:9-17 as safer retrieval units. The boss must either sustain those alternatives or state why local form closure outweighs detachment risk. The Hebrew and literary specialists agree on fourteen units but disagree at 3:4: Hebrew favors 3:1-3 plus 3:4-8, while literary favors the complete 3:1-8 lawsuit scene. That dissent remains reviewable even if the larger scene is selected.
