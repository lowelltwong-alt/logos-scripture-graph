# Zephaniah corrective literary strategy (T558)

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. This strategy governs the corrective rereview of Zephaniah under Lowell Wong's literary-only completion ruling. It may identify literary units and preserve retrieval context; it may not decide theology, canon, authorship, date, genealogy, historical reconstruction, national identity, fulfillment, textual-witness preference, or promotion. Agreement inside the M7 mesh is evidence, never authority.

## Inventory and coordinate guard

- WEB inventory: 53 verses, Zeph.1.1 through Zeph.3.20, each exactly once and in order; chapter counts are 18, 15, and 20.
- The local OSHB and UXLC WLC-family views use the same Zephaniah chapter and verse coordinates as WEB. Their agreement is correlated, not an independent textual vote.
- WEB wording, WLC paragraph marks, accents, morphology, lemmas, roots, Strong-number hints, and translation footnotes are evidence only and never boundary authority.

## Literary-form routing

Read the book as a prophetic sequence whose global-day movement narrows to Judah and Jerusalem, turns through a compass-like nations cycle, returns to the corrupt city, and closes with purification, remnant, Zion song, and restoration promises. Test these local forms:

1. word-event superscription and genealogy/date notice;
2. programmatic sweeping-judgment oracle;
3. Judah/Jerusalem cult and non-seeking indictment;
4. hush summons, prepared-sacrifice tableau, and punishment catalogue;
5. city soundscape, merchant lament, lamp search, complacency saying, and wealth reversal;
6. intensified day-of-Yahweh battle and darkness poem;
7. gather-before and seek summons;
8. Philistia/coast oracle with Judah-remnant reversal;
9. Moab/Ammon reproach oracle, reversal, and nations-worship horizon;
10. brief Cush direct-address oracle;
11. Assyria/Nineveh desolation, animal habitation, and proud-city taunt;
12. rebellious-city woe contrasting corrupt leaders with righteous Yahweh;
13. destroyed-nations example, correction summons, and failed response;
14. wait summons and judicial assembly pivot;
15. purified-peoples and dispersed-worshiper promise;
16. proud removal, humble remnant, truthful speech, rest, and safety promise;
17. Zion song, removed judgment, royal presence, fear-not reassurance, and divine rejoicing;
18. feast-related restoration, rescue, gathering, praise, return, and closing speech formula.

These are hypotheses for blind review, not a pre-authorized route. Prefer the larger coherent unit when a possible child cannot stand without its accusation, judgment, reversal, remnant, address, song, or mandatory parent.

## Deciding markers and hard seams

- Zeph.1.1 is a recoverable word-event superscription; first-person sweeping speech begins at 1:2.
- Zeph.1.2-3 repeats sweeping verbs, expands across human, animal, bird, and fish, and closes with a speech formula. Do not merge the title into this programmatic oracle.
- Zeph.1.4-6 narrows from the broad sweep to Judah and Jerusalem, then catalogues cultic allegiance and failures to seek. Preserve the indictment and its coordinated objects together.
- Zeph.1.7 begins with a hush imperative and grounds it in the near day, prepared sacrifice, and consecrated guests. Punishment continues through 1:9; OSHB samekh after 1:9 supports closure.
- Zeph.1.10 opens an in-that-day city soundscape. Cry, wail, merchant collapse, lamp search, complacent quotation, and property/vineyard reversal continue through 1:13.
- Zeph.1.14 restarts with the great day is near and develops a repeated day-register through wrath, darkness, alarm, blindness, blood, fire, and total end. OSHB samekh follows 1:18.
- Zeph.2.1-3 is an urgent communal gather-before/seek appeal. Repeated before and seek language resolves in possible hiding; do not detach 2:3 as generic advice.
- Zeph.2.4 begins with for and names four Philistine cities; OSHB samekh follows 2:4, while 2:5 opens a coastland woe and 2:6-7 reverses desolation into pasture for Judah's remnant. Test 2:4 as a dependent catalogue child versus whole 2:4-7; keep the full coast movement as mandatory parent.
- Zeph.2.8 changes to first-person hearing and names Moab/Ammon reproach. Oath, Sodom/Gomorrah comparison, remnant inheritance, pride rationale, and the nations-worship horizon continue through 2:11.
- Zeph.2.12 is a complete second-person Cushite oracle in one verse. Test it as a recoverable compass-cycle child only with 2:4-15 hydration.
- Zeph.2.13 changes to northern Assyria and Nineveh; desolation, animal occupation, exposed architecture, proud self-speech, and passerby response close at 2:15 before a samekh.
- Zeph.3.1 opens a city woe. Four failures, predatory leaders, treacherous prophets, profaning priests, and the contrast with righteous Yahweh continue through 3:5.
- Zeph.3.6-7 supplies destroyed nations as warning, quotes the correction summons, and concludes with the city's eager corruption. Test a 3:5|6 seam and retain 3:1-7 as the mandatory city parent.
- Zeph.3.8 begins therefore wait for me and announces assembly, indignation, and all-earth fire. Test the one-verse pivot separately only with 3:8-13 hydration.
- Zeph.3.9 turns with for then to purified speech and joint service; 3:10 adds dispersed worshipers and offering. At 3:11 in that day shifts to removal of pride; 3:12-13 completes the humble, truthful, secure remnant. OSHB samekh follows 3:13. Test 3:9-10 and 3:11-13 as dependent promise children versus one 3:9-13 restoration movement.
- Zeph.3.14 starts the Zion/Israel/Jerusalem song summons. Removed judgments, royal presence, fear-not speech, saving presence, and divine rejoicing continue through 3:17.
- Zeph.3.18 shifts to first-person restoration language whose translation and subject scope are difficult. Rescue, gathering, praise, return, and the closing says-Yahweh formula continue through 3:20. Test 3:18-20 as the closing restoration oracle without detaching 3:20.

## Original-language and translation hot zones

- Divine speech and prophetic voice shifts, feminine city address, the geography and rhetoric of the nations cycle, repeated day formulas, remnant terminology, and first-person promises are evidence for form and seam, not identity or theology decisions.
- The terse wording of 2:1, the relation of the paragraph marker after 2:4 to the 2:4-7 coast movement, the one-verse Cush oracle at 2:12, the quotation and pronoun scope in 3:7-8, and the difficult wording or subject of 3:18 require explicit counterevidence and confidence calibration.
- WLC-family samekh after 1:9, 1:18, 2:4, 2:15, and 3:13 is strong closure evidence, not automatic surface authority.
- Roots are not meanings. Morphology, lemmas, accents, glosses, etymological proposals, place-name wordplay, and Strong-number tags cannot select a total meaning, reading, or boundary.
- No local primary LXX, DSS, Targum, rabbinic, ANE, or Second-Temple claim may be made unless a qualified, passage-pinned source packet is actually available. Otherwise record the evidence gap.

## Whole-Bible relation guard

Later or parallel day-of-Yahweh language, creation-reversal imagery, Sodom/Gomorrah comparison, Nineveh material, remnant promises, Zion song, and whole-Bible reuse may be recorded only as canonical context. They may not decide dependence, chronology, fulfillment, national identity, theology, wording preference, or local seams. Immediate address, form, causal sequence, speech turn, refrain, and closure lead.

## Parent hydration and proof-text premortem

Expected mandatory parents to test and record are 1:1-18, 1:7-18, 2:1-15, 2:4-15, 2:4-7, 3:1-7, 3:8-13, 3:14-20, and 3:1-20. In particular:

- never surface broad sweeping or day imagery without the local Judah/Jerusalem and prophetic context;
- never detach 1:7 as a silence command, 1:12 as a saying about divine inactivity, or 1:18 as an isolated total-end claim;
- never surface 2:3 without the 2:1-3 summons;
- never surface 2:12 without the nations-cycle parent, and never use a compass route to decide history or ethnicity;
- never detach 3:8's assembly and fire from the purification and remnant movement that follows;
- never surface 3:12-13, 3:14-17, or 3:18-20 as unqualified identity or prosperity promises apart from their city judgment, remnant, song, and closing restoration contexts.

## Review and hold discipline

For every proposed decision, three blind roles - Hebrew/textual/versification, prophetic literary form, and canonical retrieval premortem - must supply decision-specific support, counterevidence, and source references. Each scoped challenge receives an author response; a fresh boss rules without forcing consensus; every specialist gets a post-ruling appeal opportunity; all losing alternatives remain append-only.

Accept a boundary when form and seam evidence support it. Hold only a genuinely contested retrieval choice with a specific, answerable human-or-external-AI question. Textual difficulty, geography, later reuse, speaker scope, or dense theology lowers confidence only when it materially changes the surface boundary. Do not defer the whole book by default.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

The expected outer architecture is day-and-Jerusalem judgment (1:1-18), communal summons and nations cycle (2:1-15), corrupt-city woe and failed correction (3:1-7), judicial pivot to purified people and remnant (3:8-13), and Zion song plus restoration close (3:14-20). Candidate surface units must remain linked to those parents. When paragraph pressure conflicts with causal, address, speech, reversal, or song continuity, preserve the larger coherent surface and record the child seam as dissent.

## list_register_function_check

Zephaniah's sweeping creatures, cult practices, city districts, merchants, day descriptors, Philistine cities, nations, animals, leaders, corrupt practices, removed pride, remnant traits, losses, rescued groups, and praise terms function as cumulative judgment, woe, compass, remnant, song, or restoration registers. Preserve each register's rhetorical result; never turn list members, place names, glosses, or roots into independent chunks or total meanings.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable. Zephaniah is prophetic oracle, woe, summons, nations cycle, remnant promise, song, and restoration close rather than an epistle. Argument-movement rules for letters do not override its prophetic and poetic forms.

## over_split_risk_check

Highest risks are separating 1:4-6 indictment members, detaching day slogans, splitting 2:4 from the coast reversal without parent hydration, isolating 2:12 as ethnicity evidence, detaching 3:8 from purification, fragmenting 3:9-13 into proof texts, isolating 3:17, or separating 3:20 from the closing restoration. For tied evidence, keep the larger surface, lower confidence when needed, and preserve the child seam as dissent rather than a default hold.
