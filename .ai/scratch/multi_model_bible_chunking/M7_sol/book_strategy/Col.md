# Colossians candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace all four chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB Colossians coordinate set (95 verses). Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, audience/opponent reconstruction, apostolic authority, Christology, cosmology, angelology, soteriology, baptism doctrine, ascetic practice, household-role authority, slavery, ethics, history, preferred witness, reading, punctuation, hymn-source theory, or speaker.

## faithful_route

Trace prescript; thanksgiving and hope/love report; knowledge/endurance prayer; deliverance/kingdom transition; image/creation/reconciliation poetic or confessional movement; reconciliation application and ministry/stewardship report; struggle/mystery assurance; rooted-walk warning, fullness/circumcision/burial/life and triumph argument; festival/food/Sabbath, humility/angel and ascetic-regulation warnings; raised-life inference; put-to-death/put-off catalogue; new-person unity and put-on catalogue; peace/word/thanksgiving frame; household address through 4:1; prayer/watchfulness and outsider speech; Tychicus/Onesimus mission; greetings/coworker notices; Archippus charge and autograph/grace close. Prefer complete thanksgiving-prayer, poetic/confessional movement plus application, ministry/stewardship, struggle/mystery, rooted-fullness-triumph argument, warning/regulation argument, raised-life inference, vice/virtue catalogue frame, household address, prayer/outside-speech unit, messenger/greeting or epistolary-close unit.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

Koine prescript, thanksgiving/prayer formulas, hina clauses, participial chains, relative-clause and anaphoric structure, image/firstborn/fullness/reconciliation recurrence, gar/de/oun/alla, inferential oun, syn- compounds, rhetorical warning imperatives, vice/virtue catalogues, household-address pairs, travel/greeting formulas and autograph are evidence only. Textual/translation/source/speaker hot zones include Col.1.2, 1.7, 1.14, 1.15-20, 1.18, 1.23-24, 1.27, 2.2, 2.8, 2.11-15, 2.16, 2.18, 2.20-23, 3.1, 3.4-5, 3.11, 3.16, 3.18, 3.22, 4.1, 4.8, 4.13, 4.15 and 4.18. No witness, reading, punctuation, hymn source, quotation extent, speaker, opponent, cosmology, event sequence or history is selected. Roots, etymology, Strong-style tags, morphology and headings are not authority.
