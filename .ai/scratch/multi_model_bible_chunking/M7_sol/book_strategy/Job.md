## faithful_route

Trace the complete dramatic and poetic architecture before judging local seams:

1. Job.1.1-2.13: prose frame, two earthly catastrophe sequences, two heavenly scenes, bodily affliction, wife dialogue, and friends' silent arrival.
2. Job.3.1-3.26: Job's opening curse-lament, moving from cursed birth-night to death-rest longing and present anguish.
3. Job.4.1-14.22: first dialogue cycle: Eliphaz (4-5), Job (6-7), Bildad (8), Job (9-10), Zophar (11), Job (12-14).
4. Job.15.1-21.34: second dialogue cycle: Eliphaz (15), Job (16-17), Bildad (18), Job (19), Zophar (20), Job (21).
5. Job.22.1-27.23: disrupted third cycle: Eliphaz (22), Job (23-24), Bildad (25), Job (26), and Job's renewed discourse in 27. Do not manufacture or reassign a missing Zophar speech.
6. Job.28.1-28.28: wisdom poem on mining, wisdom's inaccessibility, divine knowledge, and the closing fear-of-the-Lord saying. Preserve its speaker ambiguity.
7. Job.29.1-31.40: Job's renewed final discourse: former honor, present humiliation, and oath-of-clearance climax.
8. Job.32.1-37.24: narrative transition to Elihu, Elihu's opening rationale, and four explicitly renewed speeches at 33:1, 34:1, 35:1, and 36:1, culminating in storm and divine-majesty imagery.
9. Job.38.1-42.6: Yahweh's first whirlwind speech (38:1-40:2), Job's short reply (40:3-5), Yahweh's second speech (40:6-41:34), and Job's reply (42:1-6).
10. Job.42.7-42.17: prose epilogue: divine address to Eliphaz, friends' offering and Job's prayer, restoration, renewed kinship, daughters, descendants, and death notice.

These are mandatory parents, not a pre-authorized surface map. Target roughly 65-95 genuine units only as a plausibility range, never a quota. An entire named speech may remain one parent while its independently coherent complaint, accusation, example, hymn, catalogue, oath, or creature tableau surfaces as a child with mandatory speaker-turn and cycle hydration. Prefer the larger coherent movement whenever a smaller unit loses its question, answer, comparison, address, or rhetorical result.
