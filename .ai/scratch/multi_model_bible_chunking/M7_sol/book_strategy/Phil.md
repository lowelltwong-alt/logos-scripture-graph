# Philippians candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace all four chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB Philippians coordinate set (104 verses). Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, imprisonment location, audience/opponent reconstruction, apostolic authority, Christology, soteriology, perfection doctrine, citizenship doctrine, gender/office authority, ethics, financial practice, history, preferred witness, reading, punctuation, hymn-source theory, or speaker.

## faithful_route

Trace prescript; thanksgiving, partnership report and discernment prayer; imprisonment/gospel-advance report; rival-preaching contrast and rejoicing; life/death deliberation and confidence; worthy-conduct appeal and suffering frame; unity appeal; self-emptying/exaltation poetic or confessional movement; obedience, shining and labor/drink-offering exhortation; Timothy commendation and travel plan; Epaphroditus report and commendation; rejoicing warning and rival-confidence catalogue; autobiographical loss/gain and knowing/pressing movement; mature-minded appeal and imitation/citizenship contrast; Euodia-Syntyche and coworker appeal; rejoicing/gentleness/prayer/thought/practice paraenesis; renewed-gift gratitude, contentment and partnership account; greetings and grace close. Prefer complete thanksgiving-prayer, imprisonment report, life/death deliberation, conduct-unity-poetic-example movement, exhortation plus rationale, coworker commendation, rival-confidence/autobiographical argument, pressing/citizenship movement, dispute/paraenesis sequence, gift-partnership account, or epistolary close.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

Koine prescript, thanksgiving/prayer formulas, gar/de/alla/oun chains, men-de contrasts, purpose clauses, rhetorical repetition, syn- compounds, joy/rejoice recurrence, life/death antithesis, politeuesthe language, poetic parallelism in Phil.2.5-11, inferential hoste, imperatives, catalogues, exempla, travel-report formulas, accounting/partnership vocabulary and benediction formulas are evidence only. Textual/translation/speaker hot zones include Phil.1.1, 1.7, 1.14, 1.18, 1.20, 1.27, 2.1, 2.5-11, 2.6, 2.12, 2.17, 2.25, 3.1-2, 3.3, 3.8-9, 3.12, 3.15, 3.20-21, 4.2-3, 4.5, 4.7, 4.10, 4.13, 4.18 and 4.23. No witness, reading, punctuation, hymn source, quotation extent, speaker, opponent, imprisonment location, event sequence, or chronology is selected. Roots, etymology, Strong-style tags, morphology and headings are not authority.

Risks: chapter fallback; splitting thanksgiving from prayer, imprisonment report from gospel outcome, rival motives from rejoicing, life/death alternatives from decision, conduct appeal from unity/example, Phil.2.5-11 from its framing and inference, obedience from shining/labor imagery, travel plan from commendation, warning from confidence catalogue, credentials from loss/gain and pressing, citizenship contrast from 4.1 appeal, named dispute from coworker remedy, prayer commands from thought/practice, gift thanks from qualification/contentment/partnership, or greetings from grace; proof-texting 1.21-24, 2.5-11, 2.12-18, 3.2-16, 3.17-4.1, 4.2-9 or 4.10-20; deciding Christology, salvation, perfection, citizenship, gender/office, ethics, money, imprisonment, opponents or textual readings. Prefer larger units, alternatives, LOW/deferred.
