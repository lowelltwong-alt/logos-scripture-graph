# Haggai corrective literary strategy (T559)

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. This strategy governs the corrective rereview of Haggai under Lowell Wong's literary-only completion ruling. It may identify literary units and preserve retrieval context; it may not decide theology, canon, authorship, date, Persian-period history, priestly law, temple legitimacy, royal or messianic identity, fulfillment, textual-witness preference, or promotion. Agreement inside the M7 mesh is evidence, never authority.

## Inventory and coordinate guard

- WEB inventory: 38 verses, Hag.1.1 through Hag.2.23, each exactly once and in order; chapter counts are 15 and 23.
- The local OSHB and UXLC WLC-family views use the same Haggai chapter and verse coordinates as WEB. Their agreement is correlated, not an independent textual vote.
- WEB wording, WLC paragraph marks, accents, morphology, lemmas, roots, Strong-number hints, dates, titles, and translation footnotes are evidence only and never boundary authority.

## Literary-form routing

Read the book as a sequence of dated word events whose forms include accusation and futility, renewed commission and drought explanation, narrated communal response, encouragement and presence, shaking-and-glory promise, priestly instruction dialogue with prophetic application, retrospective blessing oracle, and a final Zerubbabel address. Test these local forms:

1. dated word-event frame and quoted delay claim;
2. renewed word event, paneled-house accusation, consider-your-ways summons, and futility register;
3. repeated consider-your-ways commission, building command, withheld yield, and drought explanation;
4. leader-and-remnant response, messenger assurance, divine stirring, work report, and dated coda;
5. dated encouragement oracle, former-glory question, threefold be-strong summons, covenant, presence, and Spirit reassurance;
6. shaking-of-creation-and-nations promise resolving in greater house glory and peace;
7. dated priestly-instruction dialogue about holiness and corpse impurity with prophetic application to people, work, and offering;
8. doubled consider-from-this-day retrospective, crop-failure register, foundation-day hinge, and blessing promise;
9. second same-day word event to Zerubbabel, overthrow oracle, servant address, signet image, election statement, and closing speech formula.

These are hypotheses for blind review, not a pre-authorized route. Prefer the larger coherent unit when a date, speech formula, priestly answer, list member, or promise cannot stand without its accusation, commission, response, application, reversal, or mandatory parent.

## Deciding markers and hard seams

- Hag.1.1 gives the first date, word-event formula, named addressees, and saying introduction; 1:2 supplies the quoted delay claim. OSHB pe after 1:2 supports a complete dated opening word. Test 1:1-2 as the surface rather than detaching a syntactically dependent heading.
- Hag.1.3 repeats the word-event formula. The paneled-house question leads to the first consider-your-ways summons and a six-part futility register through 1:6; OSHB pe follows 1:6.
- Hag.1.7 repeats the divine-speech and consider-your-ways formulas. Mountain, wood, and build imperatives lead to pleasure/glory, disappointed expectation, withheld dew and yield, and the called drought through 1:11; OSHB samekh follows 1:11.
- Hag.1.12 changes from oracle to narrated obedience by Zerubbabel, Joshua, and the remnant. The messenger assurance at 1:13, stirring at 1:14, work response, and chronological notice at 1:15 complete the response movement. OSHB pe after 1:14 creates a real date-coda seam, but 1:15 is too dependent to surface without 1:12-14.
- Hag.2.1 opens a new dated word event; 2:2 names the same leaders and remnant. The former-glory question at 2:3 leads to the threefold be-strong/work summons at 2:4 and covenant/Spirit/fear-not reassurance at 2:5. OSHB samekh follows 2:5.
- Hag.2.6 restarts with a fresh divine-speech formula and yet-once-more shaking. Heavens, earth, sea, dry land, nations, treasure, silver, gold, latter glory, and peace continue through 2:9; OSHB pe follows 2:9. Test 2:1-5 and 2:6-9 as dependent encouragement and promise children under the complete 2:1-9 oracle.
- Hag.2.10 gives a third date and word-event formula. The command to ask the priests concerning instruction opens two question-and-answer exchanges at 2:12 and 2:13; Haggai applies their asymmetry to this people, nation, work, and offering at 2:14. Never detach a priestly answer from its application.
- Hag.2.15 begins the first consider-from-this-day-and-backward summons, followed by shortfall, blight, mildew, hail, and failed return through 2:17. Hag.2.18 repeats the consider formula, names the foundation-day threshold, and turns in 2:19 from absent produce to from-today blessing. OSHB samekh follows 2:19. Test 2:15-17 and 2:18-19 as dependent retrospective and blessing children versus the larger 2:15-19 reversal movement.
- Hag.2.20 explicitly says the word came a second time on the same day. Direct address to Zerubbabel at 2:21 develops through creation shaking, overthrow of thrones and chariots, mutual sword, servant address, signet image, election statement, and the final speech formula at 2:23.

## Original-language and translation hot zones

- Repeated date formulas, word-event introductions, this-is-what-Yahweh-of-Armies-says formulas, consider-your-ways refrains, named addressees, question-answer turns, and speech closures are form evidence, not historical or theological authority.
- The syntax and scope of Hag.1.1-2, the pe after 1:14 before the date in 1:15, the covenant/Spirit relation in 2:5, the shaking and nations language in 2:6-9, and the relation between the two consider refrains in 2:15 and 2:18 require explicit counterevidence and confidence calibration.
- The priestly instruction exchange in 2:11-14 uses asymmetrical contact cases. Roots, glosses, ritual categories, and later legal harmonization cannot select a total meaning, doctrine, or boundary; preserve question, answer, and prophetic application together.
- The rendering and referent of the nations' desired or valuable things in 2:7, the temporal force of yet once more, and the servant/signet/election language in 2:23 remain translation and reception hot zones. Do not select a preferred English rendering, person, office, or fulfillment scheme.
- WLC-family pe after 1:2, 1:6, 1:14, and 2:9, and samekh after 1:11, 2:5, and 2:19 are strong paragraph evidence, not automatic surface authority.
- No local primary LXX, DSS, Targum, rabbinic, ANE, Persian-period, or Second-Temple claim may be made unless a qualified, passage-pinned source packet is actually available. Otherwise record the evidence gap.

## Whole-Bible relation guard

Ezra's references to Haggai and Zechariah, Exodus covenant and presence language, temple and glory relations, prophetic shaking imagery, Davidic or servant/signature-ring parallels, and Hebrews 12 reception of Hag.2.6 may be recorded only as canonical context and retrieval risk. They may not decide authorship, chronology, historical reconstruction, dependence, priestly law, temple status, royal or messianic identity, fulfillment, theology, wording preference, or local seams. Immediate date frame, address, speech formula, refrain, question-answer form, causal sequence, reversal, and closure lead.

## Parent hydration and proof-text premortem

Expected mandatory parents to test and record are 1:1-11, 1:3-11, 1:12-15, 2:1-9, 2:10-19, 2:15-19, 2:20-23, and 1:1-2:23. In particular:

- never surface Hag.1.1 as an authorship, date, or office claim apart from the quoted message it introduces;
- never detach the futility list in 1:6, the build command in 1:8, or the drought explanation in 1:9-11 from the house accusation and consider-your-ways movement;
- never surface 1:13 as an unconditional presence slogan or 1:15 as an isolated chronology claim apart from the obedience-and-work response;
- never detach be strong, covenant/Spirit, shaking, treasure, glory, or peace phrases from the 2:1-9 encouragement oracle;
- never surface the priestly answers in 2:12-13 without Haggai's application in 2:14 and the wider 2:10-19 dated movement;
- never detach from-today blessing in 2:19 from retrospective shortfall, failed return, and the foundation-day hinge;
- never isolate the signet image or chosen-servant address in 2:23 from the direct Zerubbabel overthrow oracle, and never use it to settle identity or fulfillment.

## Review and hold discipline

For every proposed decision, three blind roles - Hebrew/textual/versification, prophetic literary form, and canonical retrieval premortem - must supply decision-specific support, counterevidence, and source references. Each scoped challenge receives an author response; a fresh boss rules without forcing consensus; every specialist gets a post-ruling appeal opportunity; all losing alternatives remain append-only.

Accept a boundary when form and seam evidence support it. Hold only a genuinely contested retrieval choice with a specific, answerable human-or-external-AI question. Translation difficulty, date formulas, paragraph marks, later reuse, priestly terminology, or royal imagery lowers confidence only when it materially changes the surface boundary. Do not defer the whole book by default.

## Completion checks

Haggai is complete only when the active candidate has bespoke per-chunk rationales, real literary-form labels, rejected alternatives, honest confidence, decision-local reviews, preserved dissents and appeals, integer indices 1..N, exact 38/38 ordered coverage, passing whole-map, corrective-depth, exact-coverage, and literary-quality validators, and a fresh hash-bound postcheck. Global sidecar installation is not a completion gate under the owner ruling.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

The expected outer architecture is the first dated confrontation and commission (1:1-11), narrated obedience and work response (1:12-15), encouragement and shaking/glory promise (2:1-9), priestly dialogue and retrospective blessing movement (2:10-19), and second same-day Zerubbabel oracle (2:20-23). Candidate surface units must remain linked to those parents. When paragraph pressure conflicts with syntactic dependence, address, refrain, question-answer application, reversal, or promise continuity, preserve the larger coherent surface and record the child seam as dissent.

## list_register_function_check

Haggai's dates, leaders, remnant, futility outcomes, building materials, agricultural losses, drought targets, former-glory questions, be-strong addressees, shaken realms, nations, precious materials, foods, impurity cases, crop shortfalls, blight agents, unproductive plants, kingdoms, chariots, horses, riders, and sword outcomes function as chronological, accusatory, commission, encouragement, priestly-test, reversal, or overthrow registers. Preserve each register's rhetorical result; never turn list members, titles, glosses, or roots into independent chunks or total meanings.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable. Haggai is a sequence of dated prophetic word events, narrative response, priestly instruction dialogue, and promise oracles rather than an epistle. Argument-movement rules for letters do not override its prophetic and narrative forms.

## over_split_risk_check

Highest risks are detaching the 1:1 date from its saying, atomizing the futility or drought registers, isolating 1:13 or 1:15, splitting covenant/Spirit from the be-strong encouragement, detaching shaking/glory/peace phrases, separating priestly answers from prophetic application, isolating 2:19, or surfacing the signet image apart from the final oracle. For tied evidence, keep the larger surface, lower confidence when needed, and preserve the child seam as dissent rather than a default hold.
