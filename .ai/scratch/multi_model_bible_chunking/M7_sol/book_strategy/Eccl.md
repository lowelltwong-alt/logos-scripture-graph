## faithful_route

Read the whole book afresh as framed wisdom discourse, reflective prose, poems, proverb/catalogue sequences, royal experiment report, enjoyment refrains, and closing frame. Prefer the larger coherent movement when a famous saying, refrain, or later reuse would otherwise be detached from its argument. Require a real change in speaker/frame, discourse act, governing question, experiment stage, poetic form, refrain function, addressee, catalogue purpose, or epilogue voice—not chapter numbers.

Test a macro-route of opening frame/motto; observation and experiment cycles; time poem/reflections; oppression, labor, companionship, kingship, worship, wealth, and appetite movements; wisdom/folly and authority/death collections; mortality/enjoyment; risk, youth, aging, motto reprise; and narrator epilogue. This is a docket, not a predetermined map. Children require mandatory parent hydration whenever extraction could conceal tension or counterevidence.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

Use OSHB morphology and UXLC text as correlated WLC-family views, not independent witnesses. Do not treat roots as lexical conclusions, choose vocalization, normalize Hebrew, or select ketiv/qere, witness, speaker, theology, or translation. Exact source-language quotation requires completed collation in both views; otherwise use coordinate-and-function paraphrase.

OSHB and UXLC encode four section markers at 1:11, 3:1, 3:8, and 9:10. They are layout/performance evidence, not automatic seams. The marker at 3:1 follows the time-poem heading while 3:8 closes its paired catalogue; syntax and form must govern. The 9:10 marker may close an exhortation before chance/time observation, but the rhetorical relation must be argued.

The English/Hebrew chapter seam around WEB 5:1 and MT/WLC 4:17/5:1 is a mandatory versification hot zone. Record both systems; neither numbering tradition decides a boundary. Refrains conventionally rendered vanity, under the sun, striving/shepherding wind, and nothing better are cycle/closure evidence only; their roots do not supply one book-wide meaning.

## literary_form_decision_matrix

- Frame/superscription: 1:1 and 12:9-14 require narrator/Qoheleth voice testing; recurring title alone does not merge them.
- Motto/refrain poem: test 1:2-11 as a patterned opening and 12:8 as reprise/hinge; do not isolate either as a slogan.
- Royal experiment: test 1:12-2:26 by shifts from wisdom inquiry to pleasure/projects, wisdom versus folly, legacy, toil, and enjoyment.
- Time poem: preserve 3:1-8 as a paired catalogue unless children are parent-hydrated; test 3:1 as heading, not standalone thesis.
- Reflective argument: group observations by governing question and inference, not every first-person formula.
- Proverb chain: decide whether sayings are independent aphorisms, linked better-than sequences, or evidence within sustained argument.
- Enjoyment refrain: retain the problem/observation that licenses it; never surface as uncomplicated doctrine or promise.
- Wisdom example: preserve problem, observation, outcome, and remembered/forgotten contrast, especially 9:13-18.
- Youth/aging poem: compare 11:7-12:8, 11:9-12:8, and 12:1-8; preserve the aging sequence and motto reprise.
- Epilogue: test 12:9-14 as narrator evaluation/warning/judgment close; split only if each child stays context-safe.

## decision_docket_and_expected_hard_seams

1. `1:1 | 1:2-11 | 1:12`: frame, motto poem, and royal report may be three acts or one hydrated opening.
2. `1:12-18 | 2:1-11 | 2:12-17 | 2:18-26`: real experiment turns, with the royal inquiry as mandatory parent.
3. `3:1 | 3:2-8 | 3:9-15`: heading versus poem, source markers, and work/question reflection.
4. `3:16-22 | 4:1-3`: injustice/death and oppression continue a mortality problem but change observed scene.
5. `4:4-6 | 4:7-12 | 4:13-16`: rivalry/rest, isolation/companionship, and poor-wise-youth kingship.
6. WEB `5:1` versus MT/WLC `4:17`, with worship/vows through WEB 5:7: numbering mismatch must not create a false seam.
7. `5:8-20 | 6:1-12`: exploitation, wealth, enjoyment, failed gift, appetite, and unknown future cross a chapter boundary.
8. `7:1-14 | 7:15-29 | 8:1-9`: better-than sayings, righteousness/wisdom limits, 7:27 voice formula, and authority counsel.
9. `8:10-17 | 9:1-10 | 9:11-18`: delayed judgment, shared death/enjoyment, chance/time, and poor-wise-man example.
10. `10:1-20`: test proverb clusters by shared folly/governance function, not verse atoms or chapter convenience.
11. `11:1-6 | 11:7-10 | 12:1-8`: risk/uncertainty, light/youth, remembered Creator, aging poem, death, and motto reprise.
12. `12:8 | 12:9-12 | 12:13-14`: motto may close Qoheleth or open frame; narrator characterization, warning, and judgment may form one epilogue or hydrated submovements.

Speaker ambiguity at 1:2, 7:27, 12:8, and 12:9 is a hold trigger. No route decides authorship, historical persona, editorial correction, or whether the epilogue approves, limits, or reframes every prior saying. Later biblical reuse and theology illuminate relations but never settle the surface.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

Compare every child with its complete experiment cycle, refrain-bearing argument, poem, proverb cluster, or narrator frame. Under tied evidence keep the larger unit, lower confidence if warranted, preserve the child as a non-authorizing relation, and ask a specific human/external-AI question.

## list_register_function_check

Classify time pairs, project inventory, better-than sequences, social observations, wealth/appetite catalogues, aging images, and proverb chains by function. Split only at a new governing act/form. Lists accumulating one case, experiment, contrast, or poem remain together despite formatting.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not an epistle. Apply analogous wisdom-argument integrity: do not extract a famous aphorism, enjoyment saying, command, or judgment formula from its observation, counterexample, limitation, or frame.

## source_metadata_evidence_only_check

Chapter/verse numbering, paragraph blanks, OSHB/UXLC markers, morphology, lemmas, crosswalks, titles, and labels are observations only. They cannot decide speaker, authorship, preferred reading, theology, canon, or boundary authority. Record unavailable rabbinic, Second-Temple, ancient-version, or ANE evidence as a gap; fabricate none.

## over_split_risk_check

Premortem failures: verse atoms in the time poem/proverbs; enjoyment refrains isolated as promises; severed aging images; every under-the-sun recurrence treated as a seam; chapter 5 numbering treated as literary authority; first-person speech assigned without ambiguity; or 12:8 detached from both contexts. Require rejected alternatives and context consequences for every decision.
