## Greek, textual, translation, and context hot zones

- `1:1-4`: extended prescript syntax, promise/time language, entrusted proclamation, kinship and greeting formula.
- `1:5-9`: purpose clause, `presbyterous`/`episkopon` relation, `mias gynaikos anēr`, household/children wording, qualification list function, trustworthy-word purpose; no office or eligibility policy.
- `1:10-16`: opponent labels, household effect, gain language, Cretan quotation and attribution, rebuke purpose, Jewish myths/commands, clean/defiled conscience, confession/works contrast. Do not endorse ethnic stereotyping or reconstruct opponents/history.
- `2:1-10`: changed group/addressee classes, list-function parallelism, `hieroprepeis`, teaching/training chains, household/status terms, 2:5 textual/translation pressure, master conduct and teaching-adornment purpose. No gender, household, labor, or social policy.
- `2:11-15`: grace-appearance syntax and universal terms, training participles, hope/appearing grammar including `tou megalou theou kai sōtēros`, redemption/people/good-works clauses, concluding imperatives. No doctrinal extraction or preferred translation.
- `3:1-8`: reminder imperatives, civic terms, former/now contrast, kindness/mercy/saving/regeneration/renewal/Spirit/justification/hope clause chain, faithful-saying scope, good-works insistence. Preserve possible confessional form without deciding sacramental or salvation doctrine.
- `3:9-11`: controversy/genealogy/law-quarrel list, avoid formula, `hairetikos anthrōpos`, first/second warning, self-condemnation syntax. No later technical label or discipline policy imposed.
- `3:12-15`: travel/person/request register, Nicopolis/winter wording, legal-expert name/role, provisioning, urgent-needs/good-works purpose, greetings and plural grace. No chronology or biography reconstruction.
