# Isaiah corrective literary strategy — candidate evidence only

This book remains candidate-only and non-authorizing. The rereview is isolated
from M1–M6 maps, comparison synthesis, and T417 layers. Agreement inside the
same-model mesh is evidence, not authority; external-model convergence and
human adjudication remain separate later gates.

## Selected strategy — selected_strategy

Read Isaiah as a prophetic anthology whose local units include book and oracle
headings, covenant lawsuits, vision and commission, sign narrative, woe and
taunt poems, nation oracles, songs, judgment/restoration diptychs, historical
prose scenes, trial disputations, servant and Zion poems, invitations, laments,
communal prayers, and final new-creation/judgment frames. Prefer the larger
coherent form when a proposed child lacks an independent speaker, scene,
syntax, refrain, formula, or functional close. Neither chapters nor later reuse
supplies boundary authority.

## Literature type or mixed genre — literature_type_or_mixed_genre

The book mixes prophetic poetry, prose narrative, symbolic sign reports,
dialogue, juridical speech, taunt, hymn, lament, prayer, invitation, catalogue,
and apocalyptic or cosmic imagery. Every active record must name its actual
local form. A generic prophecy or discourse bucket is not sufficient.

## Literary-form decision matrix — literary_form_decision_matrix

- Superscription or burden formula: test whether it is an independent heading,
  integral opening, or parent for several complete child oracles.
- Vision or sign narrative: retain date, scene, action, dialogue, interpretation,
  and consequence unless a new scene or speech formula supplies a real seam.
- Woe, taunt, or nation oracle: keep its named addressee, accusation, image,
  verdict, and restoration or closure together.
- Song, hymn, lament, or prayer: follow speaker, refrain, stanza movement,
  question-and-answer, remembered history, petition, and doxological close.
- Trial or disputation: retain summons, witnesses, questions, answer, and verdict.
- Historical prose: use time, location, actors, envoys, reported speech, and scene
  resolution rather than chapter coordinates.
- One-verse heading or saying: return only with mandatory parent and sibling
  hydration when the local function is independently defensible.

## Substrate markers considered — substrate_markers_considered

Use canonical WEB wording and paragraph evidence together with OSHB and UXLC
syntax, morphology, accents, qere/ketiv, speech formulas, vocatives, addressee
changes, messenger formulas, date and king formulas, burden headings, woe calls,
in-that-day recurrence, refrains, songs, lists, and scene transitions. OSHB and
UXLC are correlated WLC-family witnesses rather than independent votes.

## Strong evidence discipline — strongs_metadata_considered_evidence_only

Strong tags, lemmas, roots, morphology, and semantic ranges are evidence only.
Roots are not total meaning. They cannot settle speaker or servant identity,
preferred translation, authorship, chronology, source strata, historicity,
fulfillment, ethics, doctrine, theology, canon, or a seam without local form.

## List/register function check — list_register_function_check

Keep sin catalogues, nation and city sequences, idol lists, remnant names,
historical delegations, creation acts, trial witnesses, servant tasks, Zion
children, watchmen, fast-and-justice duties, communal confession, and
new-creation blessings or judgments within the speech or scene that governs
their function. Do not atomize every metaphor, woe, or repeated phrase.

## Epistle-unit check if applicable — epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable. The analogous governing forms are complete oracle, vision,
sign narrative, prose scene, disputation, poem, lament, prayer, and invitation.

## Over-split and under-split premortem — over_split_risk_check

Over-split risks include one child per woe or in-that-day phrase, extracting
famous Immanuel, child, servant, Zion, Spirit, or new-creation verses, dividing
a sign from interpretation, and cutting prose at chapters. Under-split risks
include chapter fallback, swallowing explicit nation headings and burden
formulas, merging several disputations, flattening servant or Zion movements,
and treating broad judgment/restoration themes as one giant unit. Each chosen
seam must record the strongest exact rival and why it lost.

## Sidecar specificity plan — sidecar_specificity_plan

Only low and medium-low decisions enter the three global sidecars. Each row
must identify the exact span, concrete competing treatment, downstream context
risk, active appeal if any, and requested specialist. Confidence and held
disposition are independent axes. Accepted dissent remains append-only without
masquerading as a current unresolved packet appeal.

## Canonical relation discipline

Relations to Torah, Samuel–Kings, Chronicles, Psalms, wisdom, other prophets,
and later canonical reuse may illuminate form and retrieval context. They do
not dictate seams, harmonize narratives, identify a speaker or servant, select
a reading, prove fulfillment, settle chronology or authorship, or authorize
canon and theology claims.
