## faithful_route

Read Malachi as a linked sequence of prophetic disputations and covenant accusations, not as four chapter containers:

1. 1:1-5: superscription hydrated with the love disputation, Edom counterclaim, reversal, and recognition close.
2. 1:6-2:9: priestly honor and polluted-offering dispute, command, covenant-of-Levi comparison, and judicial consequence.
3. 2:10-16: communal covenant-treachery movement, including the foreign-god accusation and the wife-of-youth dispute.
4. 2:17-3:5: justice complaint answered by messenger, coming, refining, purified offering, and judgment witness.
5. 3:6-12: unchanging commitment, return summons, tithe-and-offering accusation, test, blessing, and nations coda.
6. 3:13-4:3: harsh-words/service protest, fearers' counter-scene, treasured possession, discernment, and coming-day contrast.
7. 4:4-6: Moses-and-Elijah remembrance/sending coda with reconciliation and curse-risk close.

These are mandatory parents, not a pre-authorized route. Test approximately 9-12 surface units at real question-answer, address, command, paragraph, action, and rhetorical-result seams. Prefer the larger coherent disputation when a quoted objection, accusation, answer, consequence, promise, or coda cannot stand alone.

## decision_docket_and_expected_hard_seams

- 1:1-5: reject a naked 1:1 metadata chunk. Test whole title-plus-love disputation versus a dependent 1:1 heading and 1:2-5 child.
- 1:6-14: priest address, honor question, polluted-table objection, defective offerings, governor comparison, rejected worship, nations contrast, renewed table accusation, curse, and great-King close form one dispute. Test 1:6-9 and 1:10-14 as dependent children; the samekh after MT 1:13 is counterevidence against silently swallowing 1:14.
- 2:1-9: explicit priestly command opens a new movement. Keep threatened curse, covenant purpose, Levi ideal, priestly knowledge, present turning, stumbling, corruption, and public consequence together. Any 2:1-4 | 2:5-9 split needs the whole parent.
- 2:10-16: test 2:10-12 communal treachery and 2:13-16 wife-of-youth dispute as dependent children. Do not use the difficult 2:15-16 syntax to settle marriage law, Spirit, offspring, hate, divorce, or violence.
- 2:17-3:5: highest-value cross-chapter unit. The justice objection in 2:17 is answered by messenger preparation, coming, endurance questions, refining, restored offerings, and swift witness. Do not detach 3:1 as an identity or fulfillment proof text.
- 3:6-12: test 3:6-7 return frame and 3:8-12 robbery/remedy/blessing dispute versus whole. The `but you say` at 3:7 continues directly into the concrete accusation at 3:8.
- 3:13-18: harsh words and profit-of-service protest lead to the fearers' response, divine hearing, memory record, possession promise, sparing, and discernment. Test 3:13-15 and 3:16-18 as dependent children.
- 4:1-3: WEB chapter edge is not a source seam, but MT samekh after 3:18 and the `for behold` onset create a real dependent day-oracle child. Compare whole 3:13-4:3.
- 4:4-6: Moses remembrance and Elijah sending form a paired coda. Test 4:4 | 4:5-6 only under the mandatory whole coda; never isolate the final curse.

Likely holds are limited to comparably supported retrieval treatments: 1:13/14, 2:12/13, 2:15-16 syntax insofar as it affects surface form, 2:17/3:1, 3:7/8, 3:18/4:1, and 4:4/5. Each hold must ask one exact human or external-AI question and argue both options. Translation difficulty or later reuse alone is not permission to defer.
