# 1 Thessalonians candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace all five chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB 1 Thessalonians coordinate set (89 verses). Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, audience/opponent reconstruction, apostolic authority, election doctrine, persecution history, collective guilt, sexual ethics authority, church office, resurrection doctrine, parousia timetable, wrath doctrine, sanctification doctrine, history, preferred witness, reading, punctuation, or speaker.

## faithful_route

Trace prescript; thanksgiving for work/labor/endurance and reception report; Macedonia/Achaia example and turning/waiting close; arrival and bold proclamation defense; non-flattery/non-greed witness and nurse/parent metaphors; labor, conduct and exhortation; reception/persecution comparison and wrath statement; separation, desire and hope/crown; Timothy mission and concern; Timothy report, joy and return desire; prayer for way/love/strength; conduct/holiness appeal and sexual-conduct rationale; brotherly love and quiet work; grief/hope instruction concerning the dead and coming/meeting consolation; times/seasons and day/night vigilance argument; mutual encouragement; recognition/peace and community imperatives; rapid rejoice/pray/thanks/Spirit/prophecy/test imperatives; peace prayer, intercession, greeting, public-reading charge and grace close. Prefer complete thanksgiving/reception, arrival-defense, nurse/parent/labor movement, reception/persecution paragraph, separation/mission/report/prayer sequence, conduct/holiness rationale, love/work unit, dead/coming consolation, day/night vigilance, community paraenesis, rapid imperatives, or epistolary-close unit.

Textual/translation/speaker hot zones include 1Thess.1.1, 1.3, 1.5, 1.10, 2.2, 2.7, 2.14-16, 2.16, 3.2, 3.5, 3.11, 4.4, 4.6, 4.13-18, 4.15, 4.17, 5.1, 5.3, 5.9, 5.12-13, 5.19, 5.22-23 and 5.27. No witness, reading, punctuation, speaker, opponent, persecution event, collective identity, chronology, timetable or history is selected. Roots, etymology, Strong-style tags, morphology and headings are not authority.
