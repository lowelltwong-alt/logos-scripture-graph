# Proverbs corrective literary strategy — candidate evidence only

This strategy is candidate-only, non-authorizing, and isolated from M1–M6,
comparison synthesis, and T417 layers. Agreement inside the mesh is evidence,
not authority; independent-model convergence and human review remain later gates.

## Selected strategy — selected_strategy

Read Proverbs as a wisdom anthology whose stable outer forms include
superscriptions, parental instructions, personified-Wisdom speeches, named
proverb collections, numerical sayings, royal instruction, and an alphabetic
poem. Prefer a larger coherent instruction or collection when a proposed child
lacks its own scene, speaker, syntax, refrain, numbered form, or functional
closure. Chapter numbers and English headings never supply boundary authority.

## Literature type or mixed genre — literature_type_or_mixed_genre

The book mixes anthology prologue, parental exhortation, invitation and warning
speeches, paired aphorisms, catchword clusters, words-of-the-wise instructions,
royal/court observations, numerical catalogues, prayer, and acrostic praise.
A real form label must name the local function; generic discourse buckets are
not acceptable substitutes.

## Literary-form decision matrix — literary_form_decision_matrix

- Superscription or collection formula: test whether the title is integral to
  the following speech, or functions as a heading over an internal child.
- Parental instruction: retain vocative, commands, reasons, illustration, and
  consequence unless a renewed address or complete new form supplies a seam.
- Aphoristic cluster: require a genuine lexical envelope, repeated comparison,
  speaker frame, or social function; adjacency alone is insufficient.
- Numerical saying: keep the count formula and all counted members together.
- One-verse saying: permit retrieval only when syntactically complete and always
  hydrate it with the named collection parent and nearby siblings.
- Alphabetic poem: retain Proverbs 31:10-31 as one poem.

## Substrate markers considered — substrate_markers_considered

Consider WEB wording and paragraph signals together with OSHB and UXLC syntax,
parallelism, accents, morphology, vocatives, imperatives, conditional clauses,
speaker changes, repeated catchwords, numerical formulas, and alphabetic order.
OSHB and UXLC are correlated WLC-family witnesses, not independent votes.

## Strong evidence discipline — strongs_metadata_considered_evidence_only

Strong tags, lemmas, roots, morphology, and semantic ranges are evidence only.
Roots are not total meaning. They cannot settle a preferred translation,
personification ontology, gender referent, authorship, chronology, ethics,
doctrine, theology, or a boundary without local literary evidence.

## Source metadata evidence-only check — source_metadata_evidence_only_check

The local WEB witness supplies canonical English coordinates. OSHB and UXLC
provide Hebrew-family observations, but their correlation is disclosed. No
local Septuagint Proverbs witness and no pinned rabbinic or Second-Temple corpus
are available; those gaps are recorded rather than simulated. The translation
pressures around qanah at 8:22, massa at 30:1 and 31:1, names in Proverbs 30,
the animals at 30:31, and eshet hayil remain evidence-only hot zones.

## Larger-unit preservation check — larger_unit_preservation_check

Retain operational parents for 1:1-9:18; 10:1-22:16; 22:17-24:22;
24:23-34; 25:1-29:27; 30:1-33; and 31:1-31. Proverbs 8:1-36 is also a
mandatory complete-speech hydration parent for its four internal movements.
These relations preserve context without erasing defensible child forms.

## List/register function check — list_register_function_check

Keep the six-and-seven abomination list, the numerical catalogues in Proverbs
30, body-part portraits, comparison triads, royal instructions, and acrostic
lines grouped by their governing rhetorical function. Do not atomize every
bicolon, but do not use the existence of a larger collection to suppress an
explicit numbered or instruction-level form.

## Epistle-unit check if applicable — epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable. The analogous governing units are instructions, speeches,
aphoristic clusters, named collections, numerical catalogues, and poems.

## Over-split and under-split premortem — over_split_risk_check

Over-split risks include one chunk per verse, famous-saying extraction,
breaking a parental argument before its reason, and returning a child without
collection context. Under-split risks include chapter fallbacks, swallowing the
formulas at 10:1, 22:17, 24:23, 25:1, 30:1, or 31:1, and merging numerical
lists with neighboring counsel. Every selected seam records its rejected rival.

## Sidecar specificity plan — sidecar_specificity_plan

Only low or medium-low decisions enter the three global sidecars. Each row must
name its exact question, competing treatment, consequence, active appeal, and
requested specialist. Medium confidence and held disposition remain separate
axes. Accepted dissent is preserved append-only in the book appeal ledger and
does not masquerade as an active unresolved packet appeal.
