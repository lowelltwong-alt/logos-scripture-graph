# Song of Songs literary strategy — candidate evidence only

Candidate-only, non-authorizing, independent of M1–M6/comparison/T417. Read Song 1:1–8:14 as a lyric sequence of desire, praise, invitation, search, procession, garden and love poems with ambiguous speakers and recurring adjurations. Chapter numbers, supplied English speakers and headings are never boundary authority. Prefer a complete poem, dialogue exchange, search scene, descriptive praise song, invitation or refrain-governed movement; preserve larger parents where speaker or scene seams are disputed.

## Literary-form decision matrix — literary_form_decision_matrix

Use grammatical gender/number and person, direct address, vocatives, imperatives, question-answer, arrival/departure, setting and imagery clusters, repeated adjurations to Jerusalem’s daughters, “until the day breathes,” garden/vineyard imagery, descriptive body-praise sequences, search/find/lose movements and closing calls. These are evidence only; a speaker label or allegorical reading is not a seam.

## Source-metadata evidence-only check — source_metadata_evidence_only_check

WEB headings/speaker labels/layout and OSHB/UXLC lemmas, morphology, accents, poetic markers, qere/ketiv and Strong tags are evidence only. They cannot decide roots-as-meaning, speaker, stanza/scene, gender referent, textual preference, authorship, ethics, interpretation or theology without local lyric/discourse evidence. OSHB/UXLC are correlated WLC-family witnesses.

## Over-split risk check — over_split_risk_check

Highest risks: treating every gender change, line, metaphor, body part or supplied speaker label as a new chunk; separating invitations from responses; breaking search/find scenes; fragmenting body praise catalogues; detaching adjurations from the movement they close; and forcing a continuous drama. Opposite risk is leaving eight chapter blocks and ignoring cross-chapter poems and refrains. When uncertain, retain the larger lyric movement, lower confidence and append the appeal.
