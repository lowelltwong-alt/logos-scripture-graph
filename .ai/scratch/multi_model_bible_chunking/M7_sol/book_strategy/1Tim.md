# 1 Timothy independent literary-chunking strategy — M7_sol

## Scope and authority

This strategy replaces the six chapter-frame fallback placeholders with a candidate-only literary map. It is independent of M1–M6, `comparison/`, and T417. It may identify discourse, epistolary, paraenetic, list, prayer, hymn/confession, charge, warning, household, and closing seams, but it may not decide authorship, date, office policy, gender policy, doctrine, canon, historical reconstruction, a preferred textual witness, or a contested Greek meaning. Agreement is evidence, never authority. Every unresolved material alternative remains LOW and `deferred_human_or_external_ai`.

## Larger literary architecture

Prefer the larger coherent unit when two routes remain plausible. Test at least these parent movements rather than treating chapters as units:

1. `1Tim.1.1-1Tim.1.20` — prescript; stay/charge concerning divergent teaching; law/gospel contrast; autobiographical thanksgiving/doxology; renewed charge and named warning example.
2. `1Tim.2.1-1Tim.3.16` — community conduct: prayer and mediation rationale; gendered conduct/instruction hot zone; overseer and servant/deacon qualification lists; household-purpose statement and confession/poetic close.
3. `1Tim.4.1-1Tim.4.16` — predicted departure and creation/thanksgiving response; instruction and embodied example charge.
4. `1Tim.5.1-1Tim.6.2` — household-age address; widow honor/enrollment/work obligations; elder honor/accusation/discipline/appointment counsel; slave/master instruction.
5. `1Tim.6.3-1Tim.6.21` — false teaching and gain/godliness contrast; direct charge and doxological confession; charge to the rich; final deposit/knowledge warning and grace close.

The map may split these parents only at real form, address, list-function, command, quotation/confession, or epistolary seams. It must also retain each complete parent as an exact larger alternative.

## Boundary markers and list-function rules

- Treat `parakalō`, `prōton pantōn`, `pistos ho logos`, `tauta paragelle`, `to pneuma rhētōs legei`, `presbyterō`, `hosoi`, `ei tis heterodidaskalei`, `sy de`, and `ō Timothee` as discourse evidence, not automatic cuts.
- Keep a command with its stated purpose or ground when the syntax depends on it; do not isolate famous verses.
- Qualification and widow lists are functional wholes unless a changed subject/class, repeated conditional onset, or explicit result/ground supports a seam. Do not atomize every trait.
- Preserve thanksgiving, doxology, and confession/poetic material with the discourse that introduces or applies it unless a complete form and closure justify independence.
- Speaker and quotation shifts must be evidenced; typography and supplied quotation marks have no authority.
- Cross-chapter continuity outranks chapter numbering, especially 2:1–3:16 and 5:1–6:2.

## Greek, textual, and translation hot zones

All items below are evidence-only and must not select a reading or policy:

- `1:3-7`: scope of the infinitival charge, `heterodidaskalein`, genealogies/speculations, and the relation of command, love, conscience, and faith.
- `1:8-11`: conditional law discourse, vice-list grammar, and the relation of law to the entrusted good news.
- `1:12-17`: clause linkage in the autobiographical thanksgiving, mercy rationale, “faithful saying,” and doxological closure.
- `1:18-20`: `tautēn tēn parangelian`, prophetic references, warfare metaphor, conscience/shipwreck, and the named examples; no reconstruction beyond the discourse.
- `2:1-7`: prayer types and objects, universalizing terms, mediation/ransom syntax, and the speaker’s self-description; no harmonization or doctrinal settlement.
- `2:8-15`: addressee scope, `boulomai`, adornment syntax, `hēsychia`, `authentein`, creation/deception argument, pronoun shifts, and `sōthēsetai dia tēs teknogonias`; preserve multiple exact routes and forbid gender/office/policy conclusions.
- `3:1-13`: “faithful saying” attachment, `episkopē`, `mias gynaikos andra`, household analogies, `neophytos`, `diabolos`, `diakonous`, and `gynaikas`; do not decide office identity or eligibility.
- `3:14-16`: household/pillar syntax, confession/poetic lineation, and the `hos/theos` textual hot zone; no witness selection or doctrinal extraction.
- `4:1-5`: reported Spirit speech, last-times framing, agent/source ambiguity, prohibition list, creation/thanksgiving/word/prayer response.
- `4:6-16`: metaphor and imperative chains, `mythous`, training/godliness saying, age/example list, public reading/exhortation/teaching, gift/prophecy/laying-on language; no office-history inference.
- `5:3-16`: shifting widow categories, honor/enrollment/household obligations, age and conduct conditions, imperatives, and case transitions; list function governs.
- `5:17-25`: elder honor, double honor/pay citation, accusation witnesses, rebuke audience, laying-on/hasty participation, health aside, and visible/hidden deeds; preserve the counsel sequence and competing local seams.
- `6:1-2`: yoke/master address and the believer-master contrast; test whether 6:2b closes this unit or opens the following teaching charge.
- `6:3-10`: repeated divergent-teaching language, vice chain, gain/godliness correction, contentment rationale, desire-to-be-rich warning, and money-love maxim.
- `6:11-16`: direct vocative turn, flee/pursue/fight/take/command chain, confession-before-witnesses, appearing horizon, and doxological predicates.
- `6:17-21`: rich-person charge, treasure/future-life purpose, deposit guard, falsely named knowledge warning, plural grace close, and any textual/translation pressure.

## Internal-Bible and ancient-context controls

Use Torah, Prophets, Psalms/Wisdom, Gospel traditions, Acts, other epistles, Revelation, Second Temple material, and later rabbinic or patristic reception only to illuminate form, phrase, list function, prayer, household discourse, citation, or semantic range. No later reuse forces a boundary; no relation establishes authorship, source, historical event, office structure, gender policy, doctrine, canon, chronology, or theology. Record direct literary dependencies separately from analogies.

## T467 and premortem checks

For every candidate seam ask:

1. Does it survive removal of chapter numbers, headings, paragraphing, quotation marks, and supplied speaker labels?
2. Is the apparent seam a new form/addressee/function, or only a topic shift inside a syntactically dependent argument or list?
3. Does a Greek connective, imperative chain, purpose/ground clause, refrain, citation, confession, or epistolary formula support or resist it?
4. Would splitting detach command from rationale, warning from remedy, qualification list from its function, prayer/confession from its frame, or charge from its doxological horizon?
5. Are all exact smaller routes and the complete larger parent preserved with span-specific markers and rejected alternatives?
6. Has the route avoided deciding `authentein`, childbirth/salvation, office/gender eligibility, `hos/theos`, authorship, historical reconstruction, or policy?
7. Does every LOW decision have matching appeal, frontier, atlas, peer, boss, and postcheck evidence?

Highest failure risks are chapter fallback, atomized vice/qualification/widow lists, proof-text isolation, chapter-driven cuts at 2/3 or 5/6, gender/office-policy smuggling, textual-witness selection at 3:16, and generic repeated rationales. The full mesh must challenge each risk, preserve dissent append-only, and retain the larger coherent unit whenever adjudication remains unresolved.

## T467 harness declarations

### literary_form_decision_matrix
Use prescript, charge, prayer, warning, qualification list, household register, confession/doxology, paraenesis, vocative charge, and epistolary close as competing form hypotheses. Require a form onset plus a closure or changed discourse function; topic alone is insufficient.

### larger_unit_preservation_check
For every selected unit preserve its complete macro parent and every exact finer route. If reviewers do not converge, retain the larger coherent parent or child, lower confidence, and defer.

### list_register_function_check
Test whether each vice, qualification, widow, elder, household, or conduct sequence performs one governing list/register function before splitting internal items. Strong's, lemma, morphology, and roots are evidence only and never boundary authority.

### epistle_unit_check_if_applicable
Test prescript, thanksgiving, charge, prayer, body movement, paraenesis, doxology/confession, personal address, deposit warning, and grace close, including cross-chapter continuity.

### source_metadata_evidence_only_check
Chapter numbers, headings, paragraph breaks, supplied quotation marks, speaker labels, source metadata, textual witnesses, and translation layout are evidence only; none independently authorizes a seam.

### over_split_risk_check
Reject proof-text isolation, command/ground separation, list atomization, confession detachment, and a seam supported only by an English paragraph or chapter edge.

### sidecar_specificity_plan
Every LOW decision receives exact-span low-confidence, frontier, and atlas rows containing its marker, alternative, language/translation hold, downstream risk, appeal, requested reviewer, and candidate-only non-authorizing state.
