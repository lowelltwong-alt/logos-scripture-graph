## Selected strategy

Read Deuteronomy as a sequence of attributed covenant addresses whose large rhetorical movements contain retrospective narrative, exhortation, covenant instruction, case law, liturgy, blessing/curse proclamation, covenant renewal, succession, embedded song, tribal blessing, and narrative epilogue. Preserve each speech's argument and appeal, but realize genuine seams at marked addressee, speaker, scene, legal function, ceremony, poem, refrain, commission, or narrative closure. Chapter numbering is never sufficient evidence. Prefer a larger coherent unit whenever a narrower seam would detach a condition, rationale, response, sanction, or liturgical frame.

## Macro reading frames — not automatic chunks

1. Deut.1.1-4.43: superscription and first retrospective/exhortatory address, including Horeb-to-Moab recollection and the refuge-city notice.
2. Deut.4.44-28.68: covenant instruction from Horeb recitation through worship, social, judicial, leadership, humanitarian, ritual, and blessing/curse movements.
3. Deut.29.1-30.20: Moab covenant renewal, warning, restoration, command accessibility, and life/death appeal.
4. Deut.31.1-34.12: succession, Torah deposit/readings, Song of Moses, final commission, tribal blessing, death, and epilogue.

These are discourse parents. They do not authorize chapter-sized rows or erase internal literary movements.
