# Lamentations candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. This strategy governs Sol's independent structural reading of Lamentations and replaces the five-row chapter scaffold. It does not decide theology, canon, authorship, date, historical reconstruction, ethics, divine agency, fulfillment, identity, preferred witness, or a preferred translation.

## faithful_route

Read the received five-poem sequence as a connected lament collection while locating internal movements at sustained voice/addressee changes, lament-to-prayer turns, imperatives, rhetorical questions, acrostic blocks, wisdom reflection, communal response, narrative recollection, and closing petitions. Prefer a larger coherent movement over atomizing each alphabetic stanza. Chapter limits may coincide with complete poems, but chapter coordinates alone are never evidence.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

- Direct Hebrew alphabetic organization is primary form evidence: poems 1, 2, and 4 use successive alphabetic stanzas; poem 3 intensifies the device with three lines per letter; poem 5 retains 22 lines without a full acrostic sequence.
- The pe/ayin order in poems 2-4, the normal order in poem 1, spelling, rare terms, syntax, lineation, and versification are evidence only. They cannot select a textual history or force a seam.
- Traditional descriptions of qinah meter are a testable poetic observation, not an infallible boundary rule. Meter must not override voice, address, syntax, or a complete lament movement.
- MT, LXX, DSS fragments, Syriac, Vulgate, and later Jewish liturgical or rabbinic reception may illuminate wording, order, headings, speaker traditions, and use; none may authorize a preferred witness, Jeremiah authorship, chronology, identity, theology, or a boundary by itself.
- Hebrew roots are not meanings. Wordplay and repeated lexemes require contextual, morphological, syntactic, poetic, and translation checks.
- Strong's identifiers are lexical locators only; they are not meanings and never boundary authority.

## larger_unit_preservation_check

Every proposed seam must name its complete poem parent and the exact larger alternative. Poem 1 must preserve the city-lament and Zion-speech macro movement; poem 2 the divine-destruction, observer-grief, address, and final petition sequence; poem 3 the afflicted-speaker, remembrance, wisdom, communal return, tears, pit-memory, and closing appeal sequence; poem 4 the siege reversal, social/religious indictment, pursuit, and Edom/Zion coda; poem 5 the communal catalogue and direct closing petition. If voice or syntax is disputed, retain the larger movement, lower confidence, and queue it.

## list_register_function_check

Catalogues of losses, body/social reversals, siege horrors, officials, priests/prophets, and communal afflictions are rhetorical accumulations inside lament or prayer. Do not split each item, social group, alphabetic letter, or image into an independent proposition. Preserve catalogue function, escalation, address, and closure.

## epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Not applicable: Lamentations is poetic lament, not an epistle. No epistle salutation, thanksgiving, argument, paraenesis, travel plan, doxology, or benediction template may be imposed.

## source_metadata_evidence_only_check

Canonical WEB coordinates, OSHB/UXLC morphology, translation metadata, headings, later manuscript titles, LXX introductory material, rabbinic/Jewish reception, and modern form labels are evidence or navigation only. They do not decide Jeremiah authorship, date, temple-event reconstruction, redaction layers, community identity, divine-agency ethics, theology, or chunk authority.

## over_split_risk_check

Highest risks are one-verse acrostic atomization, chapter fallback disguised as literary form, isolating 3:22-24 or 3:31-33 from the affliction/reflection movement, separating a signifying image from its address or petition, and forcing certainty at ambiguous speaker turns in 1:11b-12, 1:17-18, 2:11-13, and 3:40-48. Prefer larger units, mark every final seam LOW, preserve exact alternatives, and defer to human or external AI.

## sidecar_specificity_plan

Every LOW decision must project to all three sidecars with the exact span, decision ID, deciding marker, complete poem parent, rejected alternative, Hebrew/acrostic/meter/translation pressure, speaker/addressee ambiguity, premortem risk, and requested reviewer. Appeals remain append-only. Cross-references to Jeremiah, Kings/Chronicles, Deuteronomy, Psalms, Job, Isaiah, Ezekiel, the Twelve, and later reuse are evidence only and cannot harmonize history or force symmetry.

## independent_review_plan

Use at least two blind primaries: (1) Biblical Hebrew/acrostic/textual/translation specialist and (2) lament-poetry/voice/discourse specialist, plus a canonical-relations/premortem challenge when available. A peer crosscheck must challenge every final unit and audit preservation of every overlapping proposal. Sol answers every challenge; the boss may retain larger LOW units but cannot force consensus. Every unresolved objection becomes an append-only appeal. A fresh role-separated postchecker must recompute exact 154-verse coverage, integer indexing, review parity, sidecars, T467 quality, hash bindings, and non-authority guards before a completion receipt.
