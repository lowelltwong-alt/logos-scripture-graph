# Nehemiah literary strategy — candidate evidence only

Candidate-only, non-authorizing, independent of M1–M6/comparison/T417. Read Nehemiah.1.1–13.31
as first-person memoir, report-prayer-response, imperial petition and commission, nocturnal survey,
public appeal, building register, opposition scenes, internal economic crisis, covenant reform,
genealogical/resettlement registers, Torah-reading assembly, historical confession, sealed
commitment, population lists, wall dedication, and final reform memoir. Chapter numbers and English
headings are never boundary authority. Prefer the larger coherent unit where prayer needs its
reported crisis, a petition needs its commission/outcome, opposition needs its defensive response,
a register needs its governing purpose, or a reform needs its narrated implementation and closing
prayer.

## Literary form decision matrix — literary_form_decision_matrix

Use dated memoir superscriptions; messenger report and lament-prayer; court dialogue and letters;
journey/arrival/survey; direct public appeal and communal response; gate-to-gate building roster;
taunt, prayer, plot, defense and work-resumption cycles; complaint, rebuke, assembly-oath and
restitution; governor-practice defense and remembrance prayer; enemy intrigue and prophetic-test
scenes; completion/fear/source report; security appointment and genealogical inquiry; returnee
register; Torah-reading and interpretation assembly; festival implementation; confession-prayer;
sealed covenant names and stipulations; resettlement lots/registers; priest/Levite succession
registers; dedication processions; cultic appointments; and final reform episodes with repeated
“remember me” closures.

## Larger-unit preservation check — larger_unit_preservation_check

Preserve Hanani’s report with Nehemiah’s lament/confession/petition; the royal dialogue with letters
and arrival; nocturnal inspection with public appeal and response; the wall-building register as a
function-governed circuit; mockery-prayer-work and conspiracy-defense-work cycles; economic
complaint through restitution and oath; Nehemiah’s governor-practice defense and prayer; Sanballat’s
successive messages through Shemaiah’s test and the wall-completion outcome; security orders with
genealogical inquiry and the returnee register; Torah reading through interpretation and communal
response; festival discovery through observance; confession through covenant transition; the
sealed names with commitment stipulations; resettlement decision with Jerusalem/Judah registers;
priest/Levite succession through service arrangements; wall dedication with processions,
sacrifices, joy and appointments; and each final reform problem through corrective action and
remembrance prayer while preserving the chapter-wide reform sequence as a parent alternative.

## List/register function check — list_register_function_check

Builders, gates, districts, households, weapons/watches, nobles/officials, returned families,
disputed genealogies, priests/Levites, covenant sealers, obligations, Jerusalem residents, village
settlements, priestly successions, service divisions, dedication participants, storeroom portions,
and excluded/removed persons remain grouped by governing function. Split only when purpose,
constituency, geography, office, scene, speaker, source notice, or closing total changes. Do not
atomize names or infer identity from similar names.

## Epistle unit check if applicable — epistle_unit_check_if_applicable

Nehemiah is not an epistle. Preserve embedded royal letters, open letters, messages, covenants, and
written registers as complete document units within the larger narrative exchange or administrative
purpose they serve; do not detach a message from its response merely because its wording is
document-like.

## Language, translation, textual, and versification gates

The book is predominantly Biblical Hebrew with Persian/Aramaic administrative names, titles,
loanwords, and document terminology; do not invent an extended Aramaic section. Flag without
deciding: dates, royal titles, place and gate names, distances and wall terms; `tirshatha`/governor,
official and military vocabulary; qere/ketiv; repeated builder names and district syntax; mockery,
anger, prayer, curse and divine-agency wording; weapons/watch/work syntax; debt, interest, slavery,
land and restitution language; governor allowances; prophecy, bribery and fear language; wall
chronology; genealogy and purity/exclusion vocabulary; `sepher hayyahas`/genealogical-record
terminology; Torah reading, sense/interpretation, mourning, joy and festival terms; confession and
covenant formulas; mixed first-/third-person voice; tithe, Sabbath, foreign-marriage, language,
priesthood and expulsion vocabulary; and each remembrance prayer. No preferred reading, imperial
chronology, identity reconstruction, ethnic/racial theory, marriage doctrine, legal/cultic
authority, imprecatory theology, providential ruling, or historical reconstruction.

## Source metadata evidence-only check — source_metadata_evidence_only_check

WEB layout/headings/chapters/footnotes and OSHB/UXLC lemmas, morphology, accents, paragraph
markers, qere/ketiv, and Strong tags are evidence only. They cannot decide roots-as-meaning,
speaker identity, textual preference, chronology, authorship/source theory, ethnicity, legal or
cultic authority, theology, or seams without local form/discourse evidence. OSHB/UXLC are
correlated WLC-family witnesses.

## Over-split risk check — over_split_risk_check

Highest risks: isolating Nehemiah’s prayer from the report and royal petition it prepares;
atomizing the wall-building or returnee registers; splitting opposition taunts from prayer,
defense and resumed work; detaching the economic complaint from restitution; fragmenting the
successive intrigue/prophetic-test sequence; separating Torah reading from interpretation and
response; splitting confession from covenant commitment; atomizing covenant names/stipulations or
resettlement registers; dividing dedication processions from communal joy and appointments; and
turning each final reform or remembrance prayer into an isolated doctrinal proof-text.

## Canonical relation candidates

Record evidence-only relations to Torah covenant, debt, Sabbath, festival, priestly/Levite and
foreign-marriage texts; Kings–Chronicles destruction/restoration; Ezra’s returnee register,
commission, confession and assembly; Psalms of Zion and communal confession; prophetic restoration
and wall/city imagery; Daniel/Esther’s Persian setting; and later canonical reuse. Relations cannot
harmonize lists or chronology, define ethnicity, select witnesses, force boundary symmetry, or
authorize legal/cultic/theological conclusions.

## Ancient Jewish and rabbinic context gap

No qualified pinned ancient Jewish/rabbinic corpus is available. Do not simulate it. Route Persian
administration, wall construction and city organization, scribal/Torah-reading practice,
genealogical and priestly status, covenant oath, Sabbath/economic practice, intermarriage/language
terminology, textual history, and later reception to qualified external specialists with chronology
labels.
