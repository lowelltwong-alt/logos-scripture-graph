# 2 Samuel literary strategy — candidate evidence only

Candidate-only, non-authorizing, independent of M1-M6/comparison/T417. Read 2Sam.1.1-24.25 as royal narrative containing messenger reports, lament poetry, accession and civil-war scenes, genealogical and administrative lists, covenant oracle and prayer, warfare and siege reports, court and family conflict, flight/return sequences, wisdom-like forensic speech, succession tensions, census-plague narrative, and altar closure. Chapter numbers are never boundary authority; prefer complete scenes, speech-response units, and cause-to-outcome sequences.

## Literary form decision matrix — literary_form_decision_matrix

Use explicit time/location/actor resets, messenger arrival and report-response closure, embedded lament/song/oracle/prayer, speaker/addressee change, battle phase, enthronement or covenant procedure, court petition and judgment, narrator summary, genealogy/list/register, pursuit/flight/return stage, and death/burial or altar closure. Keep report with response, oracle with prayer, intrigue with consequence, and accusation with answer and ruling.

## Larger-unit preservation check — larger_unit_preservation_check

Preserve Saul/Jonathan death report with David's response and lament relation; Judah and Israel accession/civil-war trajectories; ark transfer with Uzzah episode and completed arrival; Nathan's oracle with David's prayer; Ammon/Aram campaign phases where one supplies the other's outcome; David-Bathsheba-Uriah report with Nathan confrontation and consequences; Amnon-Tamar-Absalom sequence; Absalom exile-return-revolt-flight-battle-death-return; Sheba revolt and its resolution; closing song/oracle/warrior lists as distinct forms within their larger appendix; and census, plague, intercession, altar purchase, and sacrifice as one causal closure.

## List/register function check — list_register_function_check
