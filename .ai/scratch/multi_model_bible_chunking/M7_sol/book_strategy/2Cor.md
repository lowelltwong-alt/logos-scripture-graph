# 2 Corinthians candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace all thirteen chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB 2 Corinthians coordinate set. Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, audience/faction reconstruction, letter-partition theory, apostolic authority, discipline, forgiveness policy, covenant system, atonement, collection ethics, gender/office, history, preferred witness, or preferred reading.

## faithful_route

Trace prescript and consolation/affliction blessing; Asia trouble and deliverance; travel-plan defense, integrity, sealing and spared-visit explanation; grief/letter/forgiveness and triumph transition; ministry commendation, letter/Spirit contrast and unveiled reading argument; treasure-in-jars affliction/hope, earthly/heavenly dwelling, judgment and love; reconciliation/ministry appeal and holiness exhortation; open-heart appeal, grief report and repentance/comfort close; collection example, completion appeal and delegation safeguards; generous-sowing argument, thanksgiving and prayer close; presence/absence defense and warfare/commendation/measure; jealousy, rival-minister comparison and fool’s-speech transition; hardships/escape/visions/thorn/weakness; spending, visit preparations, feared disorders, third-visit warning, self-test and closing exhortation/benediction. Prefer larger coherent travel defense, grief-forgiveness sequence, ministry/covenant argument, affliction-hope analogy, reconciliation appeal, collection argument, delegated-mission unit, apostolic-defense movement, fool’s-speech parent, warning or epistolary close.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

Textual hot zones include 2Cor.1.12/15/20/22, 2.1/10/14/17, 3.3/6/14/17, 4.4/6/13/14, 5.3/10/14/17/19/21, 6.2/14, 7.8/12, 8.4/7/19/24, 9.4/5/10, 10.7/8/12, 11.3/4/6/21/23/32, 12.1/7/9/14/19/21, 13.2/4/5/13. No witness/reading selection. Roots, Strong's, morphology, punctuation, headings and partition theories are not authority.
