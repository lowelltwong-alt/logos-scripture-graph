# 2 Thessalonians candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace all three chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB 2 Thessalonians coordinate set (47 verses). Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, audience/opponent reconstruction, apostolic authority, persecution history, judgment doctrine, collective guilt, parousia/day timetable, rebellion identity, man-of-lawlessness identity, temple referent, restrainer identity, delusion doctrine, election/sanctification doctrine, tradition authority, work/economic policy, discipline authority, history, preferred witness, reading, punctuation, or speaker.

## faithful_route

Trace prescript; thanksgiving for growing faith/love and perseverance report; righteous-judgment/recompense/relief argument and revelation description; worthiness/power/name prayer; coming/gathering and day-not-present appeal; rebellion/man-of-lawlessness sequence; restraint/revelation/destruction sequence; deceptive-sign/delusion/judgment close; thanksgiving and calling/stand-firm exhortation; comfort/strength prayer; prayer request for word/rescue and confidence; heart-direction prayer; command concerning disorder, received tradition and apostolic work example; work rule, busybody report and quiet-work exhortation; doing-good appeal; disobedience marking and sibling admonition; peace prayer, autograph authentication and grace close. Prefer complete thanksgiving/perseverance, judgment/relief argument, prayer, 2:1-12 warning sequence, thanksgiving/stand-firm/prayer, prayer-request/confidence, disorder/work-example/rule/exhortation, discipline/admonition, or epistolary-close unit.

Textual/translation/speaker hot zones include 2Thess.1.1, 1.5, 1.7-8, 1.10, 1.12, 2.1-2, 2.3-4, 2.6-7, 2.8, 2.10-11, 2.13, 2.15, 3.2, 3.6, 3.10-11, 3.14, 3.17 and 3.18. No witness, reading, punctuation, speaker, opponent, event identity, temple referent, restrainer, chronology, timetable, history or theology is selected. Roots, etymology, Strong-style tags, morphology and headings are not authority.
