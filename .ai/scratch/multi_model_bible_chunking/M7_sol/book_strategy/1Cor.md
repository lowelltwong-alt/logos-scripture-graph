# 1 Corinthians candidate literary strategy

Candidate-only and non-authorizing. Replace all sixteen chapter scaffolds across the complete received Protestant/WEB 1 Corinthians coordinate set. Decide no theology, canon, authorship, date, audience faction reconstruction, church discipline, sexuality, marriage, gender/office, sacrament, charismatic hierarchy, resurrection system, ethics, preferred witness, or preferred reading.

## faithful_route

Trace prescript, thanksgiving and appeal; reports of divisions and cross/wisdom argument; minister/field/building/temple metaphors and steward judgment; case report, assembly response and leaven analogy; lawsuits and body argument; “now concerning” marriage/calling/virgins reasoning; food-knowledge-love argument, apostolic-example defense and self-discipline; wilderness examples, idolatry/table argument and conscience applications; worship/head-covering argument and meal rebuke/institution tradition; gifts, body analogy, ranking and greater way; love encomium; prophecy/tongues comparison and ordered gathering; resurrection tradition, objections, Adam/order, baptism/death arguments, body analogy and victory; collection/travel plans, co-workers, exhortations and greetings. Prefer larger coherent report-response, objection-answer, analogy, case, list, paraenetic movement, worship argument, encomium, resurrection proof sequence, travel plan, or closing form.

## original_language_and_textual_evidence

Koine Greek epistolary formulas, peri de transitions, reports and named-source formulas, gar/oun/de/alla chains, rhetorical questions, me genoito-style negation, vocatives, citation formulas, analogy frames, list syntax, men-de contrasts, conditional clauses, quotation/response ambiguity, imperatives and closing formulas are evidence only. Text-critical awareness includes major papyri/uncials, Byzantine/TR/critical-text differences and versional/patristic evidence without selecting a witness. Hot zones include 1Cor.1.8, 1.14, 2.1, 3.4/5, 4.6, 5.5, 6.20, 7.5/34/36-38/39, 8.8, 9.1, 10.9/19/28, 11.2/10/24/29, 12.1/2/9/13/31, 13.3/8/10/13, 14.5/16/19/34-35/38, 15.3/5/29/47/49/51, 16.15/19/22/24 and 14.34-35 placement. Roots are not meanings; etymology, Strong's tags, lemmas, morphology, punctuation and English headings are not authority.
