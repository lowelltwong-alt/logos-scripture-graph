# M7 Sol Whole-Bible Candidate Campaign

Owner authorization: Lowell Wong, 2026-07-21.

Produce Sol's independent, faithful, candidate-only literary/structural chunk map for all
66 books of the Protestant canon. Work one book at a time in `M7_sol`. Let the Bible
interpret the Bible. Prefer the larger coherent literary unit and split only at a real
scene, speaker, form, oracle/vision, stanza/refrain, list-function, argument, doxology,
benediction, or variant gate. Do not decide theology, canon, authorship, inspiration,
preferred translation/reading, or manuscript tradition.

Original-language syntax, lemmas, morphology, roots, Strong's-style tags, WJ/red-letter,
headings, footnotes, variants, editorial cross-references, internal canonical echoes, and
historical/rabbinic evidence are evidence only. Ancient Jewish evidence must distinguish
Second Temple context from later rabbinic reception and avoid anachronistic back-projection.

Every candidate chunk must receive two artifact-blind, role-separated specialist reviews,
a peer cross-check, Sol responses to every challenge, and a role-separated post-resolution
check. These roles share one model substrate unless an attempt receipt proves otherwise and
therefore count as one correlated model voice, not multiple independent model votes.
Material disputes go to a read-only boss/adjudicator. Reviewers retain an append-only
appeal right after a boss ruling. A reasoned unresolved appeal is not forced into
consensus: keep the larger unit, lower confidence, write required sidecars, and mark it
`deferred_human_or_external_ai`.

Do not read another model's chunks, whole map, sidecars, comparison outputs, or T417 model
layers before M7 is complete. Do not run comparison. Do not write reviewed gold,
`data/candidate/chunks`, or `pipelines/chunking`. Agreement and review remain candidate
evidence only.

Per book: write strategy first, author chunks using positive contiguous integer indices,
freeze their digest, conduct the review mesh, resolve or explicitly defer every
challenge/appeal, synchronize final review/hold states, rebuild digest-bound packets and
sidecars, run exact verse coverage, the official whole-Bible chunk-map validator,
review-parity, and literary-quality gates, then write the completion receipt last.
