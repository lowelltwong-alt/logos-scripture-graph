# CSNTM 1,908-record rights-gated acquisition

This is a controlled long-run campaign specification. It is not an authorization to crawl or download CSNTM.

The terminal outcome is a verified NAS collection of every metadata record and image that is demonstrably licensed for the requested effect, plus a complete, holder-grouped permission queue for everything else. Completeness never overrides CSNTM or holder rights.

Execution order:

1. Bind written CSNTM bulk catalog/API permission and capture the authorized interface.
2. Inventory 1,908 records without image retrieval.
3. Classify every record by holder, upstream source, license, and requested use.
4. Acquire only authorized sources into staging, verify them, then promote write-once originals.
5. Produce residual and permission queues; stop rather than infer rights.

The authoritative machine-readable contract is `campaign.json`.
