## Prevention

Future comparisons must preserve `disagreement_region`, all `span_observations_by_model`, each span's relation to the region, and an explicit `candidate_span_origin`. Full chapter envelopes receive no confidence credit without literary identity evidence. Manifest/progress contradictions fail closed. Before any owner packet, an exact overlap check against existing reviewed gold and output is mandatory.

The preferred workflow is a strong proposer, an independent strong critic, Codex reconciliation, frontier review for hard cases, an explicit owner gate, and route-isolated output proof. A good checking and correction system is more realistic than perfect first-pass chunking, but it does not excuse preventable harness defects.

No reviewed gold, chunk output, child spans, route/evaluator behavior, graph/retrieval/vector truth, source-tradition choice, canon change, or theology authority is authorized.
