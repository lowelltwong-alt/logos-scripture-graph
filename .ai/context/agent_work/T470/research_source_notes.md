# T470 Research Source Notes

T470 records a transparent evidence rubric for chunking research. It does not
authorize reviewed gold, chunk output, child spans, target selection, route or
evaluator behavior, graph/retrieval/vector truth, canon changes, preferred
readings, source-tradition choices, variant/inspiration decisions, or theology
authority.

## Method

The source pass focused on references that help future agents distinguish what
is well-supported, how a conclusion was reached, what remains debated, and what
downstream chunking implication follows. This is not a full literature review.
It is a control-plane transparency pass for future owner dockets and specialist
packets.

## Transparent Conclusions

- Well-supported: use discourse/literary units, make boundary rationale visible,
  treat editorial/reference layers as evidence-only, use model agreement only as
  candidate evidence, and add near-boundary/WindowDiff-style diagnostics.
- Debated: Mark 16:9-20, John 7:53-8:11, Romans 16 doxology placement,
  Deut 32:8-9, ancient delimitation inferences, exact Gospel pericope seams,
  and how much model agreement lowers review risk.
- Project-scoped faithfulness principle: let the Bible interpret the Bible where
  reasonably possible. This is owner-approved for this repo and must remain
  transparent, not smuggled theology.
