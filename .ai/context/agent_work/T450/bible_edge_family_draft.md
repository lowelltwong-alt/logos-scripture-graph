# T450 Bible Edge Family Draft

This draft is non-authorizing. It records the first-pass family map for future review by Codex, frontier models, and the owner.

## Family Summary

| Family | Default Risk | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structural source / occurrence | Low to medium | Canonical containment, adjacency, token occurrence, translation rendering |
| Editorial metadata | Medium | Edition cross-references, notes, headings, paragraph and poetry markers |
| Intertext / canonical | Medium to high | Quotes, allusions, echoes, parallels, repeated phrases, symbols |
| Prophecy / apocalyptic | High | Oracles, fulfillment, typology, Daniel/Revelation symbols |
| Orthodox creedal theology | High | Trinity, Christology, salvation, church, sacraments, doctrinal pressure |
| Covenant / biblical theology | High | Promise, sign, mediator, blessing, curse, new-covenant development |
| Sacrifice / temple / priesthood | Medium to high | Offerings, purity, atonement, sanctuary objects, priestly roles |
| Literary / discourse / genre | Medium to high | Genre, argument flow, speech, lament, acrostic, wisdom, genealogy |
| Chronology / calendar / geography | Medium to high | Feasts, Sabbath, new moon, lunar calendar, regnal years, routes |
| Linguistic / original language | Medium to high | Lemma, morphology, syntax, Strong's overlay, translation rendering |
| Manuscript / textual witness | High | Witnesses, variants, source traditions, punctuation/editorial layers |
| Historical / cultural / reception | Medium to high | Background, offices, empires, customs, early creed patterns |
| Apologetic / polemic | High | Objection response, false teaching, idolatry contrast, defense of claims |

## High-Risk Escalation Defaults

Escalate to frontier review before any owner gate for:

- prophecy, apocalyptic, Daniel, Revelation, Olivet discourse, temple/city symbols
- Trinity, Christology, incarnation, resurrection, law/gospel, justification, covenant-system pressure
- WJ or speaker attribution, especially John 3, John 13-17, Revelation voice shifts
- textual variants, source tradition, preferred reading, canon-scope pressure
- Job or Song speaker identity
- apologetic resolution of alleged contradictions
- anti-Jewish, anti-denominational, or caricature-prone polemic

## Non-Authority Repetition

No family in this draft authorizes graph edges, predicate registry expansion, retrieval truth, vector truth, embeddings, indexes, boundary imports, canon changes, preferred readings, theology authority, or apologetic conclusions.
