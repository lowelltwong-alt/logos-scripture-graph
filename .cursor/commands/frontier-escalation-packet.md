# Frontier Escalation Packet

Use this command when a hard case needs Claude Opus or equivalent frontier review.

## Mandatory Triggers

Stop local authority movement and prepare an escalation packet for:

- prophecy or oracle boundary
- apocalyptic or vision cycle
- dense epistle argument
- Gospel WJ or speaker boundary
- textual variant or source-tradition pressure
- doxology or benediction boundary
- Christology, Trinity, covenant, law/gospel, ecclesiology, office, sacramental, election,
  assurance, faith/works, or eschatology pressure
- original-language claim controls the boundary
- source metadata would become authority
- low confidence or blocked claim

## Packet Fields

Include:

- task id
- book
- OSIS ref or span
- issue type
- evidence refs
- current confidence
- why Cursor/Codex cannot decide
- possible downstream consequence
- non-authorizing status
- exact question for the frontier reviewer

Frontier review may recommend a path or required changes. It cannot authorize target selection,
reviewed gold, chunk output, route/evaluator behavior, graph/retrieval/vector truth, or theology
authority.
