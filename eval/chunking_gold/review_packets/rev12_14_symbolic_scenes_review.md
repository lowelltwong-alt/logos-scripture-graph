## Hermeneutic-Neutrality Constraint

Future Revelation chunking must preserve orthodox interpretive possibilities without making the
chunker choose among them. A chunk boundary, label, parent unit, route, evaluator check, or graph
edge must not decide:

- linear or non-linear chronology;
- premillennial, amillennial, postmillennial, preterist, historicist, futurist, or idealist readings;
- whether the seven churches are only historical local churches, enduring church patterns, or both;
- whether `Rev.12-Rev.14` is a standalone cycle, interlude, recapitulation, or chronological unit;
- exact symbolic identities for the woman, dragon, beasts, 144,000, Babylon, harvesters, or marked
  worshipers;
- how Daniel cross-references should control Revelation structure.

The minimum faithful chunking stance is descriptive and text-local: preserve observable canonical
scene, speech, hymn, proclamation, judgment, and transition signals while keeping theological
interpretive overlays separate from chunk authority. Daniel and other canonical cross-references may
be recorded later as evidence or graph candidates, but they must not become hidden authority for
Revelation chunk boundaries.
