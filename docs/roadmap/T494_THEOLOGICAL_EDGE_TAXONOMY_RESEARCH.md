# T494 Theological Edge Taxonomy Research

T494 defines research families, not predicates or graph edges. It creates no candidate rows, canonical claims, registry changes, retrieval/vector indexes, doctrine selections, preferred readings, or boundary imports.

The families cover argument grounding; Christological, covenantal, pneumatological, ecclesial/sacramental, soteriological, eschatological/apocalyptic, and lexical phrase-context pressure. Each future research record must carry exact scope, evidence, provenance, confidence, review status, competing options, limitations, and a non-authority label.

The review ladder uses Ezra for inventory, Priscilla for synthesis, Luke for independent evidence checking, Nathan for authority-leakage red-team review, and Lowell Wong for any decision or deferral. No family may become a predicate, edge, doctrine claim, fulfillment/typology assertion, preferred reading, or retrieval feature without a later owner packet and separate implementation authorization.

Unresolved questions remain questions: which family may first reach an owner packet, and whether any family should ever map to an existing predicate rather than remain dossier-only.
