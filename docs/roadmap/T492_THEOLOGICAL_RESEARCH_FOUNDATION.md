T492 is a governed research-and-architecture foundation. It creates no theology authority, canonical doctrine claims, graph edges, retrieval/vector indexes, Scripture changes, preferred readings, source-tradition choices, or boundary-literature imports.

Church Fathers, early-Christian literature, councils, creeds, reception history, and theologian writings must not enter canonical Scripture records; future source planning routes to `logos-boundary-literature`. “Who built theology on whom,” denominational development, and doctrine genealogy route only to a future, governance-registered `logos-doctrine-genealogy` repository.
