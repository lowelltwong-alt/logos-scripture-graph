## Operating rules

- Send only from an account and in the name of the actual project owner/requester. A student account may support lawful library access, but it does not grant a right to copy, retain, share, OCR, or use data with AI.
- The default request is **private, noncommercial research**. Do not use, download, scrape, screenshot, OCR, transcribe, or upload materials to an AI service before a written response expressly permits the relevant act.
- The sender must fill the bracketed identity fields before sending. Do not claim institutional affiliation, a publication, a grant, or a security practice that is not true.
- Permission is source- and holder-specific. A response from an aggregator does not replace the holding institution's permission where the holder says both are required.
- Keep each written grant, terms URL, supplied-file checksum, attribution language, and expiry/deletion condition in a rights ledger before any acquisition.

## Common requested-rights schedule

All drafts below request an explicit yes/no or modified terms for each item.

1. Delivery or approved download of specified digital images/text/metadata, preferably a stable IIIF manifest plus the highest research-appropriate source files.
2. Local encrypted storage and a limited backup for the named research project and named project personnel.
3. Private scholarly viewing, cataloguing, page/folio metadata, and citation/attribution retention.
4. Non-destructive image analysis: checksums, feature/layout extraction, page segmentation, deskewing, color normalization, and comparison.
5. OCR/HTR and locally retained, clearly labeled, non-authoritative transcription candidates.
6. Computational analysis, including local search and local embeddings/vector indexes. Any cloud-hosted or external AI processing is **excluded unless separately approved in writing**.
7. Retention of derived metadata only: hashes, folio IDs, coordinates, layout features, citation links, and non-textual analytical annotations.
8. No public display, redistribution, sublicensing, publication, or commercial use unless a separate written license expressly permits it.
9. Required credit line, watermark/notice preservation, citation format, reporting, term, deletion/return obligations, access controls, fees, and any further conditions.

## Patristic scope for the BiblIndex / critical-edition lane

Request a corpus-level license or partnership for: Apostolic Fathers; Justin; Irenaeus; Tertullian; Clement of Alexandria; Origen; Hippolytus; Cyprian; Eusebius; Athanasius; Basil; Gregory Nazianzen; Gregory Nyssa; Cyril of Jerusalem; Epiphanius; John Chrysostom; Cyril of Alexandria; Theodoret; Hilary; Ambrose; Jerome; Augustine; Ambrosiaster; Ephrem; Aphrahat; and the associated homilies, commentaries, scholia, catenae, biblical prefaces, canon-list discussions, and citation/allusion indexes.

This is evidence for historical reception and textual transmission. It is not a license to treat patristic citation text as canonical Scripture or to claim a mechanically exact reconstruction of the full Bible. Quotations may be partial, paraphrastic, translated, harmonized, or tied to a different textual tradition.
