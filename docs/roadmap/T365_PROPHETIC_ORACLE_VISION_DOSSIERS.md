# T365 Prophetic Oracle Vision Dossiers

## Purpose

T365 creates a research-only queue for prophetic oracle, vision, servant-song, temple-vision,
symbolic-action, judgment/hope, and day-of-Yahweh review cases:

```text
.ai/control/prophetic_oracle_vision_dossier_queue.yaml
```

The goal is to preserve oracle form, vision sequence, prophetic book frame, judgment/hope turns,
source metadata, and canonical-echo evidence without letting those features decide fulfillment
theology, eschatological timelines, covenant systems, Israel/church relations, messianic
identification, temple theology, reviewed gold, graph edges, retrieval truth, or chunk boundaries.

## Research Cases

The initial queue records:

- `Isa.40-Isa.55`, including `Isa.52.13-Isa.53.12`: servant and comfort oracle complex.
- `Jer.30-Jer.33`, including `Jer.31.31-Jer.31.34`: restoration and new covenant complex.
- `Ezek.40-Ezek.48`: temple, land, city, and river vision.
- `Ezek.36-Ezek.37`: restoration, Spirit, and dry bones vision.
- `Dan.7-Dan.12`: prophetic-apocalyptic vision sequence.
- `Hos.1-Hos.3`: sign-act and covenant metaphor complex.
- `Joel.2`: day-of-Yahweh, Spirit, and restoration oracle.
- `Zech.1-Zech.6`: night vision sequence.

## Non-Authorization

T365 does not authorize:

- fulfillment theology system selection
- eschatological timeline or chronology selection
- covenant system selection
- Israel/church relation selection
- servant-song referent or messianic identification
- temple theology or millennial temple view
- Pentecost/day-of-Yahweh fulfillment timing
- oracle formulae as chunk-boundary authority
- vision reports as chronology authority
- canonical echoes, cross-references, or source metadata as graph or retrieval truth
- reviewed-gold promotion
- chunk boundaries
- route behavior
- evaluator changes
- graph edges
- retrieval truth
- output changes
- boundary import
- T345

Future output-changing use of any dossier still requires exact passage scope, owner review,
reviewed gold or equivalent governed evidence, non-target identity proof, validators/tests, and a
later implementation-authorizing decision.

## Unintended Consequence Review

Confirmed risks:

- None of the T365 surfaces change raw data, canonical data, generated chunks, evaluator scoring,
  route behavior, reviewed gold, graph edges, retrieval output, or vector/index output.

Plausible risks:

- The phrase "servant song" could be read as a messianic-identification decision.
- The phrase "day of Yahweh" could be read as an eschatological timeline decision.
- Temple-vision labels could be read as selecting a literal, symbolic, realized, or millennial
  temple view.
- Daniel/Revelation or Joel/Acts echoes could be read as graph-edge or fulfillment authority.

Mitigation:

- The queue and CD-031 deny fulfillment, eschatology, covenant, Israel/church, messianic, temple,
  graph, retrieval, reviewed-gold, route, evaluator, output, and boundary-import authority.
- Future implementation still requires exact owner-reviewed gold or equivalent governed evidence.

Owner decisions needed:

- None for this non-output-changing research queue.
- A later owner decision is required before any prophetic/oracle/vision output-changing work.

## Validation

T365 adds:

- `scripts/validate_prophetic_oracle_vision_dossier_queue.py`
- `tests/test_prophetic_oracle_vision_dossier_queue.py`

The validator fails closed if the queue authorizes fulfillment theology, eschatology, covenant
systems, Israel/church conclusions, messianic identification, temple theology, output changes,
chunk boundaries, route behavior, graph edges, retrieval truth, reviewed gold, or boundary import;
loses required evidence channels; drops required dossiers; or omits non-authorization guards.
