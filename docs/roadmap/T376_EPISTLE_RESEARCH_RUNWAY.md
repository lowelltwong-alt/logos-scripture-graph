# T376 Epistle Research Runway

T376 records owner selection of `T376-A`: continue the epistle argument lane as review/prep only.
The machine-readable control record is `.ai/control/t376_epistle_research_runway.yaml`.

The key lesson is: Research autonomy is not authority autonomy. Agents may keep compiling
non-output-changing evidence and options without stopping at every research step, but they must
stop before exact target selection, reviewed-gold promotion, child spans, implementation, output,
route/evaluator changes, graph/retrieval/vector truth, boundary import, preferred readings,
source-tradition preference, canon-scope change, or denominational systematic theology as chunk
authority.

T376 therefore points the next route to `T384`: an epistle argument research/options matrix. T384
should research all serious faithful target options first, including at least:

- `Eph.1.3-Eph.1.14`
- `Rom.9.1-Rom.11.36`
- `Heb.7.1-Heb.10.39`
- `Gal.2.15-Gal.3.29`
- `Jas.2.14-Jas.2.26`
- `1Cor.11.17-1Cor.14.40`

For each option, T384 must record repercussions, downstream theological risk, contextual reading
requirements, source-metadata evidence-only handling, original-language phrase/context needs where
used, textual-variant/source-tradition sensitivity, Orthodox Hermeneutic Firewall compliance, and
the non-authorizations that keep research from becoming authority.

T376 does not select an exact target. The likely first option to compare later is
`Eph.1.3-Eph.1.14`, because it is compact and high-value, but that recommendation is not an owner
selection.

No chunk output changes, reviewed-gold promotion, route/evaluator behavior, graph/retrieval/vector
truth, child spans, boundary import, or whole-Bible output are authorized by T376.
