# T379 Textual-Critical Case Policy Selection

T379 records the owner selection of `TCP-T378-B`: case-by-case owner policy before each
variant-sensitive promotion.

Future variant-sensitive reviewed-gold promotion must record:

- exact variant-sensitive refs;
- source surface and provenance;
- boundary dependency or non-dependency;
- reviewed-gold dependency or non-dependency;
- owner confirmation;
- decision-register update;
- validators/tests.

source-tradition preference, variant dependency finding, reviewed-gold promotion, graph/retrieval
truth, route/evaluator behavior, implementation, chunk output, or output change.
