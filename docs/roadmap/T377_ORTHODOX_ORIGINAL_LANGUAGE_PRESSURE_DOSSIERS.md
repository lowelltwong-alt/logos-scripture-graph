# T377 Orthodox Original-Language Pressure Passage Dossiers

T377 adds `.ai/control/orthodox_original_language_pressure_dossier_queue.yaml` as a non-output-changing
queue for passages that need Greek/Hebrew, morphology, syntax, grammar, translation-divergence, and
orthodox-boundary review before they are used in chunking, graph, retrieval, route, evaluator, or
reviewed-gold decisions.

This is research memory, not research completion. Future packets must do the actual source work,
cite their evidence, preserve canonical context, and remain inside the Orthodox Hermeneutic
Firewall and textual-critical policy gates.

The queue starts with pressure dossiers for:

- `GEN1_26_ELOHIM_US_IMAGE`

- `PS110_1_YHWH_ADONI_MESSIANIC`

- `ONECOR15_29_BAPTISM_FOR_DEAD`
- `ONEPET3_18_4_6_SPIRITS_PRISON_DEAD`

These are queued because pressure often comes from LDS extra-canonical claims, Watch Tower/New
World Translation rendering differences, anti-Trinitarian arguments, divine plurality/unity
claims, or translation-divergence arguments. Naming a pressure source does not authorize it.

## Future Packet Rules

Future original-language pressure packets must:

- Start from canonical Scripture in the canonical 66.
- Record exact passage scope and preserve enough context to avoid isolated proof-texting.
- Separate Greek/Hebrew lemma, morphology, syntax, and grammar evidence from doctrine authority.
- Separate translation-divergence evidence from translation preference.
- Treat LDS, Watch Tower/New World Translation, and other non-orthodox sources as pressure labels only.
- Deny graph edge, retrieval truth, reviewed-gold, chunk-boundary, route/evaluator, vector, canon, boundary-import, and output authority unless a later explicit owner gate authorizes it.
- Record non-target identity requirements before any implementation proposal.
