# T468 Owner Faithful-Route Chunking Policy

## Summary

T468 records the owner's approved faithful-route chunking decisions after the
T464 comparison, T465 reconciliation gate, and T467 harness hardening. It turns
the decision conversation into durable control-plane policy for future review
packets. It is non-authorizing policy only.

The first principle is: **let the Bible interpret the Bible wherever reasonably
possible**. Chunking should follow the Bible's own literary form, discourse
movement, speaker/scenario context, canonical cross-textual signals, and
textual-transmission evidence before retrieval convenience or model preference.

## Owner Decisions

1. Default to larger coherent biblical units. Split only when the text gives a
   structural reason such as a speaker change, scene change, oracle/vision shift,
   stanza/refrain, list sub-unit, argument movement, doxology/benediction, or
   variant gate.
2. Keep whole functional lists, genealogies, ritual procedures, registers,
   allotments, censuses, and similar units together unless the text marks
   sub-units.
3. Preserve poems, psalms, laments, songs, or clear stanza movements. Do not
   split poetry merely for retrieval pressure; use later child spans if a
   reviewed parent needs retrieval refinement.
4. Chunk prophecy by oracle, vision, symbolic scene, audience shift,
   judgment/restoration movement, or fulfillment unit. Daniel, Revelation,
   major vision cycles, and unclear oracle seams require frontier review.
5. Preserve Gospel scene and discourse units. WJ/red-letter metadata is evidence
   only; it does not create boundary authority by itself.
6. Chunk epistles by argument movement and epistolary function. Romans,
   Corinthians, Hebrews, and dense epistle arguments require Codex/frontier
   review.
7. Defer output in variant and source-tradition hot zones until a transparent
   packet exists. Do not silently normalize disputed text.

## Hard Exceptions

Do not downgrade these cases merely because models agree:

- Mark 16 longer ending
- John 7:53-8:11
- Romans 16 doxology
- Deut 32:8-9
- Jeremiah source-tradition structure
- Daniel/Esther additions routing
- 1 John 5:6-8
- Dense epistle arguments such as Romans, Corinthians, and Hebrews
- WJ/speaker-heavy Gospel spans
- Revelation and Daniel vision cycles

## Non-Authorizations

T468 authorizes no reviewed gold, chunk output, child spans, target selection,
route/evaluator behavior, graph/retrieval/vector truth, source-tradition choice,
canon change, variant/inspiration decision, DAD authority, or theology authority.

Later work must still pass exact owner target selection, reviewed-gold promotion,
route isolation, non-target identity proof, and an output-changing task if a
candidate is eventually promoted.
