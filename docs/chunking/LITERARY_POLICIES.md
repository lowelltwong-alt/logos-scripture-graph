# Literary Policies

## Psalms

Default: whole psalm as primary chunk unless the psalm is long. Preserve superscription with the psalm.

Long psalms may split by stanza/acrostic unit if supported by markers or curated boundaries.

## Proverbs

Default: saying or saying cluster. Avoid isolating a proverb when surrounding sayings create an intentional collection or contrast.

## Narrative

Default: scene or episode. Keep speaker, action, and consequence together.

## Law

Default: legal unit, case law unit, ritual instruction unit, or covenant section.

## Prophets

Default: oracle, woe, vision report, symbolic action, judgment/salvation unit.

## Gospels

Default: pericope, parable, miracle story, discourse unit, or passion/resurrection scene.

## Epistles

Default: argument unit, exhortation unit, thanksgiving/opening/closing unit. If a chunk starts with "therefore," attach required prior context.

## Revelation

Default: vision cycle, oracle, seal/trumpet/bowl sequence, worship scene, or final vision unit.
