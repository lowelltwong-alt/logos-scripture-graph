# Contributor Research Map

Logos Scripture Graph needs contributors from several knowledge families. The project is not only a Bible text database and not only an AI retrieval experiment. It is trying to build a governed evidence layer where manuscripts, editions, canon scope, source-language data, material culture, commentary, and computational tools can be connected without confusing evidence with authority.

## Where Contributors Fit

| Contributor family | Useful work |
|---|---|
| Manuscript and library people | Identify sources, holders, shelfmarks, rights, IIIF manifests, provenance, and attribution. |
| Textual critics | Help model witnesses, variants, apparatus evidence, and what claims cannot be made from a manuscript alone. |
| Hebrew Bible / DSS specialists | Help separate biblical scroll evidence, Qumran context, sectarian/non-biblical material, and rights constraints. |
| Septuagint specialists | Help map Greek Old Testament witnesses, deuterocanonical material, translation tradition, and Greek canon complexity. |
| Patristics scholars | Help connect early Christian quotations and reception history without pretending they reconstruct every word automatically. |
| Archaeologists and epigraphers | Help keep material-culture claims grounded: date, place, inscription, artifact, site, and confidence. |
| Digital humanities engineers | Help with IIIF, TEI, EpiDoc, OCR/HTR, manifests, linked data, review workflows, and reproducible pipelines. |
| Reviewers | Look for boundary mistakes: canon scope, preferred readings, rights gaps, OCR confidence, graph/retrieval overclaiming. |

## What We Need To Learn

The project tracks three kinds of knowledge:

- Known knowns: source-backed facts and owner decisions.
- Known unknowns: named gaps, such as missing permission or unmapped folio coverage.
- Unknown unknowns: blind spots that a different field would catch.

The goal is not to make AI sound confident. The goal is to make the project honest enough that experts can attach their knowledge to it.

## What We Are Not Doing Automatically

Contributor work does not automatically change the Bible text, canon scope, preferred readings, graph truth, retrieval truth, or theology. It creates evidence packets for review.
