# 1 Corinthians — M9 research candidate

This is one book in an unfinished open-source Bible knowledge-graph research project. As of 2026-10-07, M9 has 13 of 66 books accepted as reviewed local candidates, totaling 365 units. Fifty-three books remain and none is scholar-ready. This page documents the research state; it is not a finished knowledge graph or a release of the source-bearing bundle.

## What was completed

The accepted 1 Corinthians candidate has 27 units covering all 437 admitted native verse coordinates and 6,812 printed Greek word occurrences. Three source-first primary proposals were frozen separately, followed by language, literary, textual/history and canonical/faithfulness review, author responses, fresh adjudication of material disagreements, root reconciliation and final semantic and engineering checks. Original proposals and changed operative fields remain separately preserved.

All 218 alternative identities remain available: 205 appeal wrappers and 13 records whose geometry coincides with selected units. Coincident geometry does not resolve their arguments or waive review rights. The candidate retains 399 full records and all unresolved holds and dependencies. The source-preserving local acceptance does not mean every interpretation is settled.

Annotation correspondence, Unicode/apostrophe differences, the critical-sign case, coverage limits and source-specific rights remain distinct from the printed text. Chunk selection grants no new annotation or redistribution qualification.

M7, M8 and M9 are separate development lines. Their outputs can inform future method comparison and convergence, but this page does not claim a completed cross-generation comparison, a randomized A/B/C experiment or an independently established best method. Match book code, source revision, native coordinates and exact candidate identity; never compare files by completion order alone.

## Decisions still open to challenge

The full candidate preserves evidence, counterarguments, alternatives, confidence qualifications, missing evidence and appeal paths. Examples needing careful review include the scope of counsel and ellipsis in chapter7; the connected argument across8.1–11.1; speech, setting and contrary evidence across chapters11 and14; the hinge at12.31 and its relation to chapters13–14; and resurrection argument structure and disputed interpretation in chapter15. This list is illustrative, not a replacement for the complete holds.

The split at12.31 is a modest preference. Joined structures and a split at13.1 remain serious alternatives. The15.11/15.12 division is subordinate to the whole chapter, and its joined rival remains accessible. Boundaries do not decide original readings, speaker attribution or doctrine.

Required contexts accompany units and alternatives. Graph edges named supports identify retained evidence-locator associations; they do not prove an interpretation or causal impact. Consumer-to-prerequisite edges express review and context obligations. Broad declared review scopes must not be mistaken for claims that every included verse is causally affected.

## Verification and its limits

Final engineering checked the exact source/record geometry, preserved alternatives, operative overlays and required graph associations; eight targeted corruptions were rejected through the same task-local composed validator, with restored baselines and reverse replay. Final semantic review combined prior exact-input reading with complete changed-field reading and record equality. Fifty distinct declared finished-field quotation entries were checked against their attributed raw spans. This is not a count of every repeated quotation occurrence or certification that all undeclared quotations are detectable.

The final semantic reviewer also authored the earlier comparative adjudication. That review is nonauthor of the assembled candidate, not an additional blind-primary vote or independent re-adjudication of every question. Source-only boundaries were instruction-based; enforced filesystem isolation, complete hidden-context attestation and actual attention are not certified. Historical truncation, inherited-metadata repairs and other incident evidence remain retained. No claim of personally reading all repeated content in the approximately77MB bundle is made.

The full protected-release harness and actual downstream consumer runtime are not qualified. Scholar readiness, production retrieval, canonical promotion, scheduler/successor use and portability are not granted. Raw-source redistribution and a complete public evidence package require their separate checks. The source-bearing bundle is retained locally and is not uploaded by this documentation update.
