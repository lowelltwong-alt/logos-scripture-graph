# M9 Bible Knowledge Graph: Research in Progress

M9 is a research effort toward an open-source Bible knowledge graph. It develops passage candidates, retains their necessary context, and records competing interpretations and review decisions. It is an unfinished research build. The repository license and each source's terms govern reuse; open-source intent does not establish redistribution permission for every embedded layer.

As of 2026-10-22, **16/66 Protestant Bible books are accepted as reviewed local research candidates, comprising 455 units; 50 books remain and zero are scholar-ready.** The [canonical-order book index](M9_BOOK_INDEX.md) identifies the retained versions. Candidate acceptance is reversible and does not authorize production retrieval, public source release or canonical promotion. This documentation contains no raw candidate bundles.

## Latest accepted book: 1 Kings

1 Kings (`1Kgs`, canonical position 11) contributes 33 selected units under eight regnal context envelopes. Its exact candidate and additive acceptance receipt appear in the index. The retained evidence includes independently scoped assembly/final-byte, raw/literal source and saved-consumer/reference checks. Those checks support named local implementation requirements; they do not certify all historical, lexical, morphological, annotation, theological or scholarly claims.

All 11 qualifications/holds and their appeals remain attached to the local candidate. They include source and annotation limits, chronology and labor, cult terms, ethical agency, blood/place/person/house questions, vision, open horizons, measurement/place/plant issues, and consumer/reference-role limits. The four shared Hebrew/WEB association groups are qualified whole-verse contexts, not demonstrated word or fragment alignment. Main-text words, notes/qere, segment signs, English alignment and interpretive claims remain distinct.

The selected grain remains provisional. Formal regnal structure and other whole-book or ceremony alternatives remain serious competing proposals. Full arguments, particular counterarguments, responses, context dependencies, uncertainty and equal appeals must accompany any later release-eligible package. This overview cannot replace that evidence or silently release a hold.

## Sources, uncertainty and independent review

The operational original-language anchors are WLC/OSHB and SBLGNT, with WEB Classic kept as separate English alignment. Preserve raw bytes, Unicode, provenance, source revisions and annotation gaps. Selecting an anchor does not settle original readings, doctrine or passage boundaries. Source terms and attribution must be checked for each embedded layer before redistribution.

Source-first proposals receive separate language/translation, literary/discourse, textual/historical and canonical/faithfulness review, author responses and fresh adjudication where material disagreements remain. A non-author checker reopens final artifact bytes. Actual access and review scope matter: instruction-based independence does not prove enforced filesystem isolation, hidden-context freedom or backend settings.

Low-confidence boundaries and high-risk questions remain reviewable. Particular attention is needed where a boundary could change speaker attribution, detach necessary context, conceal a variant, imply lexical equivalence or overstate a theological or historical conclusion. Confidence, model identity, majority agreement and successful parsing do not establish truth. Contrary evidence and minority positions survive local acceptance.

## Relationship to M7 and M8

M9 is a distinct research line in the broader Bible-chunking effort. The [M7 method record](M7_SOL_AGENT_SYSTEM.md) and [public project overview](PUBLIC_PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md) describe other lines and dated progress snapshots. Their current book-level progress is not reverified by this update.

The [T610 architecture report](../../.ai/audits/reports/20260818-T610-public-entry-convergence.md) concerns convergence of two architecture designs; that result is distinct from book-content convergence. The inspected M9 publication materials identify method reconciliation and intended comparison, but no completed M9-to-M7/M8 book-content convergence report. Do not infer a controlled A/B/C experiment from lane names. Any comparison must bind exact versions by book name/canonical ID and source coordinates, disclose shared inputs and exposure, and retain unresolved disagreements.

## Academic contributions and navigation

Scholars, translators, textual critics, literary and historical researchers, theologians and graph engineers can contribute through the [repository contribution process](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [review workflow](../workflows/AGENT_COORDINATION_WORKFLOW.md). Identify the book, exact artifact revision, source coordinates, challenged claim or boundary, supporting evidence, strongest counterargument and proposed correction. Existing holds and alternatives are legitimate review targets; publication does not settle them.

Start from the [repository README](../../README.md) and [AI table of contents](../../AI_TABLE_OF_CONTENTS.md). This project page links the [book index](M9_BOOK_INDEX.md) and the existing proposed [1 Corinthians status page](M9_1COR_RESEARCH.md). Those routes must be present together when integrated.
